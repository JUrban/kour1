Does there exist a permutation group G on Ω that has exactly m orbits on the power set P(Ω)? Comment of 2001: It is proved (S. Shelah, S. Thomas, Bull. London Math. A topological group is said to be layer compact if the full inverse images of all of its compacts under mappings x → x n , n = 1, 2, . . . , are compacts. Describe the locally compact locally soluble layer compact groups. V. M. Poletskikh 9.45. Let a be a vector in R n with rational coordinates and set S(a) = {ka + b | k ∈ Z, b ∈ Z n }. It is obvious that S(a) is a discrete subgroup in R n of rank n.
