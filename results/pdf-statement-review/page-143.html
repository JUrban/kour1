19th Issue (2018) 143 19.91. Let G be a finite group with an abelian Sylow p-subgroup A. Suppose that B is a strongly closed elementary abelian subgroup of A. Without invoking the Classification Theorem for Finite Simple Groups (CFSG), prove that G has a normal subgroup N such that B = Ω 1 (A ∩ N ). For p = 2, this is a corollary of a theorem of Goldschmidt. For p odd, this has been proved by Flores and Foote (Adv. Math., 222 (2009), 453–484), but their proof relies on CFSG. A CFSG-free proof in the special case when p = 3 and A has 3- rank 3 would already be quite interesting. This case arises in Aschbacher’s treatment of the e(G) = 3 problem, and a proof would provide an alternative to part of his argument.
