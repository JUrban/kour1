Kohl 21.76. Let σ = (σ ij ), 1 ⩽ i ̸ = j ⩽ n, be an irreducible elementary net (carpet) of order n ⩾ 3 over a field K (see 19.48). The net σ is said to be closed if the elementary net subgroup E(σ) does not contain new elementary transvections. The net σ is said to be completable if its diagonal can be supplemented with subgroups to a complete net. Completable elementary nets are closed. It is known that over fields of characteristic 0 and 2 there exist irreducible closed elementary nets that are not completable (V. A. Koibaev, Trudy Inst. Mat. Mekh. Ural Div. Ross. Akad. Nauk, 17, no. 4 (2011), 134–141 (Russian); V. A. Koibaev, Siberian Math. J., 62, no. 2 (2021), 262–266). Do there exist irreducible closed elementary nets of order n ⩾ 3 over a field of odd characteristic that are not completable? V. A. Koibaev 21.77. Let d be an integer that is not divisible √ by n-th powers of primes, let x n − d be an irreducible polynomial over Q, let θ = n d, and let K = Q(θ) be the radical extension of degree n of the field Q. The multiplicative group K ∗ of the field K is canonically embedded into the group Aut Q (K) of all invertible Q-linear mappings of the Q-space K; let T be the image of K ∗ under this embedding. In the natural basis 1, θ, θ 2 , . . . , θ n−1 of the Q-space K the group Aut Q (K) corresponds to G = GL(n, Q), and the subgroup T to a subgroup T (d) (unsplit maximal torus). Every subgroup H of G containing T (d) and some one-dimensional transformation is rich in elementary transvections (V. A. Koibaev, St. Petersbg. Math. J., 21, no. 5 (2010), 731–742) and thus defines a net σ = σ(H) (V. A. Koibaev, A. V. Shilov, J. Math. Sci. New York 171, no. 3 (2010), 380–385).
