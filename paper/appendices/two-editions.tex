\section{Version 4: full and mathematical editions}
\label{app:two-editions}
Following a request from the Notebook's editor, version~4 provides a
second presentation, \emph{Results on problems from the Kourovka Notebook},
centred on the questions and their mathematical arguments. It begins
with a current inventory and orders the candidate arguments by problem
number, followed by partial results and prior-work deductions. The
present full edition retains the experimental narrative and historical
review documents. Both are built from one maintained source tree.

The question blocks and author credits are shared. They are concise
restatements checked against rendered pages of the supplied Notebook;
their metadata cover 69 entries, including the withdrawn question,
the repeated almost Engel question and the already solved 19.63
criterion. This is a metadata count, not a result count. The scope
qualifications and contemporary attribution are shared with the proofs.
The historical \artifact{reports/FINAL_REPORT.md} remains unchanged;
its inventory structure informed the current mathematical index.

The elementary two-dimensional centralizer fact used by 20.90 now
appears separately as Lemma~\ref{lem:matrix-centralizer}. Thus omitting
the withdrawn application to 10.35 from the mathematical edition removes
no premise of that proof. The rational-field observation and the full
withdrawal record remain in this edition. Both editions disclose the
withdrawal and the limits of the review evidence.

The source and preservation records are in
\artifact{paper/reviews/v4-2026-09-19/}. They identify the extracted proof
inputs and editorial changes, and preserve the complete version~3 PDF.
No new mathematical verification or priority claim follows from this
change of presentation. The raw editor correspondence is not reproduced.
