\section{Computational proof inputs}
\label{app:math-artifacts}
The proofs state their imported mathematical results and identify any
finite input on which they depend. The
\href{https://github.com/JUrban/kour1}{public repository} contains the
proof files, exact checking programs, retained finite data and source
references named in the text. The companion full account describes
their experimental provenance. A recorded computation and a fresh
independent replay are different kinds of evidence.

For 19.61--19.62, Appendix~\ref{app:carpet-tables} displays nineteen
representative universal integer derivations. The accompanying portable
ancillary files supply the complete derivations, Chevalley constants and
four standard-library Python checkers. These finite identities enter the
written arbitrary-ring proofs; they are not a search through selected rings.
For 15.92, the finite transcription and cycle controls are indexed by
\artifact{results/15.92-summary.json}; the choice between the primes
11 and 13 is fixed by the relevant diagram, as the proof explains.

One substantial input is unavailable in the public repository: the
1.27\,GB certificate for the final $n=7$ case of 20.100 was removed by
the repository's large-file filter. The written reduction and historical
verification logs remain, but those public files alone do not permit a
complete new replay of that case. Its reported finite verification must
be read with this limitation. The other stated cases and the unrestricted
torsion-free argument have their own proofs and dependencies.

The mathematical and full editions are compiled from the same sources.
Their separate source archives include the portable carpet inputs;
the full source archive also includes the current inventories, problem
metadata and revision checks. The historical reports retain the claims
and counts of their respective dates. Current scope is stated in this
edition and its shared metadata.
