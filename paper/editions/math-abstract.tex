\begin{abstract}
We present arguments for 45 proposed answers to problems or specified
subparts in the Kourovka Notebook, together with partial results and
deductions from earlier work. Each entry gives the question, its author,
the scope of the answer and its mathematical argument. The constructions
include finite and infinite groups, linear groups, varieties and
quasivarieties, and related algebraic structures. Imported results and
overlapping contemporary solutions are credited alongside the proofs.
This mathematical edition shares its statements and proof sources with
the full account of the research experiment.
\end{abstract}
