dated literature audits in the frozen archive. Reading a statement for an
application is not a full audit of its proof or a comprehensive novelty search.

  Bibliography now uses volume 209, pages 215–240 (2026), and records the
  October 2025 online date. The linked correction,
  DOI 10.1007/s00605-025-02129-3, was opened: it corrects an author-name
  concatenation, not the mathematics cited here.
- Kida: retained published opening pages establish author, title,
  journal 28 (2025), 697–712. Conjecture 1.3 and Example 5.5 reread:
  the order-96 group contains SL2(3). The example alone is not the
  manuscript's nonmonomial counterexample.
- Tsang: retained accepted-version opening pages and arXiv metadata
  establish the normalizer definitions and published reference,
  JPAA 229 (2025), 107843, DOI 10.1016/j.jpaa.2024.107843.
  Theorem 4.5, its exceptions, and the following discussion reread.

  original p.239 read. It explicitly extends the definition to a domain
  outside the target quasivariety. This is the convention used for the
  smaller 16.20 construction. Its later abelian classification is not used.
- Plotkin–Gvaramia: primary arXiv author/title record checked. Section 1.1
