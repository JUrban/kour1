\section{Rediscoveries and deductions from prior work}
\label{app:prior}

Earlier theorems settled several questions encountered during the run,
sometimes after the agent had developed a separate argument. The examples
below include strong amalgamation, character and coset constructions,
self-similar nilpotent groups and effective universal groups. We retain
the proofs and deductions with their sources, distinguishing an explicit
prior answer from a consequence requiring an additional argument.
These results remain outside the deadline candidate count.
