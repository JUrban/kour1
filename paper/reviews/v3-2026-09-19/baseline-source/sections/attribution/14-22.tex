
\paragraph{Version 2 attribution.}
Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-14-22} establishes the radical obstruction
over a two-generated torsion-free polycyclic subgroup of \(\SL_3(\Z)\).
His Laurent-polynomial and bilinear-extension construction is different
and removes the infinite-generation feature of our coefficient groups.
Both use normal isolated closure in the ordinary coefficient free product;
see Appendix~\ref{app:contemporary-comparison}.
