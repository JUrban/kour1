Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-15-89} gives the same graph and inverse,
and proves the stronger statement that its point spectrum on all
complex functions is exactly \(\C\setminus\{0\}\). He also identifies
the graph with the established three-arc graph of the cubic tree.
The particular example is therefore shared with contemporary work;
Appendix~\ref{app:contemporary-comparison} discusses the additional argument.
