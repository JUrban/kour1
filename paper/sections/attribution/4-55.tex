
\paragraph{Related work.}
Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-4-55} gives a substantially smaller example
over \(\Z_{(11)}[\operatorname{PSL}_2(\F_{11})]\): a rank-88 module
with two decompositions into indecomposable rank-44 summands. Both
arguments use completion and rational-character obstructions to descent,
with different groups and character data. The comparison and our new
GAP check are recorded in \comparisonlocation{}.
