
\paragraph{Related work.}
Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-21-121a} gives a different construction
for every prime \(p\): a locally finite metabelian subgroup of
\(\GL_2(\overline{\F_p})\) whose admissible exponents are exactly
\((1,\infty)\). His scalar orders come from cyclotomic values at
factorial indices. Our free-product construction has a different
infimum and concerns \(p=2\); see \comparisonlocation{}.
