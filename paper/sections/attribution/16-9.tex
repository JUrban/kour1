
\paragraph{Version 2 attribution.}
Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-16-9} gives the same reflection-length
reduction and cubic-time algorithm, with explicit reconstruction and
output bounds. His prior-work discussion also identifies a qualitative
answer already implied by Dahmani--Guirardel\cite[Theorem~4]{dahmani-guirardel-equations}:
with \(\tau(x_i)=x_i^{-1}\), decide
\(P_1\cdots P_k=w\), \(\tau(P_j)P_j=1\) for \(k\le |w|\).
The twists generate a finite subgroup of the outer automorphism group.
The first solvable system gives the minimum, and enumeration then finds
its factors. The interval recurrence is the cancellation-norm method
of Brandenbursky--Gal--K\k{e}dra--Marcinkowski\cite[Section~2.I and the
2023 erratum]{cancellation-norm}, specialized to involutions.
Thus qualitative computability and the recurrence are prior ingredients;
the explicit palindrome reduction and its assembled quantitative algorithm
are shared with contemporary work. See Appendix~\ref{app:contemporary-comparison}.
