
\paragraph{Related work.}
Jayadevan~\cite{jayadevan-18-76} gives the same free-factor and
division-ring obstruction in characteristic zero, using the cup product
of the two derivations. The cocycles differ by a coboundary and a sign;
\comparisonlocation{} gives the resulting isomorphism,
which reverses the additive kernel. Our statement also permits prime
positive characteristic; the total group is torsion-free in characteristic zero.
