\paragraph{A matrix fact used below.}
\begin{lemma}\label{lem:matrix-centralizer}
For every field $F$ and every nonscalar $X\in M_2(F)$, the matrix
centralizer of $X$ is the commutative algebra $F[X]$.
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
There is a vector $v$ such that $v,Xv$ are independent. Otherwise
all lines are invariant; applying $X$ to a basis and to the sum of
its basis vectors makes $X$ scalar. An operator commuting with $X$
is determined by its value on $v$. If that value is $av+bXv$, the
operator is $aI+bX$. Conversely, every such polynomial commutes with $X$.
\end{proof}
