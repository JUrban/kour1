# Internal audit of 21.121(a)

2026-09-10. This is the constructing agent's audit, not outside review.
No defect has been found in the candidate proof so far.

## Original requirements

The supplied PDF was visually checked on page 185. The exponent is defined
using **every finite subgroup** and a **normal abelian subgroup of order
coprime to p**. The ambient group need not be finitely generated. Part (a)
asks whether the infimum is attained; a single example at p=2 suffices for a
negative answer. Part (b) is a separate question and remains unresolved here.

## Mathematical audit

1. **Actual ambient group.** The free product of the finite G_k is countable.
   Each factor embeds by reduced-word normal form. The free-product tree shows
   that every finite subgroup lies in a conjugate of a factor. A restricted
   direct product would not serve the same purpose: it would introduce new
   finite products of subgroups and could change the infimal exponent.
2. **Every subgroup is controlled.** The proof gives an explicit admissible
   subgroup A_0=C_A(U) in every H=U semidirect A. The estimate is not confined
   to the orders of the factors G_k themselves.
3. **No hidden complement theorem.** Since |G_k| has only primes 2 and 3,
   a Sylow 3-subgroup complements the normal Sylow 2-subgroup in every H.
   Ordinary Sylow conjugacy puts it inside the standard C_3^n. No stronger
   hypothesis is needed.
4. **Kernel on the vector quotient.** An element of A acting trivially on W
   differs from the identity on U by central elements of exponent two. Since
   it has order three and fixes those central elements, it must act trivially
   on U itself. This identifies the effective rank r correctly.
5. **Independent characters, not all weights.** A faithful action of C_3^r
   can have many constituents, but its occurring characters span an r-dimensional
   space. Select an independent set of exactly r. Their isotypic coordinate
   supports are disjoint, even when there are multiplicities or inverse-character
   twists. This is what permits the code-intersection lemma.
6. **Quaternion commutator.** In the two-dimensional quotient Q_8/Z(Q_8), an
   order-three operator sends every nonzero vector to a different nonzero vector.
   Their symplectic pairing is one. Thus the commutator gives the binary support
   vector, with no sign ambiguity over F_2 and no assumption on central lifts.
7. **Central quotient losses.** The r support vectors are disjoint and independent
   before quotienting. Their images have dimension r-c, where c is their span's
   intersection dimension with the code. The proof uses the *intersection*
   dimension, not the full code dimension k, which would give no uniform bound
   for small subgroups of large factors.
8. **Code lemma.** A c-dimensional subcode has all 2^c-1 nonzero column patterns.
   A span of r disjoint supports can have at most r such patterns. This gives
   2^c<=r+1, including r=0. No distance or random-code estimate is used.
9. **Admissible normal subgroup.** C_A(U) is normal in H because A is abelian
   and U centralizes it. It has odd order. Its index is exactly |U|3^r.
10. **Uniform constant.** For e>log(24)/log(8), the expression
    (r+1)^(e-1)(3/8^(e-1))^r has finite supremum. This bound does not depend on
    the factor k, the particular subgroup, or an unbounded number of coordinates.
11. **Failure at the limit.** Every normal odd-order subgroup of G_k centralizes
    P_k and hence has trivial image in the faithfully acting C_3^n; it is therefore
    trivial. Thus the lower bound uses the actual minimum permissible index.
    The ratio at e_0 is 2^(k(e_0-1)), which is unbounded.
12. **Infimum, not just one failed bound.** Every e>e_0 works, e_0 fails, and
    any e<e_0 would imply a bound at e_0 by monotonicity. This establishes the
    claimed infimum exactly.

## Exact computations

`scripts/verify_21_121a.g` constructs S=SmallGroup(24,3), checks its isomorphism
with SL_2(3), constructs the central code subgroup and quotient, and checks the
normal odd core is trivial. It then verifies the actual subgroup inequality
|U|(r+1)>=8^r and admissibility of C_A(U) on every conjugacy class of subgroups.

- k=1: G_1 has order 12, 5 subgroup classes, 10 actual subgroups; all passed.
- k=2: G_2 has order 3,456, 614 subgroup classes, 11,781 actual subgroups;
  all passed. The bound and subgroup conditions are invariant under conjugation.

An initial invocation failed before doing mathematical checks because it used
an unavailable GAP function name. Its error log is retained. The corrected run
uses IsomorphismGroups and ends with DONE; there are no errors or warnings in
the completed log.

`scripts/verify_21_121a.py` independently checks the code lemma for every span
of disjoint supports in code lengths 1,3,7: respectively 2,15,4,140 spaces.
It also checks all 512 quaternion associativity triples, all 64 pairs for the
order-three automorphism, and the 16 commutator cases used in the proof.
Output: `results/21.121a-controls.json`, PASS.

These are finite sanity checks. The proof, not the computations, supplies the
claim for every k and every finite subgroup of the infinite free product.
