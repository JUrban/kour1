# Problem 7.58: a spanning-tree retraction criterion

11 September 2026. Working plan; not yet a complete candidate.

The base of the verbal wreath product in the question is the relatively
free V-group on Q x X, where F=F(X) and Q=F/R. Interpret these basis
elements as the directed edges of the right Cayley multigraph of Q.
In the unrestricted free edge group, the based-loop subgroup is a free
factor: choose a spanning tree, replace each non-tree edge by its
fundamental based loop, and leave tree edges as basis elements.

This suggests an explicit retraction rho onto the loop subgroup that
descends to every relatively free V-quotient. If t_q is the word of
tree edges along the path from 1 to q, the image criterion should be

    (b,q) lies in the image if and only if rho(b) = b t_q^-1.

Unlike a restatement using membership in the subgroup generated by the
embedded original generators, the right side is one equality in the
ambient relatively free base. The substitution defining rho is explicit
on every edge. It should also recover a preimage word whenever the
equality holds, by replacing each edge in b with its Schreier loop.
