# Problem 21.99: degree 47 and independent word certificates

2026-09-12. **The proposed property holds for every transitive action of
degree at most 47**, subject to the completeness of the installed
transitive-group catalogue. Every supplied action is proved directly
from its generator permutations. The arbitrary-degree problem remains
unresolved; no complete candidate is added.

All **496,284** catalogue actions have complete certificates, with no

## Producer and independently checked proof format

An action row is

    [degree, catalogue_index, generators, circuit, witnesses,
     random_steps, uncompressed_node_count].

Each generator is a permutation array on zero-based points. A word
reference zero denotes the identity; positive references denote input
generators followed by earlier circuit nodes, and negative references
denote inverses. A circuit node [a,b] denotes the product of those two
earlier words, in the right-action convention. The witness list has
one word reference per point, with zero at the first point.

The GAP producer first builds point transversals as words in the input
generators. Its Schreier generators for the first-point stabilizer are
also explicit words. Conjugating a candidate by a point transversal
gives a witness at the first point. Further conjugation by stabilizer
words transports that witness across its suborbit. Input generators
are tried first, followed by bounded random walks when needed.
Unused circuit nodes are removed before export.

Randomness affects only the search for witnesses. Every accepted word
is checked deterministically. A missing witness would have been marked
unresolved, not as a counterexample. No run reached the 5,000-step cap:
the largest required walk had 1,212 steps.

The Python verifier checks that each input is a permutation and that
every word reference is valid and refers strictly backwards. It then
evaluates every multiplication and inverse, verifies that the target
word sends the first point to the claimed target, and counts all its
fixed points. Every target must appear exactly once in a complete row.
Each supplied action's positivity and transitivity follow from these
checks. Conjugation then gives the property for every distinct ordered
pair, as proved in `21.99-partial-proof.md`.

The proof uses neither a computed group order nor an independent
membership algorithm: each witness belongs to the generated group
because it is explicitly a word in its generators. GAP's point
stabilizer, conjugacy-class and character algorithms are not imports of
the independent verification. Catalogue labels and completeness remain
imports when turning the explicit models into the stated degree bound.

The full verification evaluates **64,329,817** circuit multiplications,
or **2,484,321,676** point-level composition entries, from 1,432,657
input generator arrays. These are word evaluations; they are not an
enumeration of every group element or every pair of group elements.

## Process evidence and archived replay

The 485-group pilot comprises all transitive groups of degrees 2--12
and the last eleven degree-24 entries. It certifies 4,833 witnesses,
9,248 circuit nodes and 117,153 point-level composition entries. The
pilot producer and independent verifier both returned zero with clean
stderr and their required completion markers.

Eight GAP workers then processed disjoint index residue classes across
all 24 non-prime-power degrees. Every worker returned zero, with an
empty unresolved-case file, clean stderr and exactly one completion
marker. The controller collected all eight actual exits and itself
returned zero. This took about 264 seconds wall time, with at most
eight additional CPUs and a 3 GiB cap per worker. The existing 20.100
certificate worker continued undisturbed.

The sequential independent verifier read all original row files and
returned zero after about 238 seconds. Deterministic gzip compression
reduced 1,076,716,159 raw bytes to 224,198,201 archived bytes. Each
archive was decompressed and checked against the original producer's
SHA256 and byte count. A separate eight-process verification then read
all archived certificates and reproduced every count, in about 33
seconds. All actual process records, outputs and hashes are retained.

Five corruption types are rejected: a malformed generator, a forward
word reference, an incorrect target, an exactly-one-fixed-point witness,
and an omitted witness. These were checked on the pilot, on the full
raw verification, and separately in each of the eight archive shards.
The archive run therefore records 40 rejections of five distinct types.

The checked gzip files are the committed primary certificates. Raw
copies are retained locally but are unnecessary to replay the archived
proof. `verify_21_99_words.py extension` is the optional raw-file mode;
the archive command works from the committed files alone.

## Structural results and source boundary

The companion proof gives the prime-power-degree result, a lifting
lemma through any semiregular normal subgroup, the nilpotent case, and
closure under transitive overgroups and Cartesian product actions.
It does not assume every abelian normal subgroup is semiregular and
does not conclude the general solvable case. Priority of these partial
observations is unclaimed.

Müller's 2026 paper is read and credited in `21.99-source-audit.md`.
The independent F8 matrix control verifies an explicit witness with at
least two fixed cosets at the pair obstructing the stronger derangement
question. Its 750 matrix products check the source identities and two
explicit fixed cosets; the full Steinberg-group identification and
all other coset pairs remain imported or unchecked as stated there.

An initial exploratory use of GAP's `Factorization` on a large
degree-24 group exhausted the 4 GiB workspace and returned zero despite
the error. That tool observation is preserved and excluded. The
accepted direct-word producer avoids this algorithm. There are no
rejected or omitted actions in the final 496,284-action run.

## Reproduction

Run the independent archived proof directly with

    python3 scripts/verify_21_99_archives.py

For packet hashes, actual exits, exact census reconciliation, lossless
archive checks, pilot regeneration, all archived words, corruptions and
the matrix control, run

    python3 scripts/audit_21_99_words.py --replay

Without `--replay`, the auditor checks the exact evidence and all archive
hashes without repeating the already completed mathematical checks.
The full regeneration of all 496,284 GAP certificates is not needed
for verification; explicit word evaluation suffices. Nothing in this
packet proves the arbitrary-degree conjecture.
