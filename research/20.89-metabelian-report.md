## Mathematical outcome

The proof establishes the elementary identities E(a^-1)=E(a) and
E(ab)<=E(a)E(b) for almost Engel elements of any metabelian group.
Each E(a) is a finite normal subgroup. A direct commuting-endomorphism
argument supplies closure without assuming linearity. The source
audit then found the normal-sink and product argument in prior work;
this is retained as a rediscovery.

For virtually abelian groups, Shumyatsky's Lemma3.2 plus an explicit
finite characteristic-kernel argument proves closure, also without
linearity. This is a prior-theorem consequence. The argument uses
normal closures finite-by-(locally nilpotent); that hypothesis is
not established for all almost Engel elements of arbitrary linear
groups. None of this closes the remaining gap in20.89.

The generalized-dihedral proposition characterizes reflection sinks
for every abelian kernel A: reflections are almost Engel exactly
when A is torsion with finite odd-primary part, and their sink is
that odd-primary part. It distinguishes arbitrarily late entry into
a fixed finite sink from an infinite union of growing sinks.
