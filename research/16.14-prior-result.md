# Problem 16.14: affirmative by a prior centralizer bound

11 September 2026. Prior-theorem deduction, excluded from new candidates.

Put E=Omega_1(G) and r=d(Z(G)). Under the printed hypothesis, E is
the elementary abelian subgroup of all involutions together with the
identity, E=Omega_1(Z(G)), and d(E)=r. Every elementary abelian
subgroup lies in E, so E is a maximal elementary abelian normal
subgroup. It is central, hence C_G(E)=G.

The proof of Sambale's Theorem 1.3 supplies the prior bound

    |C_G(E) : Phi(C_G(E))| <= 2^(2r).

With C_G(E)=G, and Phi(G)=G^2 for a finite 2-group, it gives

    d(G/G^2) = d(G) <= 2 d(Z(G)),

which is exactly the proposed inequality. No exponent restriction on
G is needed for the displayed intermediate bound. The trivial group
satisfies the inequality separately. Products of r quaternion groups
of order eight attain equality and have central involutions.

Arch. Math. (Basel) 103 (2014), 11--20. The retained author manuscript

Theorem 37.1. That underlying theorem has not been independently

additional explicit statement of the same bound on PDF page 6:

The introduction and the paragraph before Theorem 1.11 were read,
and page 6 viewed. It credits Sambale and MacWilliams. The downloaded
file is the September 2023 preprint, despite the local filename's
