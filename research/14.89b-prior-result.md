# Problem 14.89(b): exact prior affirmative result

Jaikin-Zapirain's *On the abundance of finite p-groups*, Journal of
Group Theory 3 (2000), 225--231, already answers part (b). Corollary
3.1 bounds the cobreadth solely in terms of abundance. The author's
May 4, 2000 version defines cobreadth as

    cb(G)=min_{x in G} log_p |C_G(x)|,

so this is precisely the requested bound on one element's centralizer.
It is excluded from the new-candidate count. Parts (a), 14.90 and
14.91 are not resolved here.

For completeness, the short deduction from the paper's Theorem 1.4
is as follows. That theorem supplies h(a)>0 with

    log_p |G| <= h(a) p^6.

Hall's abundance formula, with |G|=p^(2n+e), gives

    k(G)=p^e+(p^2-1)(n+a(p-1))
        <= (h(a)/2+a+1) p^8.

Since k(G) is the average of |C_G(x)| over all x in G, its minimum
is at most this quantity. Thus one may take

    f(a)=ceil(8+log_2(h(a)/2+a+1)).

Increase h(a) to at least one if needed. This inference needs a
polynomial dependence on p with fixed exponent, which is stronger
than an unspecified order bound depending jointly on p and a.

## Source and reading limits

The current author site links to the older publication list at
https://matematicas.uam.es/~andrei.jaikin/preprints/articulos.htm ,
which supplies the original DVI:
https://matematicas.uam.es/~andrei.jaikin/preprints/abundance.dvi .
The public download required disabling certificate verification for
that individual request because the local trust store could not verify
the site's issuer. No credentials were involved.

The 21,608-byte DVI and a direct `dvi2tty` extraction are retained.
The full seven-page extracted text was read, particularly the definition
on p.2 and Corollary 3.1 on p.6. The notation converter renders several
inequality symbols identically; its prose statements are unambiguous.
The powerful-group estimates are imported, not independently reproved.

The installed `dvipdf` pipeline returned zero but produced an empty
one-page PDF. A subsequent direct renderer identified missing TeX font
metrics and failed. That unusable PDF and its empty extracted text
are not evidence and are excluded from the retained source packet.
No claim of visually checking the original typography is made.
