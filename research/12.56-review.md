# Internal review of the12.56 growth bounds

- The presentation-length convention was visually checked on Notebook
  page62. Counting the encoding length of generator indices instead
  would change the conclusion, so that convention is stated explicitly.
- For the upper bound, renaming generators loses no group isomorphism
  types. Allowing ordered relator lists and unreduced words overcounts,
  which is the correct direction. The empty relator list and trivial
  zero-generator group are included.
- For the lower bound, every graph contributes a cyclically reduced
  commutator presentation of length exactly v+4e. Droms/Laurence rigidity
  distinguishes the groups; merely counting graphs without this input
  would not suffice. The rigidity statement allows isolated vertices
  and disconnected graphs, so there is no unproved generic-graph condition.
- Dividing the labeled graph count by v! is a lower bound, regardless
  of graph automorphisms. A nonintegral bound is legitimate for the
  integer F(n).
- The chosen v and e satisfy the length constraint for every sufficiently
  large n and have e<=binomial(v,2). Floors contribute only the explicitly
  bounded error terms. The binomial lower bound follows term by term.
- The theorem establishes the scale log F(n)=Theta(n log n); it does not
  identify a leading constant. The function's nonrecursiveness is not
  contradicted by computable comparison functions.
- The primary2008 source's introduction and Theorem4.1 statement were read;
  the original1987 PDF was inaccessible and its proof is not claimed to
  have been read. The cited stronger theorem is an established input.
- No computational search is needed to support this counting proof.
  No additional complete-problem candidate is counted; priority of this
  elementary consequence remains unestablished.
