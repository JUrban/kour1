# Exact exponent for a restricted power of A5

12 September 2026, follow-up to the soluble-group partial result.
This is an example within 21.121(b), not a complete resolution or a
new entry in the 46-candidate ledger. Priority and outside review are
pending.

1. Prove the elementary subgroup facts about A5 and the description of
   subdirect products of a fixed nonabelian simple group.
2. Split every subgroup of A5^n into a product of diagonal A5 factors
   and a soluble group, using the absence of a nontrivial common
   quotient of a perfect group and a soluble group.
3. Apply the already proved soluble bound with J=1, and use the full
   powers to establish the exact lower exponent. Check that the ambient
   restricted power is not virtually soluble.
4. Enumerate subgroup classes of A5 and A5^2 in GAP. Independently
   reconstruct all subgroups of A5^2 from Goursat data over a permutation
   multiplication table for A5, comparing weighted histograms and the
   actual minimum normal abelian odd-order indices.
5. Preserve bounded process records and failures, if any, without
   modifying the frozen soluble-case packet. Commit the follow-up
   locally and include its limited scope in the final report.

The two-factor computation is a control; the arbitrary-number-of-factors
statement requires the written induction.
