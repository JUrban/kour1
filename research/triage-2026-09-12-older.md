# Older-problem triage, 12 September 2026

This is exploratory reasoning only. No additional complete candidate.

- 13.54 refers explicitly to 6.55, which requires a split Frobenius group
  with the stated kernel/complement decomposition. Embedding an arbitrary
  group as a malnormal free factor in a free product does not answer it.
- 13.17 asks about irreducible locally unipotent representations in
  characteristic zero, with the nilpotence bound allowed to depend on the
  vector and group element. Finite-dimensional Engel/Kolchin arguments
  do not directly apply. Faithful sums of nilpotent quotient
  representations of free groups are reducible and give no example.
- 13.3 resembles the decomposition of projective modules into countably
  generated summands. Closing subsets of a free generating set under an
  idempotent endomorphism yields small retracts, but does not establish
  a decomposition as a varietal free product. A split group extension is
  insufficient for that conclusion.
- 14.58 fixed-point-free automorphisms of an abelian group exclude
  elementary abelian subgroups of rank two by the usual finite argument.
  This alone does not exclude infinite groups of prime exponent with
  all finite subgroups cyclic. No reduction to locally finite groups was
  proved.
- 14.75 distinguishes arbitrary homomorphisms of a free group from
  epimorphisms onto the specified two-generator groups. Having no common
  identity gives the former separating maps; turning them into the latter
  is exactly the missing issue, not an automatic consequence.
- 14.84 cannot be decided merely by primitive abelianization outside
  varieties where that criterion has been established.
- 12.21 needs the intended representation-ring definition: modules over
  a product ring need not be free, so a ring idempotent is not automatically
  an admissible representation-ring counterexample.

A first web search for 13.54, 13.17 and 13.3 did not locate a new resolution.
The returned older Krasnoyarsk survey discussed infinite Frobenius
constructions, but no theorem was imported from the search excerpt.
