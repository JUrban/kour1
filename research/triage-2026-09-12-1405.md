# Triage before the class-two extension of 16.14

September 12, 2026. No complete candidate is added by this note.

- **11.19:** associated graded Lie generation still does not establish
  normal generation in the group. The existing weight-five source audit
  and the unproved weight-six lead were reread; no new finite quotient
  or rewriting certificate was obtained.
- **11.23:** the invariant-subgroup condition does not immediately
  give a chain of subgroups formed by kernels of iterated differences.
  In the nonabelian case those kernels need not be subgroups. No
  argument closes this gap.
- **20.124:** the graph of a Rota--Baxter map is a regular subgroup
  of F x F under the left-right action. Goursat's lemma alone does
  not force its kernels to be normal in all of F. A proposed graph
  of a homomorphism F'->F would require a bijective difference map;
  no such map or impossibility proof was constructed.
- **20.103:** vertex retractions identify intersections of derived
  subgroups with parabolic subgroups. This does not prove that the
  abelianization of the full derived subgroup is torsion-free: in a
  graph-of-groups argument, edge relations still need to be controlled.
  The primary abstract of Lopez de Gamiz Zearra--Martinez Perez,
  arXiv:2405.16641, gives a derived-free result in the coherent case,
  not the full graph question. The paper's proof was not read or used.
- **12.85:** two-generator soluble subgroups of a simple group would
  suffice, but the presence of an arbitrary soluble subgroup of the
  required derived length does not supply such a pair. No uniform
  reduction was proved.
- **12.56:** graph counting again gives n^(Theta(n)); this duplicates
  the existing quantitative partial in `12.56-growth-bounds.md`.
  It is not a new result from this turn.
- **16.87 and 20.92(a):** reread the complete 16.87 central-twist
  proof and the 20.92(a) proof through its polynomial inverse bound.
  No new defect was found in those portions; this is an internal
  reading check, not an outside review or a fresh audit of every
  imported input in the full packets.

The useful new step was instead the square map on (G/Z(G))[2],
which removes the exponent restriction from the class-two case of
16.14. That theorem and its controls have a separate proof packet.

A subsequent ledger reconciliation found that the original 16.14 bound
was already recorded as a prior affirmative consequence. The new work
therefore supplies an elementary class-two refinement and repairs stale
status entries; it is not a new answer to a previously unresolved problem.
