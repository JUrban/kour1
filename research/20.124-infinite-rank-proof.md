# A Rota–Baxter operator with commutator image in every infinite rank

**Partial answer to20.124.** If F is a free group of any infinite rank,
there is a weight-one Rota–Baxter operator B:F->F with image exactly
F'=[F,F]. The finite-rank nonabelian case is not settled by this proof.
No complete-candidate increment or priority claim is made.

All products below are ordered as written. No infinite product is used:
each displayed sequence of factors is eventually the identity.

## 1. A locally terminating endomorphism

Let kappa be an infinite cardinal. Partition a free basis X of F into
disjoint sets X_0,X_1,..., each of cardinality kappa. Write F_m for the
free subgroup on X_0 union ... union X_m. Each F_m has cardinality
kappa, as does F_m': the upper bound follows from finite words, and
the lower bound follows from the distinct commutators [x,y] with one
fixed basis element x and kappa other basis elements y.

For each m>=0 choose a surjection

    theta_m : X_{m+1} -> F_m'.

Define an endomorphism phi:F->F by phi(x)=1 for x in X_0 and
phi(x)=theta_m(x) for x in X_{m+1}. Freeness gives this endomorphism
without additional compatibility conditions. It lowers levels:
phi(F_m) <= F_{m-1} for m>=1, and phi(F_0)=1. Thus phi^{m+1}(g)=1
for every g in F_m. Every element of F lies in some F_m.

Its image lies in F' because every basis image is a commutator-subgroup
element. Conversely, F'=union_m F_m'. One can see the reverse
inclusion in this equality by expressing an element of F' as a finite
product of commutators and putting all words involved into a common
F_m. Each F_m' is contained in phi(X_{m+1}), so phi(F)=F'.

In countable rank this can be made computable: label X_m by the natural
numbers, enumerate all finite reduced words on the levels up to m with
zero exponent sum at each basis letter, and map the successive letters
of X_{m+1} onto that enumeration. A fair enumeration by finite word
encodings is enough. The abelianization of a free group shows that this
enumerates exactly F_m'. The proof does not require a particular speed
for that enumeration.

## 2. Two inverse set maps

More generally, let phi be an endomorphism of any group G such that
each g is killed by some iterate. Put

    D(g) = g phi(g)^-1,
    T(g) = g phi(g) phi^2(g) ... .

Both maps are well-defined. If phi^r(g)=1, the product defining T(g)
stops before its r-th iterate. The identities

    D(T(g)) = (g phi(g) ... phi^{r-1}(g))
               (phi(g) ... phi^{r-1}(g))^-1 = g

and

    T(D(g)) = (g phi(g)^-1)(phi(g) phi^2(g)^-1)... = g

prove that D and T are inverse bijections of sets. The first identity
uses phi(T(g))=phi(g)...phi^{r-1}(g); the second uses that phi is a
homomorphism. They do not assert that T or D is a group homomorphism.

## 3. The operator and its identity

Define

    B(g) = phi(T(g)) = phi(g) phi^2(g) ... .

Thus B(D(u))=phi(u). Since D is onto, write g=D(u), h=D(v). Then

    g B(g) h B(g)^-1
      = u phi(u)^-1 phi(u) v phi(v)^-1 phi(u)^-1
      = uv phi(uv)^-1
      = D(uv).

Applying B yields

    B(g B(g) h B(g)^-1) = phi(uv) = B(g) B(h),

which is the exact weight-one identity printed in20.124. Also
Im(B)=Im(phi), since T is bijective. Apply this general construction
to Section1 to obtain Im(B)=F'.

Surjectivity onto F' has particularly direct witnesses. If w in F_m'
and x in X_{m+1} is chosen with phi(x)=w, then

    B(x w^-1) = B(D(x)) = w.

The descendant group, whose multiplication is
g circle h = g B(g) h B(g)^-1, is isomorphic to the original free
group through D, since D(u) circle D(v)=D(uv).

## 4. Limits and provenance

This construction cannot be obtained in finite rank by choosing an
endomorphism phi with the same image: the image of a finitely generated
group under an endomorphism is finitely generated, whereas the derived
subgroup of a nonabelian free group of finite rank has infinite rank.
That is a limitation of this construction, not a proof that no other
Rota–Baxter operator works in finite rank.

The passage from a bijective difference map of homomorphisms to a
Rota–Baxter operator is the familiar bijective-cocycle construction;
the general background is credited to Guo–Lang–Sheng and
Bardakov–Gubarev in the source audit. The level-lowering map and the
precise image assertion are proved here directly. Their priority has
not been established. In particular, existence in every infinite rank
does not answer the question for an arbitrary specified finite-rank F.
