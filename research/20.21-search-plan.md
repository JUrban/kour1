# Problem 20.21: bounded search after withdrawal audit

12 September 2026. General question remains unresolved.

The January 2026 negative claim was withdrawn; the separate source
triage records this. Search actual finite groups for isomorphic normal
subgroups K,L of index twelve with quotients C12 and A4. Begin with
orders 12,24,48,96,192,384, without relying on the external minimality
reduction. These are an explicitly bounded family, not all possible
orders through 384. If the pilot is fast, consider order 768.

For each group, first test whether its abelianization admits C12.
This is equivalent to divisibility of its abelian exponent by twelve.
Then enumerate all normal subgroups of index twelve and identify both
quotient types by structural or isomorphism tests, never by the string
"V4". Compare the candidate kernel groups by IdGroup and, for any
collision, an explicit isomorphism. Retain a row for every group that
has both quotient types, including the invariant types of all kernels.
No filter on characteristic subgroups is allowed.

The independent control should reconstruct normal subgroups from full
multiplication tables for several small groups with both quotients,
and verify that the omitted groups fail the necessary abelianization
condition. Successful executions require actual zero exits, clean
logs, sentinels and hashes. A negative bounded search is not a proof
of the general negative answer.
