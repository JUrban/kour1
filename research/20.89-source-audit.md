# Sources and scope for the 20.89 partial results

September 12, 2026. Exact statements for 19.83 and 20.89 are extracted
from the existing Notebook index in `results/20.89-statements.json`.
Both ask about all linear groups over arbitrary fields, with a single
finite sink per element. They are duplicate questions, not two counts.
The actual PDF pages142 and160 were rendered and visually checked;
both unstarred statements and the uniform finite-set quantifier agree
with the extracted index. The two page images are retained.

## Primary sources used

1. Pavel Shumyatsky, *Almost Engel linear groups*,
   [arXiv:1610.03126v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1610.03126), published in
   Monatsh. Math.186 (2018), 711–719. The cached PDF/text already existed
   from the earlier triage. Re-read the introduction, all preliminary
   statements and Section 3 through Theorem 3.3 and its proof. Theorem
   3.3 supplies finite-by-nilpotence for a virtually soluble group
   generated by finitely many almost Engel elements. The preliminary
   Gruenberg theorem identifies Engel elements with the Hirsch-Plotkin
   radical. The full-group main theorem does not by itself answer
   product closure in an arbitrary ambient group.
2. B.A.F. Wehrfritz, *Weak Engel Conditions on Linear Groups*,
   [AGTA7 (2019), 143–157](https://www.advgrouptheory.com/journal/Volumes/7/Wehrfritz%20-%20Weak%20Engel%20conditions%20on%20linear%20group.pdf),
   DOI10.32037/agta-2019-006. Downloaded PDF and extracted text are
   hashed in `results/20.89-source-download.json`. Read pp.143–149,
   especially the minimal-sink periodic-point characterization, the
   scalar-layer argument and Hall/Baer application in step (b), and
   Remark 3.3 about the Fitting and Hirsch-Plotkin radicals. Our
   triangular characterization explicitly adapts the scalar-layer
   method. The article does not state the original general closure
   assertion in those passages. No new priority is claimed for this
   adaptation.
3. Bogdan Nica, *Linear groups — Malcev's theorem and Selberg's lemma*,
   [arXiv:1306.2385v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1306.2385). Read the theorem
   statements, Section 1, and Section 3's residual-finiteness deduction
   from Lemma 3.2(iii),(iv), including the proofs of those ring facts.
   This verifies the arbitrary-field Malcev input. We do not substitute
   characteristic-zero virtual torsion-freeness for residual finiteness.
   PDF/text and retrieval hashes are retained.

Hall, Baer and Gruenberg are classical imported theorems, with their
exact forms exposed in the proof files. The original books/papers were
not newly downloaded; the cited primary expositions supply the forms
used. The partial proofs are therefore not presented as independent
proofs of those classical results.

## Searches and limitations

Queries included "almost Engel elements subgroup linear", "almost
Engel residually finite", "almost Engel torsion-free linear", and
"Engel sinks generators bounded". Search results also pointed to
Dal Berto–Caldeira–Shumyatsky, [arXiv:2505.01409](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.01409),
whose abstract concerns all p-elements of a finite or profinite group.
That theorem is not an input to our deductions. Search excerpts for
its finite normal-subgroup lemmas did not establish the needed global
product-sink conclusion and are not used as a substitute for a proof.

The Bath Engel-problem list gives a different, weaker-looking sink
quantifier in its parenthetical definition. The Notebook's exact
quantifier controls here. No answer is inferred from that discrepancy.

No published answer to the complete question was identified in this
limited search. This does not establish novelty of the partial results.
They remain excluded from the complete-candidate count and await review.
