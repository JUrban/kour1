# Internal audit of the candidate for17.33

Date: 2026-09-11. This is an internal mathematical audit, not an
independent reviewer endorsement or a confirmed priority claim.

## Exact scope

The supplied Notebook's printed page108 was inspected as an image.
The question concerns the quasivariety generated by
K=<a,b | a^(-1)ba=b^(-1)> and asks for a set of quasiidentities in a
finite set of variables. It is unstarred. There is no restriction to
finite groups, no coefficients, and no fixed ambient variety.

The proposed negative answer covers even an infinite axiom set with
a uniform finite variable bound. For every r, the proof supplies a
finitely presented group satisfying every r-variable quasiidentity of
K and failing one other quasiidentity of K.

## Critical steps checked

1. **The integer model of K.** Take a=(0,1), b=(1,0) in
   (u,k)(v,l)=(u+(-1)^k v,k+l). The relation holds. Conversely, collecting
   a word to b^u a^k gives precisely this law and unique coordinates.
   The projection to the second coordinate has infinite cyclic kernel.
2. **The parity constraints define a subgroup.** Signs are one modulo2,
   so c+D_e d has the required parities e_j+f_j. Inversion gives
   (e,-D_e c), with the same prescribed parities. All coordinate
   translations by two and every holonomy value occur.
3. **Torsion-freeness.** A nonzero e has a nonzero annihilating character
   only when n>=2, which is explicitly imposed. A coordinate with
   e_j=1 is odd and fixed by that character's action, so the square
   is nonzero. A finite-order element would square into a torsion-free
   translation subgroup and hence have square one. This is impossible.
4. **Finite abelianization kills every map to K.** Each translation
   generator is inverted by some lift, putting its square in the derived
   subgroup. The resulting finite abelianization first kills the
   composition with K->Z and then kills the remaining image in its
   kernel Z. No assumption that the homomorphism is injective is made.
5. **Local embeddings.** For every proper L, choose psi annihilating L.
   Its n coordinate functions are genuinely additive on the *whole*
   preimage Gamma_L, including all translations. The integer homomorphism
   ell_chi has parity chi(e). Thus each map (c_(chi,j),ell_chi) has the
   Klein bottle multiplication law. The product is injective because
   the first coordinates recover all of c, whose parities recover e.
   The construction is uniform in L and includes L=0. Any subgroup
   with fewer than n generators lies in one such Gamma_L.
6. **Finite presentation is sufficient for exclusion.** The displayed
   relations collect words to t^z g_1^epsilon_1...g_n^epsilon_n. An
   identity in the affine image forces epsilon=0 and then z=0, so
   these are a presentation, not just valid relations. The finite
   conjunction of all relators implies a nontrivial generator is one
   in every K-assignment, since every Gamma_n->K map is trivial.
   This finite Horn sentence fails in Gamma_n and separates it from
   q(K). We do not assume finitely generated members of q(K) are
   residually K without a finite-presentation hypothesis.
7. **Variable bound.** For any r-variable assignment, evaluation takes
   place in its r-generated subgroup, which lies in q(K) when n>r.
   Hence Gamma_n satisfies *all* r-variable quasiidentities of K at
   once. This contradicts any proposed bounded-variable basis.

## Reproducible controls

Run `python3 scripts/verify_17_33.py` and
`bin/gap -q -b -T -m 128m -o 4g scripts/verify_17_33.g`.
Inspect PASS/DONE markers; a GAP exit code alone does not certify success.

The Python certificate reports 1,366 relation controls, 2,000 group-law
controls, 2,000 nonzero-square controls, and 393,350 local-map checks.
The 5,16,67,374 subspaces in dimensions2,3,4,5 are all generated; every
proper one and each of its holonomy values is covered, with sampled
integer translates. This does not exhaust infinitely many translations.
There are also150 independent full integer matrix products.

All65,536 products in Gamma_2/2N agree with an independent residue
implementation. Its centre equals the elements whose square is one
and has order64. The finite local embeddings are injective and pass
all53,248 pair products. GAP separately constructs the presentation,
identifies the quotient as SmallGroup(256,26974), and obtains derived
order2 and exponent4. The abelianization of Gamma_2 is C2^3 x C4^2;
those of Gamma_3, Gamma_4, Gamma_5 also have finite exponent dividing4.

An initial Python Klein-bottle multiplication used (-1)**k, which
produces a floating-point sign when k is negative. It was replaced
with an integer parity branch and the whole certificate rerun. The
retained result uses exact integer arithmetic throughout.

  group*, Algebra Logika46:4 (2007),407–427,
  https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/al305 , DOI10.1007/s10469-007-0021-3.
  Read the introductory definitions, the group family on page410,
  Theorem1 and its proof on pages423–424, and Theorem2's statement and

  subquasivarieties and supplies no bounded-variable axiomatization.
  The remaining proof details were not fully audited.

Searches included the exact problem number, the Klein bottle group
with quasivariety/quasiidentity/finite-variable/axiomatic-rank terms,

The searched material did not reveal an earlier resolution or the
specific affine local-embedding construction. This is limited search
evidence; it does not establish novelty. Further review should compare
older work on quasivarieties of one-relator groups and constructions
of virtually abelian groups with large local indicability thresholds.
