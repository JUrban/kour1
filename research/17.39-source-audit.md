## Exact question

The local `docs/21tkt.pdf`, physical PDF page 108 (also printed page 108),
asks for a positive integer uniformly bounding the number of system
normalizers needed to intersect to the hypercenter of a finite soluble
group. The heading is unstarred. The 2017 comment records a positive
answer under a trivial-Frattini hypothesis. The page was rendered and
visually inspected; the PNG and indexed statement are retained. The
index excerpt includes a following-page footer saying 109, which is not
the physical location of the question. No different quantifier, bound on
derived length, or Frattini assumption occurs in the question itself.

## The cited 2018 article

A. Ballester-Bolinches, J. Cossey, S. F. Kamornikov and H. Meng,
*On two questions from the Kourovka Notebook*, Journal of Algebra 499
(2018), 438–449,
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2017.12.014).
The ANU institutional repository supplies the
[accepted manuscript](https://openresearch-repository.anu.edu.au/server/api/core/bitstreams/d9d4b499-ce80-4631-afdc-bde8c7dbd7eb/content).

The complete manuscript was read, including its introduction and all
three main proofs. Question 2 on manuscript page 2 is precisely 17.39.
Theorem B and Corollary 6 on page 4 explicitly assume Phi(G)=1.
Corollary 6 gives three system normalizers under that hypothesis.
Example 7 gives an order-72 example excluding two. Section 4, pages
10–11, proves the result by passing through a prefrattini subgroup and
using the equality of its core with the formation hypercenter when
Phi(G)=1. It does not remove this hypothesis. The article does not
contain the present unbounded family. Its abstract's general language
about answering two questions must be read with the actual hypotheses.

The introduction and stated results were inspected; this paper studies

The later proofs were not audited and are not inputs here.

The definition of a system normalizer as the intersection of the
normalizers of a pairwise permuting Sylow system is also stated in
the preliminaries of
[*Profinite groups with soluble centralisers*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00605-024-02014-5).
The definition was inspected; no theorem from that article is needed.
In the constructed two-prime groups, the normal Sylow p-subgroup makes
the Sylow-system condition immediate.

## Scope of the novelty search

Searches included the exact problem number with Kourovka, both spellings
of system normalizer, and combinations with intersection, unbounded,
counterexample, extraspecial, and Heisenberg. These located the cited
article and thesis but no prior unbounded family. Many other returned
pages were unrelated and supplied no evidence. This is a bounded
search, not proof of absence from the literature.

The present proof does not invoke the 2018 orbit theorem. It gives
all normalizers explicitly and computes their intersections by a
linear-rank argument. Phi(G) has order p, so the construction is
consistent with the inspected positive case. Retain the result as a
complete candidate pending broader priority checks and outside review.
