# 19.108: character support and element order

Status: the exact search and independent controls are complete. An
elementary orbit argument now proves the resulting subclass for every
odd prime; see `19.108-partial.md` and `19.108-review.md`. No complete
resolution or novelty claim is made.

The question asks whether, for every odd-prime group P and irreducible
complex character chi, chi(x) != 0 implies that |x| divides |P|/chi(1)^2.

## Existing bounds and reading scope

Tom Wilde, [Supports of irreducible characters of p-groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.5780),
2013, Theorem C, already establishes the assertion whenever |P| <= p^9,
or chi(1) <= p^3. Theorem B covers nilpotence class at most three.
The primary introduction, statements, Lemma 2.7 and the proof of
Theorem C were read; the full character-five theorem used there was
not independently audited. A search of small odd groups would duplicate
this coverage, so none was launched.

Malle--Navarro--Tiep, [Zeros of characters and orders of elements in finite groups](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.04513),
2026, concerns the different denominator chi(1), without its square.
The primary introductory conjecture and reductions were read. This
does not settle the stronger p-group question here.

## Exact first family

Take A=(Z/27Z)^2, |A|=3^6, and P=A semidirect H with |H|=3^4.
Every possible violating character has degree at least 81 by Wilde.
Clifford theory over the abelian normal subgroup A expresses its degree
as [H:K] tau(1), where K stabilizes a linear character lambda of A and
tau is an irreducible character of K. Here lambda extends to A semidirect K
by taking value one on K. Since tau(1)^2 <= |K|, the degree is at most
|H|, and equality requires K=1. Thus every possible violation in this
family comes from a regular H-orbit O in the dual group of A.

Such a character is induced directly from A, has degree 81, vanishes
outside A, and restricts to A as the sum of the 81 distinct characters
in O. Its bound is |P|/81^2=9. Its restriction vanishes on all elements
of order 27 exactly when the indicator of O is invariant under addition
of 9A in the dual coordinates. This is finite Fourier duality: the
annihilator of A[9]=3A is 9A. Invariance is tested using translations
by (9,0) and (0,9).

Every faithful H-action is conjugate into the Sylow 3-subgroup S of
GL(2,Z/27Z) consisting of matrices reducing modulo 3 to upper unitriangular
matrices. Its order is 3^9. Four displayed generators in the script
generate S, with the order checked independently by GAP's permutation
group algorithms. Nonfaithful actions cannot have a regular dual orbit.

`scripts/search_19_108_c27square.g` enumerates the conjugacy classes of
order-81 subgroups of S using `SubgroupsSolvableGroup` with the documented
exact-size option and an explicit final size filter, then all their dual orbits. Conjugation by S preserves
the criterion because every automorphism preserves 9A. A failed
periodicity check must be certified by an exact cyclotomic orbit sum
at an element of order 27. No floating-point sums are used.

The search and subsequent induction argument also cover nonsplit
extensions with A=C27 x C27 and index 81. The broader, proved statement
in `19.108-partial.md` covers all odd p and all abelian normal subgroups
of size at least p^6 in a group of order p^10. Neither statement covers
the full Notebook problem.

## Completed execution and controls

The final log `results/19.108-c27square.log` records 571 subgroup
classes and 1,596 regular orbits, with zero failures. The raw subgroup
enumerator returns 1,035 entries. Its documented size option guarantees
coverage but permits extra subgroups, which must be filtered explicitly.
The first run omitted that final filtering step and stopped at its
subgroup-order assertion. Its output is retained as
`results/19.108-c27square-incomplete-size-filter.log`; no cases from it
are included in the completed count. This was our use of the API,
not a GAP defect. The final rerun also exports every subgroup's integer
matrix generators for independent replay.

`scripts/verify_19_108_orbits.py` passes 2,624 exhaustive Fourier
controls, including a known D16 failure. It reconstructs the entire
19,683-element Sylow group by elementary matrix multiplication and
every saved order-81 subgroup without GAP. All 1,596 regular orbits
agree, as do 174,312 exact cyclotomic sums. The nine actual group/character
controls in `scripts/verify_19_108_characters.g` also pass: 197 values
checked and eight known violating elements detected at p=2.

The Notebook statement on printed/PDF page 145 was visually checked.
