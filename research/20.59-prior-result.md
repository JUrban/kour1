# Problem 20.59: prior affirmative theorem

12 September 2026. No new-candidate count.

Ashot Minasyan, *Property (LR) and an embedding theorem for virtually
free groups*, [arXiv:2603.17596v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.17596),
20 March 2026, Theorem 1.1, gives the exact affirmative answer.
Property (LR) means that every finitely generated subgroup is a
retract of some finite-index subgroup. The theorem covers virtually
free groups without a finite-generation restriction and explicitly
identifies Notebook Problem 20.59.

The introduction pp.1--3 and the final proof of Theorem 1.1 were read;
p.1 was viewed. The argument embeds a countable virtually free group
in a double of a finite group, then uses reflections at the boundary
of an invariant subtree to obtain a finite-index retract. The embedding
and reflection results are imported; the full paper has not been
independently audited. The PDF and extracted text are cached as
`references/cache/minasyan-2026-virtually-free-lr.pdf/.txt`, with the
statement image `20.59-minasyan-01.png`.

The primary arXiv metadata and author-hosted PDF were consulted on
12 September 2026. This is a prior resolution, not work produced
by this project. It does not settle the adjacent question 20.60
about virtual retracts of right-angled Artin groups.
