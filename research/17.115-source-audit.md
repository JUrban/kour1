# 17.115: Poss1975 is a partial result

Samuel Poss, *The Frattini subgroup of locally free groups*, Math. Z.
141 (1975), 199-204,
[publisher PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF01247306.pdf).
Retrieved on 12 September 2026 after earlier metadata-only searches.

All six pages of extracted text were read; printed pages 200 and 204
were also viewed. The paper does not answer the general Notebook
question. Theorem 1 concerns ascending rank-two free groups with a
fixed embedding satisfying specified Nielsen and word-end conditions.
Corollary 1 includes the perfect direct limit defined by
W=A^(-1)B^(-1)AB and V=A^(-1)BAB^(-1). Theorem 2, for a particular
family of higher-rank commutator embeddings, is explicitly stated
without proof. The final paragraph leaves other embeddings outside
the method.

The reading establishes this scope, rather than independently verifying
all the Nielsen-cancellation arguments. Those arguments are not imported
to claim a new result. In particular, the title cannot be cited as a
proof that every nonabelian locally free group has trivial Frattini
subgroup. Problem 17.115 remains unresolved here, with no new count.

in the joint17.115/21.98 evidence packet.
