# Problem 20.1: source scope and provenance

Checked 12 September 2026. Downloads are cached under `references/cache`.

- **Original problem:** `docs/21tkt.pdf`, p.146. The statement and page
  image were read and viewed. No restriction on direct factors appears.
- **Arezoomand--Iranmanesh--Praeger--Tracey:** *Totally 2-closed finite
  groups with trivial Fitting subgroup*, Bulletin of Mathematical Sciences
  14(1) (2024), 2350004, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1664360723500042).
  Published [author-repository PDF](https://wrap.warwick.ac.uk/id/eprint/196511/1/totally-2-closed-finite-groups-with-trivial-fitting-subgroup.pdf)
  cached as `arezoomand-2024-totally-2-closed-published.pdf/.txt`.
  Theorem 1.1 on PDF p.3 was read and viewed. Proposition 4.8 and its
  proof on pp.31--32 were read. They give the needed total 2-closure of
  J1. The underlying classification, Atlas and Magma inputs remain
  imported; this is not an independent audit of their full proof.
  The [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.02253) of 4 November 2021 is
  separately cached as `arezoomand-2021-totally-2-closed.pdf/.txt`.
  Its introduction pp.1--4, Proposition 4.8 pp.21--22, and the final
  discussion in Section 8 were read; p.2 was viewed. The published and
  preprint page numbers differ. Repository metadata records initial
  online publication on 18 March 2023 and the 2024 volume issue.
- **Chen--Ren:** *Notes on finite totally 2-closed permutation groups*,
  [arXiv:2202.09765v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.09765), 22 February 2022.
  Cached as `chen-ren-2022-totally-2-closed.pdf/.txt`.
  Introduction through Lemma 2.6, Theorem 3.5 and its proof, and Section 4
  were read; pp.5--6 were viewed. Theorem C/3.5 explicitly assumes that
  the product is nilpotent. Section 4 discusses closure in a prescribed
  disjoint union action. Neither statement directly gives the present
  coprime product lemma for arbitrary finite factors. No result from
  this paper is needed for our proof.
- **Abdollahi--Arezoomand--Tracey:** *On finite totally 2-closed groups*,
  [published PDF](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mathematique/item/10.5802/crmath.355.pdf),
  Comptes Rendus Mathématique 360 (2022), 1001--1008.
  Web text of the introductory results and preliminary lemmas was
  inspected, including the coprime transitive action decomposition in
  Lemma 7. This supports the background comparison only; no theorem is
  imported from this source and no local visual audit is claimed.

Targeted searches combined total 2-closure, coprime/direct products,
nontrivial Fitting subgroup, J1 and cyclic factors. They did not locate
an exact prior statement of the proposed example. Search absence is
not evidence sufficient to certify priority. Chen--Ren's nilpotent
case and the earlier transitive decomposition must receive credit in
any later publication of the argument.
