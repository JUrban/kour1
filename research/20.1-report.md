# Problem 20.1: completed candidate report

12 September 2026. Affirmative candidate, pending independent review
and priority checks.

The example is **J1 x C13**, of order **2,282,280**, with Fitting
subgroup exactly C13. A self-contained orbit-partition argument proves
that a direct product of coprime finite totally 2-closed groups is
totally 2-closed. The published total 2-closure theorem for J1 is the
only substantial imported input. Prime cyclic total 2-closure and the
extension from finite to arbitrary action sets are proved explicitly.
See `research/20.1-proof.md` and the nineteen-point internal review.

The controls cover 177 faithful coset-union actions of C2 x C3,
Q8 x C3, S3 x C5 and A4 x C5: respectively 10, 67, 47 and 53 cases.
There are seven transitive cases, 64 with two orbits, and 106 with
three orbits; six cases repeat a constituent, and the maximum degree
is 60. The selection uses every single subgroup class, every pair of
distinct classes, doubled single classes, and triples of distinct
classes of total degree at most 30. Unfaithful actions and actions
of degree greater than 60 are omitted. This is bounded coverage.

GAP computes the 2-closure by intersecting all orbital stabilizers
inside the independent product of the transitive constituent
2-closures. Python independently enumerates all colour-preserving
permutations of the complete directed graph whose colours are the
ordered-pair orbits. It reconstructs both factor groups from their
generators and verifies the singleton intersections, faithful quotient
actions and injectivity of the two induced quotient actions of the
closure. Every enumerated closure agrees exactly with GAP.

The shared-prime mutation V4 on three two-point orbits has closure
order eight. Omitting cross-orbit information enlarges a diagonal C3
action from order three to nine. The S3 and A4 products have 14 and
34 enlarged closures respectively, testing the need for total closure
of each factor. The C2 and Q8 products have none. GAP's J1 character
table order agrees with 175560; this is an order control, not a proof
of the J1 theorem.

Two initial GAP runs failed: unsorted orbit lists were rejected by
OnSets, then the built-in TwoClosure rejected an intransitive action.
Both logs remain archived. The corrected GAP wrapper actually exits
zero in 5.17 seconds; the independent Python wrapper actually exits
zero in 3.13 seconds. Both final logs are clean and contain their
required sentinels. Scripts, controls, logs and sources are bound by
the packet manifest and replay auditor.

The product argument covers all faithful actions, so the finite checks
are supporting controls only. The proof does not determine the
smallest example and does not classify groups with nontrivial Fitting
subgroup. Relevant source statements were inspected, but the full J1
classification proof was not independently reconstructed. No exact
prior example was located in the targeted searches; priority remains
unconfirmed. Nothing has been submitted, sent to others or pushed.
