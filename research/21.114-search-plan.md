## Target and proposed reduction

Write a(G)=|G/G'|. A finite group is weakly ab-maximal when a(H)<=a(G)
for every subgroup H. The cited Lisi--Sabatini paper reduces the problem to
p-groups, proves quotient and direct-product closure, and gives metabelian
examples of unbounded nilpotency class. Its Question A is the exact present
question; nilpotency class alone is insufficient.

The proposed construction is this: if Z<=Z(P) has order p and P/Z is weakly
ab-maximal, form the central product of P with an extraspecial group E of
order p^(1+2r), identifying Z with Z(E), for r>=1. Its abelianization has
order a(P/Z)p^(2r). For a subgroup H we may first replace H by HZ. If
Z<=H', the quotient H/Z is a subgroup of (P/Z) x C_p^(2r), so the desired
bound follows from direct-product closure. Otherwise H intersect E is
abelian, of order at most p^(r+1), and the quotient image in P/Z supplies
the same bound. This gives weak ab-maximality while preserving a copy of P.
The proof and possible capability obstruction must be checked before using
this to claim an unbounded family.
