# Problem 19.61: exhaustive G2 prime-field pilot

12 September 2026. Bounded negative result, not a solution of the general
problem. This supplements the independently archived C2 pilot.

Every carpet over G2(F2) and G2(F3) is already closed in the tested integral
Chevalley matrix model. There are respectively 174 and 217 carpets. No
counterexample, and not even a larger closure, was found.

| Field | Additive assignments | Valid carpets | Larger closures | Failures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| F2 | 4,096 | 174 | 0 | 0 |
| F3 | 4,096 | 217 | 0 | 0 |

## Construction and exhaustive coverage

`scripts/export_19_61_g2.g` obtains a rational Chevalley basis of the
14-dimensional Lie algebra of type G2 from GAP. The order is six positive
root vectors, six negative root vectors, and two Cartan vectors. It exports
the twelve root weights, all fourteen adjoint matrices, the Cartan matrix
[[2,-1],[-3,2]], and each integral divided power A_r^k/k! up to nilpotence.
The convention is that columns represent left multiplication by a basis
vector. The integral Chevalley construction, including its identification
as type G2, is imported from GAP's Chevalley basis implementation; the
finite computations do not constitute an independent construction theorem.

The separate Python preparation checks antisymmetry, all 2,744 basis
Jacobi triples, the Cartan weights, integrality, every divided-power
identity, and termination of each root exponential. After reduction
modulo p it checks x_r(t)x_r(u)=x_r(t+u) and injectivity of each root map.

For each ordered pair of distinct nonopposite roots, it lists all roots
ir+js with positive i,j (at most four terms), ordered by i+j and then i,j.
It enumerates every parameter tuple in that ordered product and verifies
uniqueness. Each actual matrix commutator is looked up in this normal
form for every t,u. Thus vanishing bad-prime coefficients are retained
exactly; the test does not assume a generic-characteristic formula.
Every additive subgroup of a prime field is either zero or the whole
field, so all 2^12 assignments are checked against all factor conditions.

Complete row-vector orbits give permutation actions of degrees 63 and
728. GAP checks the resulting ambient orders 12,096 and 4,245,696 against
q^6(q^6-1)(q^2-1). It generates the subgroup for each valid assignment,
tests membership of every root element, and tests the resulting closure
against the same carpet conditions.

## Independent controls and the action kernel

The orbit spans have ranks 14 over F2 and 7 over F3. In particular the
F3 orbit is **not** claimed to span the adjoint module. The conclusion
about carpet closure does not rely on an unproved faithfulness assertion
for that action: `scripts/check_19_61_g2.py` independently enumerates
the generated groups as actual 14-by-14 matrices and checks every root
membership for all 390 cases other than the full F3 assignment.
The latter assignment has full root closure by its definition.

| Field | Matrix-enumerated cases | Full assignment handled directly | Matrix states |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| F2 | 174 | 0 | 17,305 |
| F3 | 216 | 1 | 109,693 |
| Total | 390 | 1 | 126,998 |

The largest enumerated groups have orders 12,096 and 5,832. The 20,000
state guard was never reached; no truncated enumeration is accepted.
The matrix checker uses right multiplication by the sparse deviations
of root matrices from the identity and does not use permutation data.
All enumerated matrix orders also agree with GAP's independently
exported subgroup orders. The same integral matrices and initial
carpet preparation are shared inputs, so these are independent closure
calculations, not independent sources of the Chevalley representation.

## Reproducibility and limits

The process observations record actual exit codes and tool chunks for
the export, preparation, both GAP searches, both order exports, and
matrix controls. All seven exited zero and their logs have the expected
sentinels. `scripts/audit_19_61_g2.py` verifies the frozen hash manifest,
recreates both complete GAP inputs byte for byte in a temporary
directory, compares preparation data apart from elapsed time, and
replays every matrix control. Its completion is recorded separately
after the manifest is frozen, avoiding a self-referential hash.

This covers two prime fields only. It neither establishes closure over
arbitrary characteristic-two or characteristic-three rings nor addresses
other root systems. The complete-candidate count remains 41.
