# 20.122: exact finite intersection families

September 12, 2026. Initial investigation plan. Subsequent status:
the order-48 hit now has a complete elementary proof for (b) and its
soluble version in (c); see `20.122-proof.md` and `20.122-report.md`.
The minimum-order assertion remains unresolved.

For nilpotent subgroups A,B,C of a finite G the problem concerns all
minimal intersections A intersection B^x intersection C^y, not merely
the existence of one intersection in F(G). Zenkov's 2021 theorem gives
the latter, so it cannot be substituted for either question here.
The source abstract was read; its full classification proof is not
being imported as a proof of the stronger assertions.

For a bounded pilot, enumerate conjugacy classes of nilpotent subgroups.
Only representatives outside F(G) need be considered in any of the
three positions, since otherwise every intersection lies in F(G).
Fix A as a representative; conjugacy invariance makes this complete
for the A position. The full conjugacy orbits of B and C cover x,y.
Interchange B,C to halve the number of families, without interchanging
the distinguished A position.

Export literal subsets of the group, its right-regular generator
permutations, and the Fitting subgroup. Python integer bitsets then
enumerate each complete intersection family. If a pair family
A intersection B^x contains 1, every corresponding triple family has
1 as its only minimal member; this is a valid shortcut for both parts.
Otherwise retain every distinct triple intersection and test inclusion
minimality and minimum cardinality separately.

A hit must be independently reconstructed from a faithful finite
group model, with nilpotency of all three inputs and an explicit
element of the retained minimum outside the actual Fitting subgroup.
One way to certify the last condition is to show its normal closure
is not nilpotent. A negative finite screen will remain bounded evidence,
with catalogue completeness and any unreconstructed inputs identified.

Start with orders 24,36,48,54,60,72,96,108,120,144,162,192, omitting
nilpotent ambient groups. Use at most four GAP workers and explicit
per-order time limits, collecting actual exits, stderr and markers.
An incomplete order is an omission, not a negative result.
