# 20.30: bounded character-table screen

The question asks whether |G|<=b(G)^2 for every perfect centreless
finite group, where b(G) is the largest conjugacy-class size.

The exact screen `scripts/search_20_30.g` processes all2,750 ordinary
CTblLib entries. A table is eligible precisely when it has exactly
one linear character and exactly one class of size one. These
conditions express G=G' and Z(G)=1. There are340 eligible entries;
none violates the inequality. Entries need not represent different
isomorphism types. This is bounded evidence, not a proof for all groups.

`scripts/verify_20_30_52.g` independently constructs actual permutation
groups and computes full conjugacy orbits under their generators. It
compares the class-size lists with character tables, and compares the
table eligibility predicate with actual derived subgroups and centres.
Controls include simple groups, a centreless perfect affine group,
a central cover, a group with a large abelian direct factor, and
nonperfect groups. All nine controls pass. The completed-log parser
checks all340 eligible rows and every completion marker.

The general problem and literature novelty remain unresolved here.
