# Problem 11.124: prior affirmative preprint

Checked 2026-09-11. Excluded from the new-candidate count.

Artur Tursunbaev, *Subgroups of free groups with the same commutator
subgroup*, arXiv:2609.06755v1, submitted 6 September 2026, proves the
exact assertion as Corollary 1.2. Theorem 1.1 treats arbitrary ranks:
subgroups H,K of a free group with H'=K' nontrivial are equal.

Primary source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06755v1
Full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.06755v1
The seven-page PDF (391,663 bytes) and extracted text are retained.
All six mathematical pages, including every proof, were read; pp.2,3,6
were additionally visually checked. Page7 contains only the address.

The internal review checked the finite-index rational relation-module
argument, the finite path obstruction in Schreier graphs, the
intersection dichotomy, the resulting finite family of possible
subgroups, and the final torsion-free abelianization argument. No gap
was found. Auslander--Lyndon's two classical results, Brown's homology
sequence and the relation-module sequence are imported; the original
1955 paper and those textbook proofs were not fully audited here.
This is not a claim of refereed status or an independent new proof.

For the Notebook deduction, R is free and noncyclic, so R' is
nontrivial. Normality of R' gives (R^g)'=R' for every g in F.
Theorem 1.1 gives R^g=R, hence R is normal in F.

The source handles arbitrary rank explicitly in the relation-module
step; no finite generation assumption is being inserted. Its theorem
must not be extended to a mere inclusion of derived subgroups:
Notebook Archive13.62 records Dunwoody's counterexample to that
stronger assertion. The nearby Problem11.115 also has different
hypotheses and is not resolved by this normalizer theorem.
