# Problem19.61: G2 in the two bad prime characteristics

12 September2026. Follow-up to the frozen five-ring C2 packet.

The next pilot uses G2 over F2 and F3, both outside the hypotheses
of the source's general G2 result. Export a rational Chevalley basis
from GAP and the integral divided powers of all twelve adjoint root
matrices. Check the integer Lie brackets, Jacobi identities, weights
and divided powers independently in Python before reducing modulo2
or3. Derive every commutator factor by exact matrix multiplication,
retaining all possible terms and their actual coefficients.

Over either prime field every additive subgroup is0 or the whole
field, so only4,096 assignments to the twelve roots need examination.
Use a complete small orbit of the integral representation reduced
modulo the prime to construct a permutation action, and check the
ambient order q^6(q^6-1)(q^2-1) before running the carpet search.
Faithfulness of this action and the identification with the Chevalley
model must be accounted for explicitly if a counterexample is found.

Any hit requires verification of the initial carpet and direct
nonmembership of an individual commutator factor, not merely a larger
closure. The prior C2 results are unchanged. TypeC3 over F2 is a
separate possible follow-up; no C3 computation has yet been launched.
