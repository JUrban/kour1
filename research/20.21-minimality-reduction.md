# Problem 20.21: audit of the prior minimality reduction

12 September 2026. This is a prior result of Marston Conder and
Natalia Maslova, not a new theorem of this project.

Source: N.V. Maslova, *2023 Ural workshop on group theory and
combinatorics*, Trudy Inst. Mat. i Mekh. UrO RAN30(1),2024,284–293,

Suppose G is a smallest counterexample, K,L are its required kernels,
M=KL and N=K intersection L. The following reconstruction checks the
whole reduction using only elementary finite-group arguments.

First K differs from L, and their equal orders ensure neither contains
the other. Thus M/L is a nontrivial normal subgroup of A4, hence V4
or A4. If M=G, the isomorphism theorems give K/N=A4 and L/N=C12.
An isomorphism f:K->L takes N to a normal subgroup R of L with
L/R=A4 and R isomorphic to N. The two normal subgroups N,R of L
then make the strictly smaller group L a counterexample. Therefore
M has index3, M/K=C4, M/L=V4 and [G:N]=48.

The map G/N -> G/K x G/L is injective. Its image is precisely the
fiber product of C12 and A4 over their common quotient G/M=C3:
inclusion is immediate and both groups have order48. Write C12 as
C4 x C3. Identifying its C3 factor with the quotient A4/V4
identifies this fiber product with C4 x A4. In particular M/N is
C4 x V4, a2-group.

If an odd prime p divides |M|, every Sylow p-subgroup of M lies in
N, because its image in M/N is trivial. Thus N,K,L,M have exactly
the same Sylow p-subgroups, not just the same Sylow orders. Let T
be the subgroup they generate. It is nontrivial, characteristic in
M, normal in G, and contained in N. Moreover any isomorphism K->L
takes this same T to itself. Hence K/T and L/T are isomorphic,
normal in the smaller G/T, with the original two quotients. This
contradicts minimality. Consequently M is a2-group and
|G|=48 times a power of2.

The printed part(1) has a harmless typographical issue: it writes
psi(K)=psi(L), whereas the intended assertion is that no
automorphism takes K to L. That assertion follows directly from
the different quotient isomorphism types. Part(1) is not needed
for the reduction above. No non-characteristic-subgroup filter
follows from it.

Combining this prior reduction with the frozen six-order pilot
rules out every counterexample of order less than768: if any
existed, a smallest one would have order48,96,192 or384, all
covered by that pilot. This lower bound remains computationally
conditional on the stated GAP catalogue and enumeration checks.
It is not a complete negative answer to the original problem.
