# Problem 15.76(b): source and scope audit

12 September 2026. Searches used both “solvable” and “soluble”, free
metabelian groups, associative words, category automorphisms, and
automorphisms of endomorphism semigroups. No exact earlier theorem for
all S_d was located. This is a bounded literature search, not proof of
new priority. No outside correspondence was sent.

## Original question

`docs/21tkt.pdf`, printed/PDF page 93, was read and visually inspected;
the image is `references/cache/15.76-notebook-p93.png`. Part (b) asks
about solvable and metabelian analogues of the full group and full
fixed-class nilpotent varieties. Our theorem uses the full varieties
S_d, d>=1, and includes the full metabelian variety. The assertion for
arbitrary subvarieties would be false by the 2019 source below.

## Category method and nilpotent predecessor

A. Tsurkov, *Automorphisms of the category of the free nilpotent groups
of the fixed class of nilpotency*, IJAC 17 (2007), 1273–1281,
[arXiv:math/0509541](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0509541),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1142/S021819670700413X).
The cached PDF is v4, 14 April 2006; its typeset front page separately
says 2 July 2018. We do not use that compile date as publication date.
Files: `tsurkov-2006-category-nilpotent.pdf/.txt`.

All mathematical text was read across the initial and follow-up reads.
Pages 1, 2, 10 and 11 were also viewed. Section 1 presents the verbal
operations method and credits Plotkin--Zhitomirski; Lemma 2.2 handles
nilpotent words and Theorem 2.1 proves the category statement. Our
section 3 is a self-contained instance of this established method.
The new candidate step is the affine coefficient induction for S_d,
not the general idea of reducing category automorphisms to words.
No theorem from this paper is an unproved premise of our proof.

## Essential limitation and prior answer to 20.72

R. Barbosa Fernandes and A. Tsurkov, *Automorphisms of the category of
finitely generated free groups of the some subvariety of the variety
of all groups*, [arXiv:1909.05955v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05955v1),
12 September 2019 (front-page date 16 September).
Files: `fernandes-tsurkov-2019-category-periodic.pdf/.txt`.

Page 4 specifies exponent dividing four, the metabelian identity, and
nilpotency class at most four. Theorem 9.1, pages 29–30, gives an outer
automorphism quotient of order two. The introduction, definitions
through section 2.1, and the final theorem proof were read; pages 4,
29 and 30 were viewed. The intermediate word and commutator
calculations were not fully audited. Thus the existence theorem is
an explicitly imported prior result. It answers the existence question
20.72 affirmatively and rules out an arbitrary-subvariety extension of
our theorem. Neither deduction is counted as a new solution here.

## Earlier word-operation literature

A. Hulanicki and S. Świerczkowski, *On group operations other than xy
or yx*, Publicationes Mathematicae Debrecen 9 (1962), 142–148,
[primary PDF](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/paper/2858/download/10_5486_PMD_1962_9_1-2_15.pdf).
The seven scanned pages were all read visually and retained as
`hulanicki-1962-page-1.png` through `-7.png`; `pdftotext` produced only
page breaks. The cache also contained a byte-identical earlier download.
Theorems 1 and 2 concern class-two nilpotent groups and reversible word
operations. They do not state our all-S_d associative-word lemma.
Their examples explain why mere associativity is much less restrictive
in a fixed nilpotent variety. No result is imported into our proof.

C. D. H. Cooper, *Words which give rise to another group operation for
a given group*, LNM 372 (1974), 221–225,
[chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/BFb0065173).
Files `cooper-group-words-1974.pdf/.txt` were already retained for 6.47.
All five pages were read again; earlier page views 221–223 are retained.
Theorem 1 treats class at most three. Theorem 3 asserts preservation of
solubility under a group word operation; it does not classify operations
uniformly on all groups of a fixed derived length. Its omitted
class-three calculations are not independently reconstructed here.

E. Płonka, *Associative words in the symmetric group of degree three*,
Algebra and Discrete Mathematics 15 (2013), 83–95,
[primary PDF](https://admjournal.luguniv.edu.ua/index.php/adm/article/download/736/267).
The cache filename `holubowski-associative-s3.pdf/.txt` is an old naming
error: the author is Płonka. The introduction, preliminary results
through Proposition 2.3, and final Lemma 3.8/Theorem 3.9 and references
were read; the intermediate proof was not fully read and no page view
is claimed. This concerns one finite group and supplies bibliography,
not an input to our theorem.

The Atabekyan--Aslanyan2018 paper on endomorphism semigroups of
relatively free groups was an additional search lead. Only its abstract
was seen through search; its cyclic-centralizer hypothesis does not
match the free solvable groups under study. No theorem from it is used.

## Proof dependencies

The local proof supplies its own Magnus kernel argument using a graph
covering and the elementary correspondence between graph homology and
abelianized fundamental groups. The other inputs are free objects,
coproducts, integral Laurent polynomial arithmetic, and derived series.
It does not rely on a general group-ring unit theorem, on the
nilpotent classification, or on the 2019 counterexample's calculations.
