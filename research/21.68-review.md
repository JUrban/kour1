# Internal audit of the order-2592 counterexample

Status: all written proof steps and two independent computations pass.
This is an internal audit, not outside review or a formal proof-assistant
certificate. Candidate negative resolution of Notebook 21.68.

## Statement and novelty

The supplied PDF page177 was visually checked. It permits a sequence of
abelian semidirect extensions followed by quotients; the constructed group
uses only split extensions. There is no nilpotency, prime-power, or
coprimality restriction. Monomial refers to all irreducible complex
characters, not merely a faithful matrix realization by monomial matrices.

Read Kida's published Conjecture1.3, Definition2.1, Lemmas2.3--2.7,
and Example5.5, available at https://d-nb.info/1364524341/34 . The base
SmallGroup(96,204) and its SL(2,3) subgroup are explicitly known there.
They are not claimed as new. The order2592 extension and its obstructed
degree8 character are the proposed resolution. The paper's computation
only reaches order240. Its Theorem1.4 imposes closure under subgroups on
an entire class; the construction does not satisfy that extra hypothesis.

Queries combining semiabelian/semi-abelian, monomial, counterexample,
Kida, 21.68, 2592, and 2026 found no earlier primary resolution. Several
searches returned irrelevant material or secondary status pages. These
do not prove novelty. The July2026 van Doorn--Judin--Monticone--Morrison
paper's complete list of eight problems was also inspected and does not
include 21.68 (nor any of our other three current candidate resolutions).

## Mathematical failure points checked

1. **Quaternion subgroup:** The displayed x,y have all three nonidentity
   image classes of order four, with the same central square z. Their
   permutation images give precisely four classes, and the two lifts in
   each class are distinct. Thus Q really has order8 and is quaternion.
2. **Order-three action:** The explicit permutation r cycles x,y,xy under
   left conjugation, so H has order24. The rational quaternion s realizes
   these exact signs and has order3; no order6 lift is accidentally used.
3. **No index-two subgroup:** The conjugation relations kill x and y in
   every abelian quotient. The remaining C3 quotient exists. Every
   index2 subgroup would be normal, contradicting H_ab=C3.
4. **Correct action on B:** It is the action of K on K/H, not the
   original four coordinates in the definition of K. Confusing these
   two four-point actions would give the wrong stabilizer. Both scripts
   construct the coset action from H explicitly.
5. **Augmentation reduction:** B has dimension3 over F3. All four
   coordinate characters remain distinct, so the inertia group is
   exactly B semidirect H. It is insufficient to show only containment.
6. **Character extension:** The selected coordinate character is H-fixed,
   which makes (b,h) -> lambda(b) an actual homomorphism on I. Tensoring
   with the quaternion representation gives an actual degree2 character.
7. **Irreducibility:** Four distinct B-weights with irreducible stabilizer
   action prove irreducibility of the degree8 induction. Both exact
   computations also find character norm1.
8. **Arbitrary inducing subgroup:** The proof does not assume a proposed
   monomial inducing subgroup contains B. It proves this from normality
   and the coprime integers |B|=27 and [G:L]=8. Then its linear character
   forces L inside the appropriate inertia subgroup and would produce
   the impossible index2 subgroup of H.

## Computational evidence

`scripts/verify_21_68.g` constructs K as an affine permutation group on
the eight even binary vectors. It identifies K as SmallGroup(96,204),
H as SmallGroup(24,3), verifies the split abelian factors, and constructs
G faithfully as a permutation group on twenty points. It builds the
inertia character and induces it, obtaining degree8 and exact norm1.
The quick `TestMonomial` routine returns unknown, which is not a result;
the subsequent complete `IsMonomial` call returns false, and the final
PASS assertion records this. The initial use of a protected GAP global
name failed before constructing any group; that log is retained and the
name was corrected before the successful run.

`scripts/verify_21_68.py` is independent of GAP and algebra packages.
It uses binary vectors and explicit permutations for K, rational
quaternions for rho, and pairs of integers for Z[zeta3]. It checks all
9,216 products of K, all576 representation products in H, all9,216
coset-action product identities, and all2,592 exact induced-character
values. Their squared absolute values sum to2592, certifying norm1.
It separately obtains H_ab=C3 and the exact four-point stabilizer.

The mathematical proof is self-contained; the computations are checks
against sign, action, order, and character mistakes. The final claim
remains a candidate solution pending independent review.
