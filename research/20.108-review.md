# Internal review of20.108(a),(b)

- The requested groups may be finite, soluble, and Frobenius. There
  is no perfectness assumption. The constructed G has order605 and
  trivial centre, meeting both(a) and(b). It says nothing about(c).
- The subgroup V is the unique Sylow11-subgroup and characteristic.
  Its automorphism action is F_11-linear because it is elementary
  abelian. A quotient multiplier must preserve the two eigenvalues.
  All four units modulo5 were checked; only1 works.
- Every vector component for the image of the complement generator
  is permissible, since its norm sum vanishes. Thus the automorphism
  classification gives the full group, not just an explicit subgroup.
- The holomorph is the full normalizer of the left regular copy.
  Its affine form follows from all automorphisms plus all translations;
  four translation-function coefficients are independently variable.
- Formula(3) is theta h theta^(-1) with ordinary function composition.
  GAP uses the opposite exponent-conjugation convention, which tests
  the inverse inclusion and gives the same normalizer conclusion.
- Theta's square is excluded from the holomorph by its action on the
  quotient coordinate, not merely by failure to be a group automorphism.
  This is essential: a holomorph element need not fix the identity.
- All605 points and all seven holomorph generators were checked in
  both systems; the holomorph order is independently compared with
  Aut(SmallGroup(605,5)). The Python full matrix enumeration verifies
  the only delicate automorphism restriction.
- The8,000 additional parameter cases and failed multiplier-omission
  control test the normalizer composition and its orientation.
- In the general family, the half-orbit of eigenvalue exponents has
  trivial multiplicative stabilizer. The last-coordinate sign uses
  r^d=-1; the quotient action proves the full claimed coset order.
- For the unboundedness argument, any prime dividing
  1+q+...+q^(q-1) cannot be q. The order of q modulo that prime divides
  q and is not1, hence is q. This supplies p=1 mod q without an
  unproved assertion about infinitely many primes of a specified form.
- The result exhibits a cyclic subgroup of order4 in T(G). It does
  not identify the full normalizer or claim T(G) is exactly C4.
- The original paper identified605 as an exception; that contribution
  is credited. Novelty and outside verification remain pending.
