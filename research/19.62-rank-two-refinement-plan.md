# A possible rank-two refinement of the 19.62 proof

12 September 2026. The complete all-type packet is frozen at d205de8.
This is a separate attempt to shorten its proof, not a change to that
packet's checked claims.

Try the stronger inclusion A_p^2 B_-p contained in B_p. Since the
opposite derived factor enters linearly, it is enough to check

    c x^2 a^i b^j in B_p,

where x belongs to A_p, a belongs to A_r, b belongs to A_s, and
i r+j s=-p is a Chevalley commutator term with absolute constant c.
Only three independent variables occur. All their root labels lie in
the plane spanned by r and s. Its intersection with the ambient root
system is of type A2, B2 or G2, and restriction preserves all root
strings. Thus these three types would suffice in every rank.
