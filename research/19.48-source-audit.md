## Prior preprint

*An irreducible elementary net over an algebraic extension that is not
closed*, author label GroupTheory50, ProofForum PF:2026.000007 v1.
The landing page records upload by Mattia Brescia on 1 September 2026;
the PDF is dated 27 August. Both pages were read, the proof page was
viewed, and the PDF and TeX were downloaded.
[Primary preprint](https://www.proofforum.org/abs/PF:2026.000007).

Its example takes R=Z and a root alpha of X^3-X-1, with T=Z[alpha].
The exceptional levels are A=Z+Zalpha+2Zalpha^2 and
B=Z+2Zalpha+Z(alpha^2-1); other levels are 2T. The unit identity
alpha^-1=alpha^2-1 lets permitted transvections produce
diag(alpha,alpha^-1). Conjugation gives the forbidden upper parameter
alpha^2. The ideal 2T proves the net condition and irreducibility.
The cubic has no rational root, ensuring coefficient independence.
These steps check directly. A separate 6-by-6 integer-matrix calculation
also verifies the unit, diagonal product and conjugation identities.

This is a preprint, not an independently human-reviewed publication.
Its posted AI review is not used as mathematical evidence. The short
argument itself and the independent exact checks support recording the
question as having a prior negative answer.

## Exact Notebook assumptions and nearby positive results

The supplied Notebook's printed page 138 was viewed. It requires a PID R,
its fraction field k, an algebraic extension K/k, nonzero levels, and
R-module structure. It does not require k-vector-space structure or K=k.
The retained characteristic-three example has R=F3[t], K=F9(t), and
each level free of rank two over R.

Koibaev--Nuzhin2017 assumes the levels are k-subspaces, and classifies
such irreducible nets over an algebraic extension of k. Only its primary
abstract was inspected here; it is not an input to our proof.
[Primary abstract](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/smj2847).

Dryaeva--Koibaev--Nuzhin2017 treats the ambient fraction field itself.
The university abstract confirms that hypothesis. Its full proof was
not imported, and a direct POMI PDF download failed TLS certificate
verification. [University record](https://elib.sfu-kras.ru/handle/2311/111023).

The 2025 Dedekind-domain extension likewise assumes the ambient field
is the fraction field of the module's base domain; its primary abstract
was read in the 21.76 source audit.
[Primary abstract](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/aa1979).

The broader 2015 nonclosed-carpet paper was located at Math-Net timm1212.
Its abstract alone does not establish the extra PID-module and algebraic
extension conditions in 19.48; the full-PDF web request failed. No claim
about its examples' exact scope is made here. Franchuk2019's G2 theorem
was also read; it assumes the module base is a field, so does not apply
to our F3[t]-modules.
