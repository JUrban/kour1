# 7.31: the prior affirmative answer of Li--Zhu

Status: excluded from new-result priority. The supplied Notebook page23
was visually checked. Its group A is a subgroup of Aut(G), where G is a
finite nonabelian 2-group and A is transitive on involutions.

Cai Heng Li and Yan Zhou Zhu,
[*A proof of Gross' conjecture on 2-automorphic 2-groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.16416v2),
[Proc. London Math. Soc.129(5) (2024),e70002](https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.70002),
Theorem1.1, classifies finite p-groups whose order-p elements form one
automorphism orbit. Its introduction explicitly identifies Notebook7.31.
For a nonabelian 2-group, it gives a generalized quaternion group or a
Suzuki 2-group; in the latter case the full automorphism group is soluble.

Here is the elementary remaining inference to the printed quantifiers.
Transitivity of A implies transitivity of Aut(G). The homocyclic case is
excluded by nonabelianness. Aut(Q8) is S4, hence soluble. For a generalized
quaternion group of order at least16, its unique cyclic subgroup of index
two is characteristic. Writing its usual generators as a,b, every
automorphism has the form a -> a^r, b -> a^s b, where r is odd. These
automorphisms form a 2-group. Thus in every case Aut(G), and consequently
its subgroup A, is soluble.
