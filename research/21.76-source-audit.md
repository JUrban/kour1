## Known rank-one lemma

V. A. Koibaev, *Closed pairs*, Vladikavkaz Mathematical Journal 13(3)
(2011), 36–41. The theorem on page 36 proves closedness for pairs of
additive subgroups of xD[x], where D is an integral domain. It therefore
includes the pair (kx,kx) used here. All six pages were read through the
primary PDF; pages 36 and 40 were also viewed as images. Section 5's
irreducible noncompletable example is in characteristic two. The paper
does not display the two-variable lift used in our proof.
[Journal PDF](https://www.vmj.ru/articles/2011_3_4.pdf).

## The cited characteristic-zero example

V. A. Koibaev, *Closed elementary nets over a field of characteristic 0*,
Sibirsk. Mat. Zh. 62(2) (2021), 326–332; English translation 262–266.
The primary full seven-page text was read through Math-Net. Its example
uses B=Zx+2Zx^3+x^4Z[x] at the two exceptional positions, and x^4Z[x]
elsewhere. It confirms the definitions and attributes the earlier
characteristic-two example to the 2011 Trudy IMM article cited in the
Notebook. It does not state either odd-characteristic construction here.
[Primary article](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/smj7558).

## Nearby results and their hypotheses

Koibaev's 2019 discrete-valuation-ring theorem assumes that the derived
levels are ideals of the discrete valuation ring and concerns sufficiency
of closedness of opposite pairs. Those hypotheses are explicit in the
primary abstract. It is not an announced noncompletable example.
[Primary abstract](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/jsfu803).

Koibaev's 2025 Dedekind-domain theorem assumes levels are modules over
that domain and that the ambient field is its fraction field. Its
primary abstract gives a classification and closedness under those
hypotheses. Only the abstract was audited here; its full proof is not
imported. The polynomial ring k[x,y] in construction (1) is not a
Dedekind domain, and its exceptional levels are not k[x,y]-modules.
The exceptional levels in construction (3) are not F9[t]-modules.
[Primary abstract](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/aa1979).
