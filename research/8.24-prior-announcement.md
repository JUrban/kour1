# Problem 8.24: finite-generation result announced in 2013/2014

11 September 2026. Literature lead only; no new-candidate count.

V. M. Kopytov, *Locally Indicable Groups of Finite Mal'tsev Rank*,
Algebra and Logic 52 (2014), 511--512; Russian original 52 (2013),
769--771, states in Theorem 1 that every finitely generated locally
indicable group of finite rank is soluble. It defines rank by a
uniform bound on generators of finitely generated subgroups. Theorem 2
announces an embedding in a two-generated group preserving local
indicability and finite rank.
[Primary paper](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s10469-014-9263-z.pdf).

Both pages were read and visually checked. The note announces the
results and sketches the reduction; it does not give their full proofs.
Theorem 1 includes finite generation. Since bi-orderable groups are
locally indicable, it addresses the finitely generated case of 8.24.
No full proof of that theorem or additional uniform derived-length
argument for arbitrary groups is supplied here. In particular, the
finite-generation hypothesis must not be silently dropped. This lead
is retained separately from independently established results.
