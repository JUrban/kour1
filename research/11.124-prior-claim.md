# 11.124: exact prior affirmative claim

Excluded from new-result priority. Artur Tursunbaev,
[*Subgroups of free groups with the same commutator subgroup*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.06755v1),
version 1 submitted 6 September 2026, claims the exact affirmative answer
as Corollary 1.2. The stronger Theorem 1.1 says that subgroups of a free
group of arbitrary rank with the same nontrivial derived subgroup are equal.

The primary abstract and the full seven-page preprint were read on
11 September. Its Schreier-chain and finite-family arguments cover
infinite ranks and indices explicitly. No gap was identified in this
reading. The imported Auslander--Lyndon results were not independently
re-read, and this recent preprint has not received outside review here.

The deduction of the printed question is direct: normality of R' makes
R and every conjugate have the same nontrivial derived subgroup.
No new solution is claimed by this project.
