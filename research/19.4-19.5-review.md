# Internal audit of the prior root-amalgam deduction

1. **Printed scope.** Page 132 permits infinitely many finitely
   generated free, free abelian, or abelian factors. Cyclic factors
   meet all three conditions. The resulting group is nonabelian.
2. **Correct amalgam.** All factors share one abstract infinite
   cyclic group, embedded by z -> a_n^n. The finite-amalgam normal
   form theorem and increasing union give the specified group.
3. **Infinite order.** The explicit rational character z -> 1,
   a_n -> 1/n proves z is not torsion, independently of GAP.
4. **Centrality.** Each generator commutes with its own nth power z.
   This is centrality in the entire group, not just within a factor.
5. **Arbitrary subgroups.** The lower-bound argument treats any S,
   not merely finitely generated or normal subgroups of G.
6. **Coset exponent.** d! works for a subgroup of index d even when
   it is not normal, via the permutation action on cosets.
7. **Integer obstruction.** An infinite cyclic quotient K/S sends
   z to a generator; n divides d! would then hold for every n>=2,
   an immediate contradiction.
8. **Upper subgroup and existence.** Allenby's theorem supplies
   mu(G)<=H. Thus both bounds agree and psi(G) actually exists.
   Merely proving lambda(G) nontrivial would not establish this.
9. **Priority.** Allenby Theorem 4 is the exact prior calculation,
   reproduced in Azarian 2011 Theorem 3.17. No new candidate counted.
10. **Source limits.** Allenby 2000 read in full; original 1999
    upper-bound proof not read. The present result is a precise
    consequence of the published theorem, not an audit of its entire
    dependency chain. Outside review remains pending.
