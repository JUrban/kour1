# Source reading for the rank-one reduction question

12 September 2026. Full 15.46 remains unresolved here.

Itarova--Koibaev, *Decomposition of Elementary Transvection in Elementary
Net Group*, Vladikavkaz Math. J. 21(3) (2019), 24–30, was downloaded
from the journal's full-issue PDF:
https://www.vmj.ru/upload/uf/b5e/21_3_.pdf .
All seven article pages were read as extracted text, and printed p.28
was inspected as an image. The other articles in the issue were not read.
The PDF download has 2,263,952 bytes and SHA256
e79fe6d508f11143c27f4434e230ec27fa0cdac43d674612cf1fcb3607a42728.

The paper refines a decomposition of an elementary transvection into
a factor from its original rank-one elementary subgroup and a second
rank-one matrix whose off-diagonal entries lie in derived-net levels.
It also bounds the diagonal entries and several products. It presents
this as progress toward 15.46, not a complete answer. Several net-level
inclusions and the ambient entry bound are imported from earlier papers
and conference proceedings. Those dependencies have not been separately
audited here, so no new conclusion is based on this source theorem.

The new omitted-pair lemma in `15.46-omitted-pair-lemma.md` has its own
proof from the completed 19.61 and 19.62 packets. It identifies the
root intersections of the complementary normal subgroup. The remaining
full rank-one intersection is precisely the distinction that prevents
the current argument from resolving 15.46.
