# Problem 21.115: prior general resolution located

On 10 September 2026, after developing the special-case arguments and a
finite search model, a broader literature check located Benjamin Sambale,
*On the complement of a union of cosets*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09052v1 , submitted 8 September 2026 at
17:09:50 UTC. Read all six PDF pages and checked the finite-group proof.

Theorem 1 covers mixed left and right cosets and implies the exact bound
in Notebook 21.115. The main lemma bounds a minimal family of certain
unions with empty intersection by the dimension of a multilinear
polynomial space. Applied to translates of the union, it bounds the
number of translates of its complement needed to cover the group.
Minimality supplies evaluation witnesses proving polynomial independence.
For finite groups there is no compactness issue: all translates already
have empty intersection. This verifies the required finite conclusion.

The source also treats infinite groups and equality. These stronger claims
are unnecessary for removing 21.115 from our active targets. Our abelian
Fourier proof and class-two linearization are retained only as special-case
rediscoveries. The solver model passed 72 independent finite-set controls;
no group search was launched. This contributes zero new resolutions.
