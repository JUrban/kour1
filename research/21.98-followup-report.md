word in every locally finite-by-nilpotent group: finite cyclic coverage
of its values forces a finite-by-cyclic verbal subgroup. In a
torsion-free locally nilpotent group the verbal subgroup is cyclic.

assumption, but does not apply to an arbitrary group merely because its
verbal subgroup is central or abelian. Partial priority is unconfirmed.
