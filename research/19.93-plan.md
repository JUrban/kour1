# Problem 19.93: triangle presentations and lower p-central width

12 September 2026. Initial plan, before computational checks.

The printed hypothesis excludes epimorphic images of the whole group
isomorphic to C_p wr C_p. It does not exclude such sections in subgroups.
The original PDF page 143 has been rendered and visually checked.

Investigate the two-generator presentation x^p=y^p=(xy)^p=1, p=7.
In the regular wreath product, an element (v,t^a) with a nonzero has
p-th power equal to the constant vector with value sum(v). Consequently
two order-p generators must project onto different coordinate axes of
the quotient C_p^2; their product has order p^2. This excludes a
surjection from the triangle presentation.

The complete algebra on X,Y with relations X^p, Y^p,
(X+Y+XY)^p has three relations of initial degree p. The filtered
Golod--Shafarevich inequality at t=2/3 gives exponential growth for p>=7.
Its finite truncations yield finite p-groups generated by 1+X,1+Y.

To force unbounded lower p-central factors, prove directly that a
uniform bound K on their dimensions bounds all augmentation quotients
by the coefficients of the K-coloured partition product
product_i (1+t^i+...+t^((p-1)i))^K. Ordered normal forms through the
lower p-central series give a spanning set, with each weight-i
generator difference lying in augmentation degree at least i. The
partition product converges inside the unit disk, contradicting GS.

Audit points: distinguish filtered from graded GS, prove the finite
algebra-to-group span map, check exactly two generators, prove all
normal-form and augmentation claims, and retain only the literal
whole-group quotient conclusion. Test the wreath obstruction on
complete small groups; compute bounded GAP p-quotients as controls;
check low-degree truncated algebra dimensions by exact linear algebra.
No finite computation will stand in for the unboundedness proof.
