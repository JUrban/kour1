# Problem 15.76(b): internal review

12 September 2026. Review by the same working agent; no independent
review or external acceptance is claimed.

1. **Exact scope.** The theorem uses S_d, the entire variety of groups
   with d-th derived subgroup trivial. This admits the affine extensions
   used in the induction. Proper subvarieties need not admit them.
2. **Kernel.** For F->F/N, the affine map records the endpoint and the
   integral edge chain of a word in the covering graph. Among loops N,
   zero chain is precisely N'. No torsion-free hypothesis on F/N and no
   finiteness of the cover is required.
3. **Coefficient order.** Left module action gives f(ab,c)f(a,b),
   not its reverse. Both exterior coefficient equations and the Fox
   boundary equation are written in ordered group-ring products.
4. **Testing all modules is allowed.** M=Z[Q]^3 is abelian as a group;
   its affine extension by a group of derived length d-1 has derived
   length at most d. Three independent module coordinates can therefore
   be compared. Finite generation of that extension is unnecessary.
5. **Induction.** The normalization identities survive passage from
   S_d to S_(d-1). Taking the opposite word preserves associativity
   and makes the projected operation xy. This is done only when needed.
6. **Specializations.** Sending the scalar a to 1 is a homomorphism of
   the coefficient group ring after the coefficient identities have
   been established. It does not impose a vanishing module coordinate.
7. **Units.** The inverse specialization takes place in Z[t,t^-1].
   Its cyclic generator has infinite order. The exponent sum gives
   h(1)=q(1)=1, and extreme Laurent exponents prove that each unit is a
   positive monomial. No assertion about general Z[Q] units is made.
8. **Two integer pairs.** Formal differentiation is valid for negative
   Laurent powers. Y^k=l+(1-l)Y forces k=0 or 1, then respectively
   l=1 or 0. The full boundary identity excludes the second case when
   Q is nonabelian.
9. **Returning from coefficients.** N=F^(d-1) gives N'=F^d. Thus
   equality of scalar and module coordinates proves equality in the
   required free S_d group. This is stronger than checking a quotient.
10. **Categorical rank.** End(F_1) is infinite commutative, End(F_0)
    trivial, and End(F_n) noncommutative for n>=2. Coproducts of F_1
    then identify all finite ranks.
11. **Basis normalization.** The image coproduct maps actually give
    a basis, by their universal property. Conjugation by its change of
    basis fixes the chosen inclusions, including the identity at F_1.
12. **Elements and word.** Hom(F_1,A) is the underlying set of A.
    Full faithfulness gives element bijections and functoriality gives
    conjugation of every morphism. The value of xy in F_2 is a single
    word defining the transported product on every object.
13. **Identities.** Surjectivity of those bijections transfers
    associativity and identity to the word operation on free objects.
    A word identity in the free group on its variables holds in every
    group of the variety.
14. **Opposite case.** Word reversal fixing a basis is inversion as a
    set map composed with the group automorphism inverting the basis.
    Inversion commutes naturally with every homomorphism. The induced
    functor is therefore conjugation by ordinary group automorphisms.
15. **All named objects.** The proof gives the explicit natural maps
    t_A=r_A^-1 v_A eta_A, including rank zero, and checks their formula
    on all morphisms. It does not rely on a skeletal-category shortcut.
16. **Evidence limits.** Computation checks all normalized reduced
    words through length ten, coefficient formulas over S3 and S4,
    and selected perturbations in deeper derived subgroups. These are
    controls for signs, order and implementation, not exhaustive tests
    of the theorem. The argument above is the general proof.

No mathematical gap was identified in this review. The greatest
remaining risks are independent proof scrutiny and missing literature.
The category method is explicitly credited; new priority is not asserted.
