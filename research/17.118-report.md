# 17.118: partial results and finite validation

12 September 2026. The proof gives optimal universal index bounds 4
for p=2 and 27 for p=3. For every prime p, an explicit finite algebra
group requires characteristic exponent-p subgroups to have index at
least p^p, with equality attained. If the given index-p subgroup has
nilpotency class c, a characteristic exponent-p subgroup of index at
most p^(c+1) exists; this bound is sharp at c=p-1. A bound depending
only on p for general p>=5 remains unproved.

Start with `research/17.118-partial-proof.md`. The deletion lemma
constructs its endomorphisms on a universal nilpotent quotient, avoiding
an unjustified deletion map on the original group. Its argument works
on the integral lower central graded Lie ring, in every characteristic.
The lower-bound example is completely explicit and its characteristic
subgroup is defined intrinsically by preservation of p-th powers under
translation. The sole external theorem needed in the resulting proofs
is Laffey's 7/9 root bound for the p=3 upper bound. See
`research/17.118-source-audit.md` for exact reading and dependency limits.

The finite validation has three implementations.

- The initial GAP p-quotient has order 3^8, class 3, exponent 9 and
  lower central subgroup orders 6561,243,9,1. Its three coordinate
  hyperplanes have order 2187 and exponent 3, and cycling the three
  generators gives an automorphism. Its initial presentation is not
  claimed to define a finite group; only the computed quotient is used.
- Independent Python arithmetic constructs the squarefree-word algebra,
  checks the p-th power formula for all 3,156 linear vectors over
  p=2,3,5 with deterministic higher-degree perturbations, and checks
  1,800 arbitrary sparse associativity triples. At p=3 it enumerates
  all 6,561 elements of the subgroup generated by 1+x_0,1+x_1,1+x_2,
  saving coefficients and a generator-word witness for each element.
- A separate validator uses coefficient convolution over cuts of words,
  importing no arithmetic from the producer. It checks all 6,561 word
  witnesses, all 19,683 generator edges, every cube, and every cyclic
  image. It rejects five deliberate corruptions of the basis,
  coefficients, uniqueness, word witnesses and reported root count.
  Native GAP then constructs a faithful 16-dimensional matrix model
  directly, reproduces the structural invariants and verifies an
  isomorphism with the initial p-quotient.
