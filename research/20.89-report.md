## Results

`20.89-triangular-proof.md` characterizes the almost Engel elements of
an arbitrary triangularizable group by kernels of diagonal characters
on its infinite additive layers. Their subgroup is finite-by-nilpotent
and has a common finite ambient sink with a uniform entry bound.

`20.89-residual-reduction.md` uses Malcev and Gruenberg to remove the
virtual-solubility hypothesis for the subgroup generated by finitely
many almost Engel elements of a linear group. Shumyatsky's Theorem 3.3
then makes that subgroup finite-by-nilpotent. Applying this to finitely
many conjugates containing one element's minimal sink proves that the
sink generates a finite subgroup. Thus the question is also affirmative
for every torsion-free linear group, without a generation assumption.

The remaining obstacle is one finite sink for a product as the input
ranges over the entire arbitrary linear ambient group. A finite sink
subgroup need not be normal in that ambient group. The explicit
order72 example in the proof prevents silently assuming normality.
