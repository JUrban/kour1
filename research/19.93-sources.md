# Problem 19.93: sources and reading scope

Checked 12 September 2026.

- **The Kourovka Notebook, 21st edition**, local `docs/21tkt.pdf`,
  page 143, Problem 19.93, P. Spiga. The indexed text and rendered page
  `references/cache/19.93-notebook.png` were read and viewed. The condition
  is absence of epimorphic images isomorphic to C_p wr C_p; it is not
  section exclusion. The entry has no resolution star in this copy.
  The online edition https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf agrees in the retrieved text.
- **Mikhail Ershov, Golod--Shafarevich groups: a survey**, arXiv:1206.0490v1,
  submitted 3 June 2012; Internat. J. Algebra Comput.22(2012), no.5,
  1230001, DOI https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218196712300010.
  Primary PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.0490 cached as
  `references/cache/ershov-2012-golod-shafarevich.pdf`, with layout text.
  For this packet, Section 2.3 on pp.9--10 was read fully, including
  Theorem 2.6 and Proposition 2.7(a). Both page images were viewed.
  The graded proof on p.6 was also read in text; the displayed p.9
  Golod group construction and p.10 Section 3.1 were viewed/read.
  The entire 54-page paper has not been read. The filtered inequality
  is explicitly imported. The source credits its general form to
  Vinberg; Vinberg's original paper was not independently inspected.
- **Aner Shalev, Characterization of p-adic analytic groups in terms of
  wreath products**, J. Algebra145(1992),204--208,
  DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/0021-8693(92)90185-O. The primary publisher
  abstract was retrieved in search; a direct open returned403. It
  describes excluding arbitrarily large wreath products as sections.
  No full text or proof was read, and no theorem from this paper is
  used in the candidate. This distinguishes the notebook's comparison
  from the whole-group quotient hypothesis actually asked.
- **GAP4.16.1**, local `lib/pquot.gd`, PQuotient and EpimorphismPGroup
  documentation inspected for finite controls. The computations use
  the existing local `bin/gap` wrapper. GAP quotients are numerical
  corroboration only; their construction is not imported into the proof.

Searches combined the exact problem number/name, epimorphic images,
lower p-central series, triangle groups, Golod--Shafarevich, finite
width, and wreath products. They found related triangle/Beauville
and pro-p growth literature but no exact prior negative answer.
Only the sources above support the proof or its stated context.
The wreath obstruction and elementary normal-form growth bound are
proved in the packet. The standard GS input is credited; priority
of their application here remains unconfirmed.
