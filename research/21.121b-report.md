## Mathematical result

Start with [the soluble-group proof](21.121b-soluble-proof.md). A uniform
constant J for normal abelian subgroups of finite p'-subgroups gives
every finite soluble subgroup G a characteristic abelian p'-subgroup
of index at most

    J^3 |G_(p)|^2.

If finite subgroups have soluble subgroups of uniformly bounded index
d, a normal abelian p'-subgroup has index at most d! J^3 |G_(p)|^2.
In particular every virtually soluble p-Jordan group has exponent at
most two, with no fixed derived-length or linearity assumption.

The [normal-Sylow proof](21.121b-normal-sylow-proof.md) gives the sharper
bound J |P| (|P/Phi(P)|-1) when P is nontrivial and normal. It also
proves sharp exponent two for AGL_1(overline(F_p)), for every prime p.
The constant two is attained in that family. This is distinct from
the older nonattainment example for 21.121(a).

The [source and internal scope audit](21.121b-source-audit.md) credits
Schur–Zassenhaus, the Fitting theorem, and the Chermak–Delgado method.
The characteristic abelian subgroup is constructed inside the
p'-radical, so its order remains prime to p. The unrestricted problem
allows unbounded indices of solubility and is not resolved here.

## Controls and their limits

The first GAP control checks 11 groups, 251 subgroup conjugacy
classes representing 1,183 actual subgroups, and 1,286 choices of
normal abelian subgroups in Hall complements. Besides affine groups,
it includes a quaternion Sylow subgroup, a nontrivial centralizer in
the complement, and a nonabelian Hall complement.

The independent integer program constructs fields of orders
2,3,4,5,7,8,9,16, checks 5,904 field triples, and enumerates every
subgroup of their affine groups. All 269 subgroup counts and histograms
of order, Sylow order, and minimum normal abelian p'-index agree with
GAP. The largest field in this control is F_16; no larger exhaustive
search is implied.

The additional soluble-group GAP program checks 10 positive models,
including both p=2 and p=3 for GL_2(3), and S4 times the exponent-three
Heisenberg group of order 27. The independent permutation/quaternion/
integer models check seven positive cases and A5 as a negative control,
with 429,192 associativity triples. On those eight shared cases all
reported Jordan constants, radical orders, characteristic subgroup
orders, quotient orders, Frattini indices, and constructed indices
agree. The two GL_2(3) cases and the order-648 product have native
controls only; they are not described as independently reconstructed.

For A5 at p=2, all odd-order subgroups are abelian, so the weak Jordan
constant is 1; the minimum normal abelian odd-order index is 60 while
|A5_(2)|^2=16. Its Fitting subgroup is trivial. This rejects the
stronger false quadratic assertion without the soluble hypothesis;
it is not a counterexample to exponent three or to p-Jordanity.

The [cross-check output](../results/21.121b-normal-sylow-audit.json)
compares the two implementations and verifies the source and process
digests. It does not turn bounded controls into a proof for arbitrary
groups. The written arguments give that part of the result.
