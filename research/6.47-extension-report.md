## Method and completed coverage

The extension uses the unchanged binary word-function exporter from
the initial pilot. It enumerates the normalized coset xy F' in the
group F of two-variable word functions, represented on automorphism
orbits of ordered pairs. Unit laws force both exponent sums to be one
modulo exp(G), so every binary word defining a group operation occurs
in this coset. See the frozen pilot report for the justification.

These comprise 622 normalized operations and 18,926,930 associativity
triples. The replay verifies all 18,610 transporters, multiplication
tables, unit and inverse laws, and powers. Among the 88 associative
operations, 86 admit explicit isomorphisms to the original group of
the form x -> x^k with gcd(k,exp(G))=1. Each proposed map is checked
on every ordered pair in its full multiplication table.

The remaining two operations, one for each order-27 group, are
abelian and have the same exponent as G. Any finite abelian group
of exponent dividing exp(G) belongs to var(G): choose cyclic subgroups
realizing each prime-power factor of exp(G), take their direct product
to obtain a cyclic group of that exponent in var(G), and use the
decomposition of finite abelian groups into cyclic factors. Thus
all 88 outputs are certified to belong to the required variety.

The order-27 groups have class two. The other completed cases have
class three or four; SmallGroup(32,18), (32,19), and (32,20) have
class four. The retained GAP class catalogue checks these labels.
This catalogue is a selection aid, not an associativity search.

depend on GAP's enumeration of the word-function group, while the

in Python. This is not a second independent enumeration of F.

The general problem remains unresolved here. Cooper's 1974 class-three

report; no novelty claim is made for these bounded cases. They
support the class-two proof and provide no counterexample.
