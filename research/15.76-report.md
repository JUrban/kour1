# Problem 15.76(b): completion report

12 September 2026. Complete affirmative candidate for every full
variety S_d, d>=1, including the full metabelian variety. This is the
38th complete candidate in the research run, pending independent
review and further priority checking.

The proof in `15.76-proof.md` first establishes a stronger statement:
every associative binary group word with identity 1 in S_d is xy or
yx. A Magnus affine model turns associativity into ordered coefficient
equations. Specializing to one infinite cyclic variable forces two
Laurent units, and a boundary identity leaves two integer pairs.
Induction on derived length completes the word classification.

An explicit categorical reduction recognizes rank one, normalizes
coproduct basis maps, and transports elements through Hom(F_1,A).
The opposite product also gives an inner functor. The natural maps are
written for every named object, so the proof covers the printed category.
The 16-point internal review identified no gap; it is not outside review.
