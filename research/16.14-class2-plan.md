# 16.14: remove the exponent bound in nilpotency class two

September 12, 2026. The preceding goal turn completed 11.115. This turn
revisited several free-group and graph-group leads, then found a stronger
version of the existing exponent-four partial proof of 16.14.

For a finite 2-group G of class at most two, put Z=Z(G), Phi=Phi(G),
V=(G/Z)[2], and S=(Z intersection Phi)/Z^2. Squaring should induce an
anisotropic quadratic map V->S whenever all involutions of G are central.
Its zero criterion uses the ability to extract a square root in Z from
an element of Z^2; it does not require exponent four. The resulting
dimension bound, together with the exact Frattini sequence, should give

    d(G) + dim_F2 Z/(Z intersection Phi) <= 2 rank(Z).

   term in the generator-count formula.
2. Include an explicit elementary parity proof and sharp families.
3. Test actual finite groups, including exponent eight, commutators of
   order four, central direct factors, and failures of the hypothesis.
4. Reconstruct the square map independently from exported multiplication
   tables; use a bounded exhaustive quadratic-form check as a control.
5. Preserve a separate packet and distinguish the elementary proof
   from the full prior solution. A ledger reconciliation found that
   `16.14-prior-result.md` already gives the original inequality in all
