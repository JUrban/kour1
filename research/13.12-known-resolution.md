# 13.12 is already answered by Carette's theorem

Status: exact prior affirmative result; excluded from new-priority counts.
The supplied Notebook page67 was visually checked. It asks whether the
automorphism group of every hyperbolic group is finitely presented.

Mathieu Carette,
[*The automorphism group of accessible groups*](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.0043v3),
Theorem6.3, gives exactly this conclusion, including groups with torsion
and with more than one end. The preprint first appeared30September2008;
the checked version3 is dated8February2010. Publication:
[J. London Math. Soc.84(3) (2011),731--748](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/jdr029).

The introduction, Theorem1.1, Lemmas6.1--6.2 and the complete proof of
Theorem6.3 were read. The proof reduces via accessibility to a finite
graph of groups with finite edge groups and at-most-one-ended hyperbolic
vertex groups, then applies the finite-presentation criterion of
Theorem1.1. It handles normalizers of finite edge groups and relative
automorphism groups explicitly. Thus the earlier one-ended torsion-free
result mentioned by the Notebook is not the full state of prior work.

The structural proof of Theorem1.1 and the earlier results it invokes
were not independently audited. The paper notes that version3 corrected
Section4; no argument is imported from an earlier version instead.
PDF/text are cached as `references/cache/carette-accessible-2008.*`.
The initial2008 conference abstract was only a discovery lead; the
status above rests on the exact published theorem and its checked scope.
