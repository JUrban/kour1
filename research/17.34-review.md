# Internal audit of the candidate answer to 17.34

Recorded 2026-09-11. This is an internal audit, not outside review.

## Statement and quantifiers

The supplied PDF page 108 was visually checked. N_c is the full
quasivariety of torsion-free nilpotent groups of class at most c.
The subgroup is divisible, with no finite generation or rank bound.
The target of the separating maps must still have class at most c.
The proof handles all these quantifiers at once. The entry is unstarred.

The issue is trivial dominion, which is stronger than merely excluding
proper epimorphic subgroups. A theorem that all epimorphisms are onto
in a related category would not, by itself, establish what is needed.

## Principal attempts to falsify the construction

1. **Wrong side of the ideal.** J=U h is a left ideal. M is a left module.
   The PBW basis puts h-factors last, so J has exactly the asserted basis.
   A two-sided ideal would instead kill the ideal of L generated by h,
   which is too large for nonideal h.
2. **Truncation killing an extra element of L.** A basis simultaneously
   adapted to h and the ambient lower central filtration makes J and
   I^(c+1) spans of subsets of a common PBW basis. Thus their sum cannot
   cancel a surviving complementary degree-one vector. The weights here
   are ambient filtration weights, not weights internal to h.
3. **Nonhomogeneous Lie algebras or subalgebras.** No grading is assumed.
   Reordering can increase weight, but it cannot decrease it; that is
   sufficient for the augmentation-filtration statement.
4. **Class c+1 instead of c.** The numerator is I, not U. Consequently
   I^c M=0. The explicit lower-central-series induction gives class <=c.
5. **Lie maps without group maps.** BCH is a finite universal Lie
   polynomial at fixed class and carries both maps to homomorphisms.
6. **Divisible subgroup not a Lie subalgebra.** Rational roots in H are
   the ambient roots by uniqueness. Inverse BCH expresses addition and
   bracket by group operations and rational powers, so log H is closed
   under the required operations. No normality is needed.
7. **Infinite rank.** The PBW construction uses a basis but never a
   finite-dimensionality hypothesis. All individual monomials and BCH
   expressions are finite. Malcev completion exists for arbitrary
   torsion-free nilpotent groups and keeps the given class bound.
8. **Additional identities.** The semidirect product is verified to lie
   in full N_c; membership in an arbitrary smaller quasivariety is not
   asserted. Budkin's stronger settings remain distinct.

The first-coordinate projections make both homomorphisms injective.
Hence the proof gives an exact equalizer, not only separation by maps
whose kernels might obscure the subgroup. H=1, H=G, and c=1 are covered.

## Exact finite controls

Command:

    python3 scripts/verify_17_34.py

Result: `results/17.34-verification.json`, all 13 cases PASS.

The verifier builds the truncated tensor algebra on an arbitrary original
basis, using all words of lengths 1 through c. It imposes every surviving
enveloping relation u(x_i x_j-x_j x_i-[x_i,x_j])v and the left-ideal
relations u h. Exact rational elimination constructs the quotient without
assuming the PBW basis or its weighted-filtration formula.

It checks the Jacobi identity of the input, that h is a subalgebra,
invariance of the presented denominator under every left multiplication,
the module representation relations, the cocycle identity, and the exact
kernel dimension. It then computes the target's lower central series
directly from all its brackets, independently of the class-bound formula.
For class <=3 it also checks 4,000 rational BCH homomorphism identities
for the two maps.

Cases include a noncoordinate abelian subspace; trivial, full, central,
ideal, nonideal and nonhomogeneous Heisenberg subalgebras; four subalgebras
of the free two-generator class-three Lie algebra, including a nonabelian
one; and two class-four examples, one with a filtration-increasing
deformation of the bracket. The largest presentation has 780 tensor basis
vectors and processes 6,172 relations. The two class-four targets have
lower central dimensions 15,12,9,5,0.

These are finite sanity checks. The general conclusion rests on the
written argument, including PBW and Malcev/BCH, not these examples.

## Literature scope and credit

- Shakhova (2015), [Math-Net record and primary paper](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/mzm10471),
  DOI 10.1134/S0001434615050302 (English); Russian DOI 10.4213/mzm10471.
  Read the introduction, preliminaries, Theorem 1 and proof, and following
  Corollary 1 on Russian pages 936--939. The c<=2 result is already known,
  and page 937 explicitly connects it to 17.34. Her necessary condition
  that absolutely closed groups be divisible holds for arbitrary c.
- Budkin (2018), [primary record](https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/smj3008),
  DOI 10.1134/S0037446618040031. Read the introduction, notation and
  preliminary lemmas, and final theorem. It proves two-closedness of Q
  in every torsion-free nilpotent subquasivariety of class <=3; the whole
  technical proof has not been independently audited.
- Bergman, [*Epimorphisms of Lie algebras*](https://math.berkeley.edu/~gbergman/papers/unpub/LieEpi.pdf),
  manuscript dated at most 1973. Visually read pages 1--6, including
  Theorem 2.1 and its PBW/enveloping argument for all Lie algebras.
  That result does not itself supply the fixed nilpotency class here.
- Reid, [*Epimorphisms and surjectivity*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01425484),
  Invent. Math. 9 (1970), 295--307, was identified as related prior work.
  Its full text has not been retrieved: GDZ timed out and the publisher
  endpoint returned the subscription preview. No assertion from its
  unread proof is used as evidence of a new result or imported theorem.
- Magidin's [author summary](https://userweb.ucs.louisiana.edu/~avm1260/research/higher.html)
  describes nontrivial dominions in fixed nilpotency varieties that permit
  torsion targets, and triviality for finitely generated ambient groups
  when the allowed nilpotency class is unbounded. Those formulations are
  different. The full papers remain a possible source for further audit.
- Bahturin--Olshanskii, [arXiv:2208.09527v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.09527v1),
  Theorem 2.8 and Lemma 3.1, and Suciu,
  [DOI 10.4171/EMSS/74](https://doi.org/10.4171/EMSS/74), Section 6.2,
  were read for the imported completion/correspondence facts. The
  inverse-operations discussion explicitly supports the subgroup step.

Targeted searches included the exact problem number and author, English
and Russian dominion/divisible-subgroup terms, nilpotent Lie dominions,
augmentation modules, and isolators. No prior general-c resolution was
located. This limited audit does not establish novelty. In particular,
older Lie or nilpotent amalgamation results could imply the conclusion.
The proof is retained as a complete candidate, with the known class-two
subcase explicitly excluded from its proposed new content.
