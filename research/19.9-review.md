## Statement and imported facts

The supplied PDF page133 was visually checked: finite generation is
required in(a), and(b) asks for all n>3. It does not impose a bound on
representation degree or a prescribed characteristic. The constructions
answer exactly these statements: Sp(10,Z) for(a), all n>=4 for(b).

The essential imported facts are the tensor/exterior/Hopf descriptions,
Deligne's theorem and the identification of the stable symplectic
universal central extension, H_2(B_n)=C_2 for n>=4, and braid-group
linearity. Each exact statement was checked in the sources listed in
the proof. We do not claim to have re-proved or fully audited those
major theorems. GAP cannot verify the infinite assertions here.

## Part(a) audit

1. Sp(10,Z) is already a subgroup of GL_10(Q). Elementary symplectic
   generators give finite generation. The stable H_1 table gives
   perfectness. Rank5 lies safely in every stated stable range.
2. Perfectness gives Gamma(G_ab)=0, so tensor and exterior squares
   coincide. The free-presentation argument proves that the exterior
   square is the universal central extension, including its perfectness.
3. The topological pullback is this universal extension in the selected
   rank. Rank3 would require addressing an extra C_2 summand and was
   deliberately avoided.
4. Deligne kills z^2 in every finite quotient, where z generates the
   infinite cyclic covering kernel. The assertion concerns that kernel;
   some discussions use 'centre' loosely despite the lift of -I.
5. Lifts of generators generate the perfect extension because E=HC
   and E'=H'. Thus Malcev's theorem applies. This finite-generation
   step is essential: arbitrary linear groups need not be residually
   finite.
6. The proof establishes an isomorphism, not just a quotient map from
   the tensor square onto a non-residually-finite group. Non-residual
   finiteness of a quotient would not suffice.

## Part(b) audit

1. The central diagonal subgroup is the image of Gamma(Z). Its generator
   d=s_1 tensor s_1 has epsilon(d)=1, proving both infinite order and
   the direct-factor decomposition. No unjustified split extension is
   used. In particular no hypothesis on H_3(B_n) is needed.
2. The Clifford algebra has rational structure constants and dimension
   2^n. Its exterior-algebra realization proves the asserted basis is
   independent. The scalar -1 is distinct from1.
3. The units u_i square to1. Adjacent braid relations hold exactly;
   distant commutators equal -1. Therefore every element of the normal
   relation subgroup R maps into the central subgroup {1,-1}, including
   arbitrary conjugates, and [F,R] is killed.
4. The class of [x_1,x_3] is in the Hopf kernel and is detected. It is
   nonzero and generates that kernel because H_2(B_n)=C_2. This covers
   n=4 too; no perfectness of B_n' is assumed.
5. The pair of representations is faithful on W(B_n): its first
   coordinate reduces the kernel to H_2(B_n), its second detects it.
   The diagonal factor is then detected by2^epsilon. The infinite
   cyclic scalar representation is over characteristic zero.
6. All three blocks use the common field Q(q,t). Left multiplication
   is injective on the Clifford unit group and the braid representation
   is faithful. The resulting dimension1+n(n-1)/2+2^n is a bound,
   not a minimality claim.

The conceptual finite-cover proof is compatible with [AKS], but the
explicit Clifford argument only needs its H_2(B_n) statement. We do
not use the false general implication that every finite central
extension of a linear group is linear.
