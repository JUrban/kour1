# Problem 21.123: prior claim and formulation scope

Checked 11 September 2026. No new resolution claimed here.

The exact referenced journal paper is Agata Smoktunowicz, *An extension
of the group of flows method for finite pre-Lie rings*, Journal of Pure
and Applied Algebra 229(12) (2025), 108128,
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpaa.2025.108128.
Its institutional record identifies an accepted manuscript, but both
browser and direct retrieval of that PDF returned 403:
https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/an-extension-of-the-group-of-flows-method-for-finite-pre-lie-ring/ .

The associated preprint is arXiv:2410.20440v2, dated 29 March 2025,
under the earlier title *On some connections between braces and pre-Lie
rings outside of the context of Lazard's correspondence*:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.20440v2 . Its PDF/text are cached locally.
Definitions 59, 60, 64, 66, Remarks 61--63, and Theorem 65 with its proof
were read. The source defines the candidate operation using a chosen
section of multiplication by p and a modified exponential orbit map.
There are visible indexing and iteration issues in the preprint
displays. We have not compared them against the published final PDF.

A prior preprint by Natanael Wildner Fraga, posted 28 August 2026,
*Protected p-Division and the Extended Agrachev--Gamkrelidze
Construction for Finite Pre-Lie Rings*, directly addresses 21.123:
https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.2109 . Its abstract,
introduction, main theorem, and beginning of the protected-division
section were read. It distinguishes failure of a typographically
literal prescription from an affirmative theorem for an explicitly
corrected and totalized prescription. Its advertised proof requires
closure of a protected BCH series in the affine pre-Lie image.

The main BCH/affine closure proof has not been audited here. Accordingly
this is recorded as a relevant prior claim with formulation caveats,
not as an independently verified resolution of every interpretation of
the Notebook's published reference. No finite search was launched and
no new-candidate count is attached to 21.123.
