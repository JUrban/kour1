# 17.100: exact character-table screen

Status: bounded evidence, not a solution of the general conjecture.

The question asserts that a finite simple group cannot have an
odd-degree irreducible complex character vanishing on an odd-size
conjugacy class. The search reads every ordinary table in CTblLib,
selects the simple tables, and tests every such row/column pair using
exact cyclotomic arithmetic.

`scripts/search_17_100.g`, run with `bin/gap`, completed with:

- 2,750 ordinary tables considered, none unavailable;
- 215 simple-table entries;
- 12,606 odd-degree/odd-class pairs;
- zero vanishing pairs.

These are library entries, not pairwise nonisomorphic groups. Alternate
tables for the same group occur. The full retained output is
`results/17.100-tables.log`.

## The elementary part of the screen

Every complex character of odd degree is nonzero on every 2-element.
Indeed, for an element of order 2^a, a>=1, its eigenvalues are powers of
a primitive 2^a-th root zeta. Modulo the ideal (1-zeta) of Z[zeta], its
trace is congruent to its odd degree, hence to 1 in F_2. The identity
element is immediate. This argument does not require irreducibility.

Consequently the conjecture holds for every group whose odd-size
conjugacy classes consist of 2-elements. In particular it holds if
the centralizer of a Sylow 2-subgroup is a 2-group: an element with
odd-size class centralizes a Sylow 2-subgroup.

Only four simple-table entries in this finite screen have odd-size
classes of elements which are not 2-elements:

| Entry | Odd-degree rows | Such columns | Pairs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| C3 | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| Alt(3) | 3 | 2 | 6 |
| L3(11) | 20 | 8 | 160 |
| U3(9) | 20 | 8 | 160 |

Thus 332 tests fall outside the elementary 2-element argument; 320 of
these belong to nonabelian simple groups. This distribution is a
limitation of the library sample, not a classification of all simple
groups. In particular it would be wrong to infer that all odd-size
classes in all nonabelian simple groups consist of 2-elements.

## Verification and literature scope

`scripts/verify_17_100.g` checks simplicity using a separate criterion:
every nonprincipal irreducible must be faithful, and the group must be
nontrivial. This is equivalent to simplicity, since any proper
nontrivial normal subgroup has a nonprincipal irreducible inflated
from its quotient. The checker also computes character tables directly
from eight actual simple groups, rather than requesting named library
tables. All 2,750 simplicity comparisons and all 72 relevant
row/column pairs from the eight actual groups passed. Results and
the final PASS marker are in `results/17.100-controls.log`.

Limited exact-question and odd-degree/Sylow-centralizer searches did
not locate a full resolution. Malle--Späth,
[*Characters of odd degree*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.07690), was located
as relevant background; only its primary abstract and the search
excerpt containing Lemma7.5/Theorem7.7 were inspected. Its general
character classification and proof have not been audited or imported
to claim a solution. The elementary observation above is not claimed
as new.
