# 20.86: Frobenius-ratio source scope

11 September 2026. No general resolution or new candidate.

Knapp–Schmid, *Sylow intersections and Frobenius ratios*,
Arch. Math. 123 (2024), 9–17,
[primary PDF](https://d-nb.info/1342125495/34),
[DOI 10.1007/s00013-024-01995-x](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-024-01995-x),
was read in full. Write s=1+kp for the Sylow count and f=1+ell(p-1)
for the number of p-elements divided by the Sylow order.

Theorem 1 proves ell>=k for strongly irredundant Sylows. The paper
also studies the weaker power inequality f^p>=s^(p-1), crediting
Gheri's p-soluble result and almost-simple reduction. Neither that
inequality nor an almost-simple reduction for it automatically
establishes the Notebook's stronger ell^p>=k^(p-1).

The elementary-abelian-complement family on pages 13–14 gives
redundant Sylows and can have ell<k. That alone is not a
counterexample to the requested smaller power of k. The paper's
S5 example has p=2, k=7, ell=6 and satisfies ell^2>=k.

The normalizer index is not provided by a character table's
p-element counts alone; the paper explicitly raises recoverability
of the Sylow count from the table as an open question. No table-only
search was launched with an assumed normalizer order. The cited
classical and Gheri dependencies were not fully audited.
