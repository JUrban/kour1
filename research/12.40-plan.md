# Problem 12.40: bounded Brauer-degree search

11 September 2026. Exploratory search; no general bound claimed.

The problem asks for the best bound on the p-part of an irreducible
p-Brauer character degree in terms of |G|_p. First test the natural
candidate phi(1)_p <= |G|_p against every available installed Brauer
table. Retain the complete ordinary-table name list, each prime tested,
all available degrees, and explicit unavailable-table rows. A table
excess would be a lead for a theoretical family, not a general answer.

Use one GAP process with the existing 4-GiB ceiling beside the six
n=7 verification workers. Preserve its actual exit, clean log and
completion sentinel. Independently recompute every p-part in Python.
Do not infer absence of examples outside the available table library.

Liu--Willems--Zhang, Problems on Brauer Characters, Section 6,
Proposition 6.1, recalls that all Brauer characters in cyclic-defect
blocks have height zero. Thus a cyclic-Sylow family cannot provide
the contemplated counterexample. Only the relevant statements and
introductory notation have been read at this stage:
https://web.math.ovgu.de/willems/papers/IJGT-7.pdf

## Revised direction: a fixed Sylow order

The table screen found degrees with p-parts exceeding the group-order
p-part. These are a motivation only. The proposed full obstruction is
G=PSL_6(5^a), a odd, with its characteristic-two projective permutation
heart of degree F(q)=q^5+q^4+q^3+q^2+q-1. Bray's 2007 Main Theorem
confirms absolute irreducibility; its hypotheses and the relevant proof
were read, with pages 2 and 3 checked visually. The Notebook statement
was checked on page 60, not the initially rendered next page.

1. Prove |G|_2=2^13 for every positive odd a.
2. Starting at a=1 modulo 2, lift a modulo successive powers of two
   so that F(5^a) is divisible by 2^k. Also produce exact valuation k.
3. Check the polynomial lift independently using roots modulo 2^k;
   compare exact integer degrees and group orders for moderate exponents.
4. After the table screen finishes, use one bounded GAP worker for
   small actual projective permutation modules. Computational checks
   support conventions; the infinite theorem rests on the proof.
5. Audit the source, quantifiers and preserved process exits; freeze
   all evidence with an explicit file manifest and local commit.

No finite bound can exist if these steps hold. Limited searches have
not located this precise consequence, but priority remains unverified.
The classical representation construction is credited to prior work.
