# 21.121(b): normal Sylow p-subgroups

12 September 2026. This is a partial result, not an answer to the
unrestricted question or a new complete-candidate count.

Prove that a uniform Jordan constant J for finite p'-subgroups yields
the bound J|P|(|P/Phi(P)|-1), for every finite subgroup with nontrivial
normal Sylow p-subgroup P. In particular exponent two suffices.

The proof will keep the chosen abelian subgroup normal in the Hall
complement, so that its centralizer of P is normal in the full group.
It will prove the coprime automorphism bound through the Frattini
quotient, including the kernel argument. The only substantial imported
finite-group theorem is existence of a Schur–Zassenhaus complement.

The family AGL_1(F_(p^n)) should establish optimal exponent two. Check
all subgroups for eight small fields independently in GAP and integer
arithmetic, and use additional nonabelian p-groups and complements to
check the Frattini factor and normality in the proposed construction.
Preserve commands, actual exits, stderr, and unsuccessful attempts.
Check the source terminology: Hu's earlier “strongly p-Jordan” is the
Notebook's “p-Jordan.” No new priority is assumed for these elementary
deductions or the affine family.

The proof attempt then extended to all finite soluble subgroups, and
to uniformly bounded indices of soluble subgroups. A second proof will
give the elementary Chermak–Delgado characteristic-subgroup lemma and
apply it only inside O_(p')(G), preserving the p'-order requirement.
The quotient by that subgroup has self-centralizing p-group Fitting
subgroup, which makes the abelian coprime automorphism bound applicable
without a normal Sylow p-subgroup of G. Add new finite controls for this
stronger assertion, including S4 and a nonsoluble negative control.
