# Problem 20.21: exclusion through order 1,536

12 September 2026. Bounded computational exclusion, not a solution of
the general question. The independently checked minimality reduction
and this catalogue search imply that any counterexample has order at
least **3,072**. No new complete candidate is counted.

## Structural coverage of the catalogue

The reduction in `research/20.21-minimality-reduction.md` shows that a
smallest example has order 48*2^r. The earlier audited packets exclude
every such order below 1,536. Here the entire next order is excluded.

For a group of order 3*2^a, a C12 quotient forces a normal Sylow
2-subgroup: take the inverse image of the unique subgroup of order
four in C12. An A4 quotient excludes a normal Sylow 3-subgroup, whose
image would otherwise be a normal subgroup of order three in A4;
the quotient kernel has 2-power order. Thus only groups with normal
Sylow 2 and nonnormal Sylow 3 need examination.

SmallGroups partitions its 408,641,062 groups of order 1,536 into the
four intervals documented in `research/20.21-order1536-plan.md`.
Exactly the 18,028 indices 408526598 through 408544625 have the required
Sylow pattern. The metadata and the corresponding construction source
are archived. Eight boundary groups were constructed and their actual
Sylow-normality flags checked; both flags are also asserted for every
one of the 18,028 searched groups. The excluded intervals rely on the
catalogue's published classification, not on construction of all
408 million groups in this experiment.

Four disjoint congruence shards each examine 4,507 indices. The
C12-eligibility test uses the exponent of the abelianization. Every
normal index-12 subgroup is then tested, and all kernels for the two
quotient types are retained. Identification of order-128 kernels is
available even though IdSmallGroup is unavailable for the ambient order.
All four workers and their controller exit zero. A separate order-96
control reproduces every previously frozen row and exact count.

## Independent witnesses from actual kernels

Four exports reconstruct every retained ambient group and every relevant
kernel, exporting the actual 128-element multiplication table of each
kernel. Exported isomorphism identifiers are used only to check that
all search occurrences are represented; they are **not** the independent
nonisomorphism witnesses. The independent calculation derives from each
table its element-order counts and the orders of elements in its
abelianization.

| First separating invariant | Kernel pairs |
|---|---:|
| Element-order counts | 26,118 |
| Exponent of the abelianization | 434 |
| Element-order counts in the abelianization | 208 |
| Total | 26,760 |

The 30,414 occurrences contain 23,096 distinct tables, where equality
means equality of the full 16,384-byte table, not equality of a hash or
an isomorphism identifier. For each distinct table the checker verifies
the identity, every row permutation, two-sided inverses and every
associativity triple. The total is 48,435,822,592 triples. It forms the
subgroup generated by all commutators, constructs its quotient table,
checks that quotient is abelian, and computes all quotient element orders.
It then verifies a separating invariant for each actual pair.

Associativity is evaluated by a small C function, with all other steps
in Python. The C routine and pure Python agree on every table of size
two and three (19,699 tables), including the exact first failing triple,
and on cyclic tables and mutations of sizes 64, 128 and 256. Invalid
entries are rejected. Source, compiler command, compiler version,
compiled library and controls are retained with hashes. This speeds
the exhaustive axiom check without replacing it by sampling.

The four compressed archives total 304,007,641 bytes and expand to
2,080,251,641 bytes. Each export is compressed only after its actual
GAP exit is observed. The independent checker reads every archive,
rechecks its uncompressed hash and byte count, and verifies that the
multisets of kernel identifiers for every quotient type exactly match
the search. The uncompressed working files are retained locally; the
compressed versions are the committed artifacts.

## Reproducibility, imports and limits

The final packet auditor checks every new hash, the unchanged prior
order-768 packet, the catalogue-source and boundary bindings, all shard
counts and congruence coverage, process observations, the native/Python
agreement controls, and a full replay of all kernel-table calculations:

    python3 scripts/audit_20_21_order1536.py

Actual exits are recorded for the interval search (5f82ee), export
controller (17e16f), and independent kernel controls (466d97). The
final packet audit has its own separate observation, written after
the manifest is frozen to avoid circular hashes.

This does not independently reconstruct the ambient multiplication
tables and normal-subgroup lattices of all catalogue groups. It relies
on GAP/SmallGroups for the catalogue, interval classification, normal
subgroup enumeration and quotient recognition. All actual retained
kernels are checked independently. The prior reduction to orders
48*2^r is a separately read and proved literature result; its full
argument is retained in the earlier note. With those explicit
dependencies, the exclusion at 1,536 advances the lower bound to
3,072. General existence and novelty remain unresolved; the complete
candidate count stays 41.
