The example is X=F(a,b), N=<<a^m>>_X and
T=ker(X->C_m), where a maps to a generator and b maps to the identity.
For every m>=2 it satisfies 1<[T,N]=[X,N]<N<T<X. Taking m=4 also
ensures T is not maximal. The short argument in `11.115-proof.md` proves
the entire equality and all hypotheses without computational inputs.

## Internal mathematical audit

- The original PDF was viewed at printed/physical page 56. Its condition
  concerns N not being maximal; no restriction on torsion in X/N or on
  normality of T is printed. The chain N<T<X verifies this condition
  even if “maximal” is read among normal subgroups.
- Both T and N are normal in X, so K=[T,N] is normal in X. Forming X/K
  is justified before using that quotient. Normality is not being inferred
  from a list of commutators involving only a few elements.
- The image of a^m commutes with b because b belongs to T, and it commutes
  with a as a power of that element. These two checks suffice because
  a,b generate X. Its whole normal closure therefore has central image.
- The maps to C_m and Z separately prove T proper and b outside N.
  The nonempty reduced words a^m and [a^m,b], and the exponent-sum map
  to Z, prove the stronger strict chain. No finite search substitutes
  for these arbitrary-word arguments.
- The semidirect description F_m semidirect Z is checked by homomorphisms
  in both directions. The action cyclically shifts a free basis and the
  m-th power of the Z-generator is central. This also identifies N/K
  with Z and T/N with F_m.
- The earlier relation-module sufficient conditions do not apply: N has
  infinite index and its abelianization image contains no primitive vector.
  The distinction is the proper power a^m, rather than a primitive element.

and normality of the subgroups of F(a,b), their free ranks m+1, and 228
free-word identities. These include conjugation in both directions,
inverse substitutions, and all displayed commutator images. It explicitly
rejects replacing the last conjugation a s_(m-1) a^-1 by s_0 in the free
group. The actual process returned zero in 1.714 seconds, with empty stderr
and the required marker (tool receipt `a22d4d`). Seven literal word models

The independent Python checker uses only signed-letter reduction. For
each model it exhausts all 13,121 reduced F(a,b) words of length at most
eight and checks another 1,000 deterministic longer samples. In total it
checks 98,847 words, 7,000 products and 7,000 associativity triples in the
semidirect normal form. It separately reconstructs the Schreier rewriting
map and its inverse on the free basis. Five deliberately corrupted models
are rejected. This process returned actual zero in 1.923 seconds, with
empty stderr and its marker (tool receipt `5938e0`).

These checks validate formulas and implementation conventions. The
infinite-subgroup equality is the mathematical proof, not an inference
