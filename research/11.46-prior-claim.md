# Problem 11.46(a): exact August 2026 prior claim

Status: excluded from new priority; full proof and certificate unaudited.
Checked 11 September 2026.

Dai, Deng, Shi, Wu and Yang,
[*A Finite E-Group of Nilpotency Class Three*, arXiv:2608.07275v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.07275v1),
7 August 2026, explicitly claims an affirmative answer to 11.46(a).
The abstract, introduction and Theorem 1.1 were read. Their witness has
order 3^84. The claimed argument treats singular endomorphisms through
rigidity of an exterior-square tensor on the nine-dimensional Frattini
quotient and an exact computation on 9,841 projective points. The
automorphism case is earlier work of Abdollahi, Faghihi, Linton and
O'Brien. That earlier automorphism property alone would not answer the
endomorphism question.

Neither the tensor argument nor the finite reference verifier has been
independently checked here. This entry records an exact prior claim, not
an independently validated solution and not a new candidate of this run.
