The [20.89 residual reduction](20.89-residual-reduction.md) was also read
completely. A finite-index normal subgroup avoids the nontrivial points
of the finitely many sinks. The resulting Engel condition is in the
subgroups generated by that normal subgroup and each selected element.
The Hirsch--Plotkin radical reduction and the central-by-finite
centralizer give the necessary virtual solubility before applying the
finite-by-nilpotent theorem. Applying that result to finitely many
conjugates covering one minimal sink proves that the sink generates a
finite subgroup. This does not prove that its ambient normal closure is
finite, or supply a common finite sink for unrestricted products in
positive characteristic.
