# 20.8: a counterexample from the Lei--Zhang construction

Status: complete explicit negative answer derived from a prior construction;
excluded from the new-priority candidate count. The implication to this
Notebook question and all graph details below have been checked internally.
Outside review remains pending. No claim of first priority is made.

The printed question on page 147 was visually checked. It allows a
rank-three subgroup of a rank-two free ambient group, and requires every
proper intermediate subgroup to have rank at least that of K.

## Explicit groups and map

In F=F(a,b), put

    u=(a^(-1)b)^2,       v=(a^(-2)b^2)^2,
    H=<a,u,v>,
    K=<u, a u a^(-1), v, a^2 v a^(-2)>.

Then rank(H)=3, rank(K)=4, and the assignment

    phi(a)=b,       phi(u)=u,       phi(v)=v

defines a homomorphism H -> F different from inclusion, fixing K pointwise.
Moreover every proper subgroup J of H containing K has rank at least four.
Thus K<H<F is a counterexample.

The construction is the n=3 instance of Lei--Zhang,
[Colored Stallings graphs and counterexamples to the Stallings equalizer conjecture](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24502v2),
Proposition 3.3 and Construction 4.1. The extra intermediate-subgroup
condition is checked below. Their general equalizer theorem is not needed
as an unverified input to this particular certificate.

## Freeness and the fixed subgroup

First consider the abstract free group D=F(t,x,y) and maps g,h:D -> F(a,b):

    g(t)=a, h(t)=b, g(x)=h(x)=u, g(y)=h(y)=v.

To verify that g is injective, replace the domain basis (t,x,y) by
(t,tx,t^2 y). Its images are

    a,       d_1=b a^(-1)b,       d_2=b^2 a^(-2)b^2.

Here is a folded core graph for these three images. It has vertices
o,P1,P2,Q1,Q2,R. There is an a-loop at o, a b-path
o -> P1 -> P2, another b-path Q2 -> Q1 -> o, an a^(-1)-edge
P1 -> Q1, and an a^(-1)-path P2 -> R -> Q2. Every inverse edge is
understood. There are six vertices and eight topological edges.
No vertex has two outgoing edges with the same label. A spanning tree
gives the displayed loops a,d_1,d_2 as a free basis. The immersion in
the two-letter rose proves injectivity. Hence (a,u,v) is a free basis
of H and phi=h g^(-1) is well defined.

For i=1,2 put c_i=a^(-i)b^i. Since c_i commutes with c_i^2,

    a^i c_i^2 a^(-i) = b^i c_i^2 b^(-i).

These identities show that g and h agree on

    K_0=<x, t x t^(-1), y, t^2 y t^(-2)> <= D,

and therefore phi fixes K=g(K_0). They differ on t, so phi(a)=b!=a.

The folded core graph Gamma of K_0 has vertices 0,1,2, with base 0,
and these positive edges:

| Label | Initial vertex | Terminal vertex |
|---|---:|---:|
| t | 0 | 1 |
| t | 1 | 2 |
| x | 0 | 0 |
| x | 1 | 1 |
| y | 0 | 0 |
| y | 2 | 2 |

It has rank 6-3+1=4. Its four non-tree loops are precisely the given
generators of K_0. Injectivity of g gives rank(K)=4. The differing
ranks also establish both strict inclusions K<H<F.

## Every proper intermediate subgroup has rank at least four

Let K_0<=J<=D. The natural morphism Gamma -> Gamma(J) to J's
Stallings core graph maps each labeled edge to one edge. Its image Y
is a connected based subgraph, so rank(J)>=rank(pi_1(Y)). The image
is obtained by a partition of the three vertices of Gamma, followed
by folding edges. All five partitions give the following possibilities:

| Initial identifications | Vertices after folding | Edges | Rank |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| none | 3 | 6 | 4 |
| 0=2 | 2 | 5 | 4 |
| 0=1 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 1=2 | 1 | 3 | 3 |
| 0=1=2 | 1 | 3 | 3 |

For 0=1, the two outgoing t-edges force 2 to merge as well. For 1=2,
the two incoming t-edges force 0 to merge; inverse edges must be
included in this folding check. For 0=2 there is a t-cycle of length
two, x-loops at both vertices, and one y-loop at the base, with no
further folding possible.

Each rank-three image is the full rose with loops t,x,y at the base.
If this rose embeds in Gamma(J), then all three generators belong to
J and J=D. Consequently J<D forces rank(J)>=4. This argument also
allows infinitely generated J, whose core graph may be infinite.
Transporting by g proves the required condition in H. QED.

## Exact checks and reading scope

`scripts/verify_20_8.py` builds the subgroups from reduced words and
checks all five vertex partitions using an independent graph-folding
implementation. Both images of D have rank three, K_0 and K have rank
four, all four generator equalities hold, and the five quotient ranks
are 3,3,4,3,4 in enumeration order. The target graph of K has 17
vertices and 20 edges.

`scripts/verify_20_8.g`, using GAP's FGA package, independently confirms
the subgroup ranks, the three distinct quotient-subgroup ranks, the
two distinct homomorphisms, and their equality on all 457 words of
length at most three in the four free generators of K_0 and inverses.
The proof that every word is fixed is the homomorphism argument above.
