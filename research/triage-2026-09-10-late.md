# Additional literature triage, 2026-09-10

These notes preserve eliminated or restricted approaches, not new results.

## 21.133: countable recognizability of generalized soluble groups

Read Section 4 of F. de Giovanni and M. Trombetti,
*A Compendium of Infinite Group Theory: Part 1—Countable Recognizability*,
Mathematics 9 (2021), 2366,
[DOI 10.3390/math9192366](https://doi.org/10.3390/math9192366).
The cached full paper is
`references/cache/giovanni-trombetti-countable-2021.pdf`.

The precise class SJ, with a subnormal series of arbitrary ordinal type
and abelian factors, remains an explicitly stated open question there,
also referenced as ADV-2B (2016). Hyperabelian, hypoabelian, and subsoluble
groups have positive countable-recognizability results. In contrast the
ascending SN* condition is not countably recognizable. The distinctions
between normal terms, subnormal terms, and adjacent normal inclusions
must be preserved. None of these nearby results settles 21.133.

## 21.41: self-similar finitely presented metabelian groups

The obvious Baumslag construction

    Z[t,t^-1,(t+1)^-1] semidirect Z^2

contains Z wr Z but is already a known failed approach to self-similarity.
Kochloukova and de Sousa Luiz,
[*Non self-similar metabelian groups*, arXiv:2509.05798v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05798v1),
prove this using a more general criterion for cyclic module domains of
Krull dimension two. The introduction and Main Theorem were read; the
full proof has not been independently audited. The authors explicitly
discuss Dantas's question and conjecture a negative answer.

In particular, the known obstruction to **transitive** self-similarity
of Z wr Z does not imply it is not self-similar: an intransitive action
exists. Problem 21.41 cannot be answered by silently requiring a
transitive action. The higher-dimensional constructions in the related
Kochloukova--Sidki work have abelian modules of finite exponent; they do
not provide an embedding of Z wr Z.

## Other ideas not promoted to claims

- 17.128: a Golod--Shafarevich attempt to prescribe exponent p outside
  the specified subgroup introduces infinitely many relators of small
  degree. The usual positive-deficiency estimate does not apply.
- 21.16 and 16.88: distinguish bounds for each generating set from the
  stronger Bergman property involving arbitrary exhaustive subsets.
  Countability alone does not justify transferring an argument between
  these notions. Khelif's result, reproduced as Theorem 2.8 in
  [*On strongly just infinite profinite branch groups*](https://doi.org/10.1515/jgth-2016-0022),
  excludes countably infinite subgroups of compact groups from Cayley
  boundedness. Its Corollary 2.9 excludes infinite soluble-by-finite
  groups. These statements and proofs were screened, not the full paper.
- 21.39: standard infinite locally finite characteristically simple
  candidates with unbounded exponent cannot have only finitely many
  automorphism orbits. No suitable example has been constructed.
- 11.46 and 21.40: no new result; further primary-literature work needed.
