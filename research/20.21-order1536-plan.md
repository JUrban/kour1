# Problem 20.21: structural catalogue interval at order 1,536

12 September 2026. The previous audited packet excludes every possible
counterexample of order below 1,536. This plan authorizes no result before
the new search and independent controls finish.

Let |G|=3*2^a and suppose G has both C12 and A4 quotients. The preimage
of the unique Sylow 2-subgroup of C12 is a normal Sylow 2-subgroup of G.
G cannot have a normal Sylow 3-subgroup: its image in A4 would be a
normal subgroup of order 3, because the quotient kernel has 2-power
order. A4 has no such subgroup. Thus both quotient types force a normal
Sylow 2-subgroup and a nonnormal Sylow 3-subgroup.

The installed SmallGroups catalogue has 408,641,062 groups of order
1,536, partitioned as follows:

| Catalogue indices | Classification | Exclusion |
|---|---|---|
| 1 through 10,494,213 | nilpotent | normal Sylow 3 |
| 10,494,214 through 408,526,597 | normal Sylow 3 | no A4 quotient |
| 408,526,598 through 408,544,625 | normal Sylow 2, nonnormal Sylow 3 | search all 18,028 |
| 408,544,626 through 408,641,062 | neither Sylow subgroup normal | no C12 quotient |

The interval boundaries are printed by SmallGroupsInformation and
confirmed in the implementation `small8/smlgp8.g.gz`: direct products,
2-nilpotent construction, 3-nilpotent construction, then the remaining
groups. We import completeness and classification of the catalogue;
we do not reconstruct the 408 million excluded groups. Eight boundary
groups are independently constructed within GAP and their two actual
Sylow-normality flags checked. Each of the 18,028 searched groups also
has both flags asserted before any quotient filter.

The new interval scripts are separate from the frozen order-768 code.
A two-worker order-96 control must reproduce every frozen pilot row
and its exact counts. The main interval uses four workers with 1 GiB
GAP workspace ceilings, alongside the six ongoing 14 GiB n=7 workers:
at most 88 GiB in these GAP workspace limits and ten main CPU workers.
Independent audit jobs use small additional memory.

The interval search retains all normal index-12 kernels when both
quotient types occur and identifies their isomorphism types. Every
apparent match requires an explicit isomorphism and then independent
review. A negative result must export actual kernel data and provide
independent nonisomorphism witnesses, as in the previous packet.
No general conclusion follows merely from the absence of a match.
