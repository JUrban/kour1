# Problem 21.76: a characteristic-three rank-one seed

12 September 2026. No solution claimed before verification.

Seek a closed but noncompletable pair of additive subgroups of F9.
The subgroup SL2(5) embeds in SL2(9), and its Sylow 3-subgroups have
order three. Two opposite unipotent generators should give a pair
F3 and beta*F3 with beta outside F3. Exact 2-by-2 matrix enumeration
will test every nonzero beta, avoiding reliance on that subgroup
classification. Check that the generated group has precisely these
two root intersections and that beta is not in F3.

If such a pair exists, put R=F9[t], K=F9(t), and take the elementary
net over F9 supported only at positions (1,2) and (2,1), with the two
closed levels. Lift it to R by inverse image under evaluation t=0.
Every lifted level contains tR, making the net irreducible for n>=3.
The elementary group has entries in R, and reduction maps it into the
closed rank-one seed group. This should prove closedness over K.
The failure of sigma12*sigma21*sigma12 inside sigma12 survives on
constant coefficients, so no completion by diagonal groups is possible.
