# Internal audit of the 19.60 coefficient obstruction

11 September 2026. Complete prior-theorem deduction for the literal
arbitrary-computable-PID reading; no new-candidate increment.

1. The printed statement allows m=1 and d=2, and imposes no
   arithmetic or finite-generation restriction on R. The source
   page was visually checked together with 19.56.
2. A fixed computable field with undecidable roots of unity is an
   established theorem, not an assumption that equality is undecidable.
   The field's effective coding includes decidable equality.
3. Inversion is an effective operation on nonzero field elements,
   either included in the presentation or obtained by search.
   Thus constructing diag(a,a^-1) from a field code is effective.
4. R=F[t] is an integral domain, is not a field, and has computable
   polynomial division and a Euclidean degree function. Hence even
   an effective Euclidean-domain interpretation does not remove
   the example.
5. Both diagonal entries are constant units of R, and their product
   is one. Every supplied X(a) belongs to SL(2,R), not merely to
   a matrix monoid or to a fraction-field group.
6. The power equivalence is exact for every positive n. Infinite
   order is precisely free generation by a one-element tuple.
   Inputs a=0 and a=1 can be handled before the freeness call;
   trivial-group conventions have no effect.
7. An algorithm only on the specified valid inputs would suffice
   for the contradiction. There is no requirement that it decide
   whether arbitrary encodings are valid matrices or units.
8. The coefficient field and its numbering are fixed. The argument
   is not a statement merely about lack of uniformity as R varies.
9. The read 1979 source contains an erroneous auxiliary cyclotomic
   bound. Its undecidability lower reduction does not depend on that
   bound. The note gives a valid replacement and explicitly excludes
   the unexamined final typeset version from reading claims.
10. The conclusion is restricted to the coefficient scope stated.
    It does not convert semigroup freeness undecidability into
    matrix-group freeness undecidability, and does not settle Z or Q.

The proof and source scope are in `19.60-coefficient-obstruction.md`.

Tucker; its direct matrix consequence is recorded as prior work.
