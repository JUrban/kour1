# 20.124 source and priority audit

September12,2026. The exact unstarred statement is retained in
`results/20.124-statement.json`. The existing rendering
`results/20.122-source-page165.png` was visually checked again: its
lower portion contains20.124, including the weight-one identity and
the phrase "Let F be a non-abelian free group." There is no stated
rank restriction. A construction in infinite rank is therefore a
substantial permitted case, but is not treated as answering the
question for an arbitrary specified finite-rank free group.

## Primary works inspected

* Bardakov–Gubarev, *Rota–Baxter operators on groups*,
  [arXiv:2103.01848v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.01848v2), later
  Proc. Indian Acad. Sci. Math. Sci.133 (2023), article4,
  DOI10.1007/s12044-023-00723-9. Read the definitions and the Section7
  construction with two homomorphisms and a bijective map, including
  Theorem7.7, and the subsequent free-group examples through
  Question7.13. This supplies the existing construction framework;
  our two inverse maps and the exact identity are proved directly.
  The downloaded file is explicitly the2021v2 preprint, not an
  independently checked copy of the final journal version.
* van Doorn–Judin–Monticone–Morrison, *On Some Problems from the
  Kourovka Notebook*, [arXiv:2607.17477v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17477v2),
  July26,2026. Read the abstract, introductory list of results and
  the complete Section5 proof. Its surjective noninjective operator
  is on a direct product with an abelian sequence group and answers
  20.125. It does not provide an operator on a free group with
  commutator image. That already solved question is not counted again.
* Guo–Lang–Sheng, *Integration and geometrization of Rota–Baxter
  Lie algebras*, [Adv. Math.387 (2021),107834](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2021.107834).
  Read the publisher's definition and introductory explanation of
  the group identity. The work is credited as the source of the
  group notion; none of its analytic integration results is used.

The first two PDFs, their extracted texts and retrieval hashes are
stored in `results/20.124-sources.json`. The source audit does not
claim to have checked the Lean formalizations mentioned by the2026
paper, nor any of its seven other complete proofs.

Queries about free-group commutator images, locally nilpotent
endomorphisms and fixed-point-free constructions did not identify a
prior statement of the infinite-rank result. Such a limited search
does not establish novelty. No new complete-candidate count is made.

## Boundaries kept explicit

The endomorphism method needs infinitely many basis elements: an
endomorphism of a finitely generated group cannot have image the
infinite-rank derived subgroup of a finite-rank nonabelian free group.
This argument does not apply to arbitrary Rota–Baxter maps, which
need not be homomorphisms for the original group operation.

The20.89 follow-up at the start of this turn did not resolve its
ambient-sink gap and produced no extension of the committed proof.
The finite-sink subgroup established there cannot simply be replaced
by its normal closure without proving that closure finite.
