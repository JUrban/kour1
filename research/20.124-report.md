## The construction

Partition a free basis into levels, each as large as the whole basis.
Map each positive level onto the commutator subgroup generated by all
previous levels, and kill level zero. The resulting endomorphism phi
has image exactly F' and kills each input after finitely many iterates.

The maps D(g)=g phi(g)^-1 and T(g)=g phi(g) phi^2(g)... are inverse
set bijections. Therefore

    B(g)=phi(T(g))=phi(g) phi^2(g)...

has image F' and satisfies the printed Rota–Baxter identity. The proof
in `20.124-infinite-rank-proof.md` gives every cancellation and an
explicit preimage x w^-1 whenever phi(x)=w. All products are finite.
