# 17.124: new prior preprint located

Achyuth Jayadevan, *Finite presentations of metabelian groups:
effective enumeration via Laurent relations*,
[arXiv:2609.10281v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.10281v1),
submitted 9 September2026, claims the full affirmative answer in
Theorem1.1. It constructs a primitive-recursive finite-certificate
predicate for metabelianity of an ordinary finite presentation.

The introduction explicitly distinguishes ordinary group presentations
from presentations inside the metabelian variety, so this is the
actual Notebook question, not merely enumeration of presentations in
a fixed variety. The stated route is an effective Bieri--Strebel
covering construction, giving finitely presented metabelian groups
cofinal under epimorphisms, with finite derivations witnessing the
maps onto the input group.

Reading scope: the primary abstract, Section1 and Theorem1.1,
Section2's two classical-input lemmas, and the start of Section3
including the Fourier--Motzkin equivalence were read. The remaining
proof and ancillary Lean formalization have not been audited or run.
The paper is cached as `references/cache/jayadevan-metabelian-2026.pdf`
with extracted text. This is a prior claim, not an independently
certified resolution in this repository. It is excluded from
new-result priority; no new answer to17.124 is claimed here.
