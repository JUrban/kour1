# Bounded finite subgroups: plan for 20.89 / 19.83

2026-09-12. This is an additional sufficient hypothesis, not a full answer
to the unrestricted linear-group question. No priority is asserted.

1. Use the already established local finite-by-nilpotent property for
   the subgroup generated by all almost Engel elements. Check that its
   torsion elements form a locally finite characteristic subgroup.
2. Use the bound on orders of finite subgroups to make this torsion
   subgroup finite. Prove that it is one sink relative to the entire
   ambient group, including the product and inverse quantifiers.
3. Derive the virtually torsion-free case without assuming that the
   specified torsion-free subgroup is normal. Record the scope, a
   positive-characteristic example, and an obstruction to dropping the
   bound. Bind the proof and its existing mathematical dependencies in
   a small evidence manifest and make a local commit.

The proof is an elementary consequence of the preceding reduction.
Finite enumeration cannot establish its infinite-group assertions, so
no computational experiment is offered as a substitute for the proof.
