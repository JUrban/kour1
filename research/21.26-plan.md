# Simultaneous inclusion-minimal Sylow intersections: 21.26

## Exact reduction

For a Sylow p-subgroup H define

    S_H={x in G : H intersect H^x is inclusion-minimal among all H intersect H^g}.

Each Sylow conjugate K has the same number |N_G(H)| of transporters x with
H^x=K. Therefore |S_H|/|G| is the fraction of Sylow conjugates giving an
inclusion-minimal intersection. Compute all intersections as sets of actual
group elements and discard those properly containing another intersection.
Minimum order is not substituted for inclusion-minimality.

For any choices H_p, the elementary union bound gives

    |intersection_p S_(H_p)| >= |G| - sum_p (|G|-|S_(H_p)|).

The cardinality of S_(H_p) depends only on p, since Sylow subgroups are
conjugate. Thus if the sum of the bad fractions is strictly below one, the
conjecture holds simultaneously for *every* Sylow choice, without enumerating
all tuples. The inequalities and fractions are exact rational arithmetic.

If this bound fails, the implementation can enumerate all choices. For one
base Sylow H, compute S_H as a disjoint union of transporter cosets N_G(H)a.
For H^a its allowed set is S_H^a. Encode these as bit lists on G. Fix one
Sylow by simultaneous conjugation, enumerate the remaining choices, and test
their set intersection. During recursion, a union bound for the remaining
bad-set sizes prunes branches only when it proves a nonempty final intersection.
This fallback has not yet been needed in the completed search ranges.

## Validation and initial results

The independent verifier directly enumerates H intersect H^x for every x in G
and every Sylow subgroup in every non-prime-power-order group of order <=60.
It compares all allowed conjugator sets to the optimized coset construction and
then checks every Sylow tuple directly. All 211 groups and 1,333 allowed sets
agree. This tests actual group elements, not just profile counts.

The first run checked all 4,188 non-prime-power-order groups of orders 2–255.
Every case satisfied the strict union bound; no fallback or counterexample.
Prime-power groups have only one Sylow subgroup and are immediate.

Further runs cover orders 256–2000 with at least three distinct prime divisors.
Completed blocks and the live job are recorded in state/jobs.json and the logs.
Do not claim completion of the full range until all DONE markers are present.

## Literature

Francesca Lisi and Luca Sabatini, *Sylow subgroups for distinct primes and
intersection of nilpotent subgroups*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.21222 , was
downloaded and inspected. Conjecture A is the question. Theorem 1.1 and the
preceding observation settle groups whose orders involve at most two primes.
Theorem 1.5 treats metanilpotent groups of odd order, and Theorem 1.4 treats
sufficiently large symmetric and alternating groups.

Burness–Huang, *On the intersections of nilpotent subgroups in simple groups*,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.03479 , claims the conjecture for all non-alternating
simple groups. This was checked at abstract level; its proof has not yet been
audited here. The revised Lisi–Sabatini paper also cites the result. Searches
should therefore concentrate on composite extensions and soluble groups with
at least three relevant primes, not repeat a simple-group range.

All negative searches are bounded computational evidence, not a general proof.
