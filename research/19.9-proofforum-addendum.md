## Symplectic counterexample

The paper uses Sp_(2r)(Z), r>=4. The required identification of
its topological-cover pullback with its universal central extension
is stated in Benson--Campagnolo--Ranicki--Rovi,
[primary published PDF](https://msp.org/agt/2018/18-7/agt-v18-n7-p10-s.pdf),
page4071. This page was freshly viewed. The printed bound is
**g>=4**; text extraction incorrectly renders the comparison as
g>4. Thus r=4 is covered. The original local choice r=5 remains valid.

Two details in the preprint's short proof need explicit attention:

1. Its sentence that every finite quotient kills a nonzero multiple
   of the central generator is insufficient if that multiple is
   allowed to vary. Even Z has that property. The actual Deligne
   theorem gives the fixed subgroup 2Z in every finite-index
   subgroup. Page4071 explicitly states this uniform conclusion.
   It proves failure of residual finiteness.
2. Malcev's theorem requires the tensor square to be finitely
   generated. The universal central extension E of a perfect G
   is perfect. If H is generated by lifts of finitely many
   generators of G and C is the central kernel, then E=HC and
   E=E'=(HC)'=H'<=H. Thus E is finitely generated.

With these supplied details, Brown--Loday identifies the tensor
square itself with E and the argument is valid. It does not rely
on the invalid inference from a non-residually-finite quotient.
The full Deligne and Brown--Loday theorems remain imported, with
the statement-reading scope recorded in `19.9-proof.md`.

## Braid groups

The new paper gives a shorter conceptual detection argument than
the local Clifford construction. In the five-term sequence of
1 -> P_n -> B_n -> S_n -> 1, the coinvariants of H_1(P_n) are Z:
the standard pure-braid basis is permuted transitively by S_n.
The induced map to H_1(B_n)=Z is multiplication by2, because
A_12=sigma_1^2. It is injective. Consequently

    H_2(B_n;Z) -> H_2(S_n;Z)

is onto. Both groups have order2 for every n>=4, so this map
is an isomorphism. These homology orders are prior inputs.

The natural morphism from B_n wedge B_n to the pullback of
S_n wedge S_n along B_n' -> A_n is then an isomorphism: it
is identity on the quotient B_n' and is the preceding
isomorphism on the central Hopf kernels. The finite exterior
square S_n wedge S_n and the linear group B_n' have linear
direct product. Its pullback subgroup is linear. Finally the
standard splitting for B_n,ab=Z gives

    B_n tensor B_n = Z x (B_n wedge B_n).

This proof covers n=4 without assuming B_n' perfect. All groups
can be represented over a common characteristic-zero field.
It establishes the same full affirmative answer as the retained
Clifford proof. The latter additionally gives an explicit degree
bound and independent finite matrix controls.

The source proof is sound with the two symplectic clarifications
above and its stated prior homological and linearity inputs.
