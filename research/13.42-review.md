# Problem 13.42: internal mathematical review

This is an internal audit, not an independent referee report.

1. **Exact target.** A=Z[t] is a unital commutative integral domain
   with torsion-free additive group. The source N is the ordinary free
   nilpotent group of rank two and class three.
2. **Universal category.** Definitions 1 and 2 in the original authors'
   paper quantify over every A-group satisfying their four axioms.
   They do not restrict targets to nilpotent groups, to faithful scalar
   actions, or to A-torsion-free groups. The relevant pages were viewed.
3. **Polynomial variable.** The t in x^t is a scalar variable. It is
   distinct from the group-algebra basis notation T^q. Conflating them
   would destroy the construction.
4. **Finite formulas.** The BCH multiplication has only terms of Lie
   degree at most three. Its realization in a truncated associative
   algebra proves associativity for all rational coordinates, without
   a convergence assumption or a finite-sample extrapolation.
5. **Faithful coordinate realization.** The five Lie vectors are
   independent, and finite exp and log are inverse polynomial maps.
   The first-three-coordinate conjugation formula and commuting
   criterion were also checked symbolically in ten generic coordinates.
6. **Commutativity criterion.** Equality of the two products in (2)
   requires all three minors, not just aB-bA. This stronger class-three
   condition is essential for the conditional axiom.
7. **Degenerate triples.** Zero triples and negative or zero scalar
   multiples cause no exception to homogeneity and additivity on a
   line. F is defined as zero when a=b=0, with no division by zero.
8. **F need not be a homomorphism.** Only its restriction to commuting
   pairs is additive. Assuming additivity on arbitrary pairs would
   give a false model; the conditional hypothesis is retained exactly.
9. **Right action.** The semidirect product is (l,v)(h,w)=(lh,v^h+w),
   so conjugation on M multiplies by T^beta(h). This agrees with the
   plus sign in c+aB-bA and the sign of the iterated commutator.
10. **Finite support.** Every F(l) is one basis vector times a rational
    number, and every operation in M preserves finite support. No
    completion of Q[Q], field of fractions, or analytic limit is used.
11. **Coherence for ring multiplication.** The identity
    f(g^n f(g)^m)=f(g)^n holds for all integers, including negatives.
    It yields the product coefficient ad+bc. This checks the full
    polynomial scalar law, not only the operation labelled t.
12. **Conditional product axiom.** For commuting g,h, equivariance
    also gives [f(g),h]=[g,f(h)]=1. These cross-commutations justify
    the rearrangement; normality or abelianness of M alone would not.
13. **Ordinary torsion.** H is torsion-free, as an extension of two
    torsion-free groups. The scalar t^2 annihilates all its elements;
    this is permissible A-torsion and is explicitly disclosed.
14. **Free source.** We only need a homomorphism from N to the subgroup
    generated by exp X,exp Y. Since that subgroup has class at most
    three, the map exists by definition of N. No unproved freeness or
    injectivity of a Lie-coordinate subgroup enters the proof.
15. **Enough of the target.** The image of N^A contains x,y and x^t.
    It need not equal all H. The nonzero iterated commutators belong
    to that image, which is sufficient to refute nilpotency of N^A.
16. **Unbounded witnesses.** (T-1)^r is nonzero because its top
    coefficient is one on the distinct basis vector T^r. This proves
    every r, not just the 64 retained finite controls. It also refutes
    local nilpotency using the fixed pair x^t,y.
17. **Class-two boundary.** Dropping V and W would make exp X commute
    with exp U. Then F(exp X exp U)=T differs from
    F(exp X)+F(exp U)=1. Thus the same proposed scalar action fails
    the conditional axiom in class two. No class-two counterexample
    or conflict with known class-two results is asserted.
18. **Earlier induction failure.** Quotienting by the A-closure of the
    original centre need not preserve the conditional axiom. We do
    not use that unjustified quotient in any part of the final proof.
19. **Evidence.** The generic associative-algebra check is independent
    of the BCH coordinate multiplication. Numerical controls use exact
    Fractions and full integral polynomial convolution, including
    higher terms, negatives and ten polynomial choices.
20. **Source limits.** Original Definitions 1 and 2 and their context
    were read; the later CSA construction is not imported. The 2026
    Amaglobeli--Miasnikov introduction and definitions were also read
    at https://arxiv.org/html/2601.17600v1 . That paper concerns
    completions in a restricted class-two category; its full proof
    was not audited and is not a premise here.
21. **Priority.** Limited searches found the original question and
    recent work on restricted class-two completions, but no explicit
    prior version of this construction. This does not establish novelty.

Disposition: no mathematical gap identified. Complete affirmative
candidate, with independent review and priority still pending.
