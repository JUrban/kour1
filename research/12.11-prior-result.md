# Problem 12.11: a published nondegenerate counterexample

Status: negative consequence of prior work; excluded from new priority.
Checked 11 September 2026.
The exact supplied statement on printed page 58 was visually checked.

Let G and H be countable groups and A proper in each, containing no
nontrivial subgroup normal in both. The Notebook asks whether G *_A H
admits a faithful highly transitive action on a countable set.

Fima, Le Maître, Moon and Stalder, *A characterization of high transitivity
for groups acting on trees*, Discrete Analysis 2022:8,
[DOI 10.19086/da.37645](https://doi.org/10.19086/da.37645), give a
nondegenerate counterexample in
[Section 8.3.1, Proposition 8.33](https://arxiv.org/html/2003.11116v4#S8.SS3.SSS1).
Their construction uses the type-preserving subgroup Gamma of tree
automorphisms whose local permutations belong to a fixed regular cyclic
group except at finitely many vertices. For an edge with endpoints v,w,
write G=Gamma_v, H=Gamma_w, A=Gamma_{v,w}. Remark 8.31 proves countability;
Remark 8.32 identifies Gamma with G *_A H. Proposition 8.33 proves that
A is core-free in Gamma, that the amalgam is nondegenerate, and that Gamma
has no faithful highly transitive action.

To match the Notebook's normality condition explicitly, suppose N is a
subgroup of A normal in both G and H. Since G and H generate Gamma,
conjugation by any alternating word preserves N. Thus N is normal in
Gamma and is contained in the core of A in Gamma, which is trivial.
The vertex stabilizers are countable as subgroups of Gamma; the indices
of A in them are the tree degree d, and d can be three. Hence all the
stated hypotheses hold, including the properness on both sides.

The construction and the entire proof of Proposition 8.33 were read.
Its obstruction to high transitivity invokes Le Boudec--Matte Bon,
*Triple transitivity and non-free actions in dimension one*, Corollary 1.5;
that underlying proof has not been audited here. The relation to Problem
12.11 is our explicit inference; the paper does not need to name the
Notebook entry for the example to settle it. No new construction or
independent proof of the imported obstruction is claimed.
