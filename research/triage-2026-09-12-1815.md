## The remaining 20.89 obstruction

The torsion-free lift in the characteristic-zero proof fails in
positive characteristic because an infinite unipotent normal subgroup
can be a torsion group. Local finite-by-nilpotence of the subgroup
generated by almost Engel elements does not alone repair this: every
locally finite group has that local property.

An attempted reduction through infinite locally finite simple sections
would require an individual almost-Engel-automorphism theorem that was
not proved. Wehrfritz's argument excluding those sections assumes
every element of the group is almost Engel; applying it prematurely
to the generated subgroup would be circular. Likewise, virtual
nilpotence of the ambient group was not obtained in general. No such
reduction is claimed.

The useful independent result is instead the unit-parameter argument
in `20.89-normal-algebra-proof.md`, covering normal subgroups of
finite-dimensional algebra unit groups over every infinite field.
The finite-field bound and explicit failure of the omitted-normality
hypothesis are checked separately. This improves partial coverage
without closing the full positive-characteristic question.

## 20.83: why the existing normal-kernel example does not suffice

Read Section 4, including Lemmas 5--6 and Examples 1--2, of Revin--
Zavarnitsine, [*The behavior of pi-submaximal subgroups under
homomorphisms with pi-separable kernels*, arXiv:2006.09752v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.09752v1).
The proof of the preceding embedding proposition was only partly
read; it is not an imported input to a new result here.

For pi={2,3}, the construction uses the two dual three-dimensional
modules V,V* for PSL2(7), exchanged by an outer automorphism. In
H=(V direct-sum V*) semidirect PSL2(7), the Sylow 2-subgroup is
pi-submaximal, while its image in H/V* is not. The exchange sends
V* to V, so this kernel is not characteristic in H. It therefore
does not answer the Notebook's characteristic-kernel question.
No construction preserving this exchange while making that kernel
characteristic was found.

For a safe concrete field choice in this discussion one may use F11,
where x^2+x+2 splits with distinct roots. The paper's general finite-
field module and automorphism assertions were not independently
validated beyond that prime-field setting. No finite group from this
source was computed or counted as our own example.

The later primary indexed final page of
[*Automorphisms of nonsplit extensions of 2-groups by PSL2(q)*,
J. Group Theory 24 (2021), 1245--1261](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/jgth-2021-0067/pdf?licenseType=limitedFree)
explicitly leaves the characteristic-kernel extension open, even for
a pi-subgroup. The browsing open returned HTTP 405, so no full-paper
audit is claimed. The 2020 PDF and extracted text were retrieved
successfully and are cached; their source record is
`results/20.83-source-download.json`.

## 20.81: a bounded-height route excluded by prior work

The tempting class of soluble groups of Fitting height at most h
cannot satisfy the requested arbitrarily large conjugacy-class
witness width. Proposition 4.1 of
[*On verbal subgroups in residually finite groups*, Bull. Aust. Math.
Soc. 84 (2011), 159--170](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/8E6179E6401EE58F4B3CAB4F1E027814/S0004972711002188a.pdf/on_verbal_subgroups_in_residually_finite_groups.pdf)
states that if every subgroup generated by four conjugates of x is
soluble of height at most h, then x lies in F_h(G). Its normal closure
therefore has height at most h. The primary indexed proposition and
its attribution to Flavell and collaborators were read; its cited
full proof was not newly audited. No general assertion for arbitrary
hereditary Fitting classes is inferred.

Further speculative routes for 17.96, 17.98 and the finite-rank part
of 20.124 produced no construction or impossibility proof. In
particular a proposed homomorphism on a free basis still does not
establish that its difference map is bijective on every word.
