# Problem 19.77: prior affirmative preprint

12 September2026. Excluded from new candidate count.

Ilya Kapovich, *Small Cancellation Stability and Isomorphism Rigidity
for Generic Finitely Presented Groups*, arXiv:2608.17238v1,
18 August2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17238, gives the exact
affirmative conclusion in Theorem A and Corollary1.2. For fixed rank
m>=2, tuple size q>=1, and any0<lambda<1, the set of tuples whose
entire automorphism orbit satisfies C'(lambda), after cyclic reduction
and symmetrization, is exponentially generic. Both freely reduced
sphere and ball models are covered, so lambda=1/6 applies to19.77.
