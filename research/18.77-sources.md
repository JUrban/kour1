# Source audit for the 18.77 partial

12 September 2026. All files below are retained with SHA-256 binding.
Reading a theorem is distinguished from verifying its full imported proof.

1. D. S. Passman, *Character Theory and Group Rings*, author-hosted PDF:
   https://people.math.wisc.edu/~passman/charprob.pdf . Section 1, Definition
   1.1, Lemmas 1.2–1.3, Problem 1.4 and Proposition 1.5 with its proof were
   read in extraction. The source states the exact problem, known small-e
   cases and counterexamples for e>=p. No new priority is claimed for
   these examples. The rest of the article is not an input here.
2. I. M. Isaacs and D. S. Passman, *A characterization of groups in terms
   of the degrees of their characters. II*, Pacific J. Math. 24 (1968),
   467–510, https://msp.org/pjm/1968/24-3/pjm-v24-n3-p08-s.pdf . The
   introduction and Section 1 from the character-kernel definition
   through Corollary 1.8 were read in text. Printed pages 472–473
   (PDF pages 7–8) were rendered and viewed. Theorem 1.5 already proves
   the class-two case, including simultaneous detection of p-e elements.
   Its minimal-counterexample argument and use of earlier character
   results were read; those earlier imported results were not re-audited.
   Later sections were searched for relevant terms, not fully read.
3. M. Boyarchenko and M. Sabitova, *The orbit method for profinite groups
   and a p-adic analogue of Brown's theorem*, arXiv:math/0608126v1,
   4 August 2006, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0608126 . Introduction,
   finite Lie-ring correspondence in Section 2.1, and Theorem 2.6 were
   read in text. Printed/PDF page 12 was rendered and viewed. The
   theorem provides the exact irreducible-character formula for finite
   p-groups of class less than p, for p>=3. Its proof begins on that
   page, but the full preceding formal-series construction and the
   complete proof have not been independently audited. The theorem
   is an explicit imported input.
4. E. A. O'Brien and C. Voll, *Enumerating classes and characters of
   p-groups*, https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~obrien/research/characters.pdf .
   Introduction and Section 3.2, Theorem 3.1 and its radical-index
   formulation, were read. Printed/PDF page 7 was rendered and viewed.
   This primary author copy corroborates the orbit size and degree
   formula used here; its later counting theorems and proofs were not
   read in full. arXiv metadata: https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.3050 .
