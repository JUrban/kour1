# Problem 13.42: an explicit polynomial-exponent model

11 September 2026. Proposed complete construction; verification pending.

Use A=Z[t] and the free nilpotent group N=F(x,y)/gamma_4(F(x,y)).
It suffices to construct an A-group H and a homomorphism N->H such
that [x^t,y,...,y] is nontrivial for every number of y entries. The
universal property then forces N^A to be non-nilpotent.

1. Let L be the rational BCH group of the free class-three Lie algebra
   on X,Y, with basis X,Y,U=[X,Y],V=[X,U],W=[Y,U]. Use coordinates
   (a,b,c,d,e). Commuting elements have proportional (a,b,c) triples.
2. Let M=Q[Q], additively, with basis T^r for r in Q. Let L act on
   M by right multiplication by T^b and form H=L semidirect M,
   with (l,v)(m,w)=(lm,v*T^b(m)+w).
3. Define F(l)=a*T^(c/a) when b=0 and a!=0, and F(l)=0 otherwise.
   Regard f(l,v)=(1,F(l)) as an element of the normal abelian M.
4. Prove conjugacy covariance, additivity on commuting pairs,
   f(g^n f(g)^m)=f(g)^n, and [g,f(g)]=1.
5. For P in Z[t], define g^P=g^(P(0))*f(g)^(P'(0)). Verify all four
   Myasnikov--Remeslennikov axioms, including their conditional axiom.
6. For x=exp X, y=exp Y, x^t=T^0 in M and iterated commutators
   are (T-1)^r, which are nonzero for every r.
7. Verify exact BCH coordinates through truncated free associative
   algebra, then test the semidirect and exponentiation conventions
   over exact rational numbers. Freeze source scope and all actual exits.

The scalar action factors through Z[t]/(t^2), but A itself is an
integral domain with torsion-free additive group. The stated category
does not require faithful scalar action or absence of A-torsion.
The underlying group H is torsion-free. Avoid substituting Hall's
nilpotent completion or a completion restricted to nilpotent A-groups.

The earlier centre-quotient induction fails because quotients by a
central A-subgroup need not satisfy the conditional commuting-product
axiom. That failed argument is not used in this construction.

The primary axioms and universal property are in Myasnikov--Remeslennikov,
Exponential groups 2, Definitions 1 and 2, pages 1 and 3:
https://web.stevens.edu/algebraic/alexeim/Publications/All_files_new/expo2jj_29.pdf .
Only the relevant definitions and introductory scope have been read
in detail; the later CSA-group construction is not used.
