# 18.111: prior negative answer

Frigerio--Sisto, *Central extensions and bounded cohomology*, Annales
Henri Lebesgue 6 (2023), 225–258, Theorem 1.2, constructs a central
extension of a finitely generated group by the infinite cyclic group
which is quasi-isometrically trivial and has an unbounded Euler class.
Primary publication: https://ahl.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/ahl.164/ .

This supplies the negative answer requested in 18.111. In the paper's
notation, rename E to the Notebook's G and the paper's G to Q. Its
definition of quasi-isometric triviality includes a quasi-isometry
E -> Z x G, so it satisfies the first of the Notebook's alternative
hypotheses. The paper also notes immediately before Question 1.9
that the cyclic kernel is undistorted, so this example satisfies the
second alternative too.

Reading scope: primary abstract, introductory definition, Theorem
1.2, its footnote, and the discussion around Question 1.9. The full
small-cancellation construction and its proof were not audited in
this pass. PDF/text cached with prefix
`references/cache/frigerio-sisto-2023`.

The footnote reports a later finitely presented example by
Ascari--Milizia; their primary abstract at
https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.03972 was inspected, but its proof was not
audited. That strengthening is unnecessary for the countable-group
question here.

Disposition: exclude from new-result priority and new-candidate counts.
The cited theorem, rather than an independently checked full proof,
is the basis for this literature exclusion.
