# Internal audit of the near-Frattini prior deductions

1. The exact printed lambda, mu and psi definitions are retained.
   Each positive deduction proves mu=1, so psi actually exists.
2. All amalgam theorems are used with proper factor inclusions.
   The collapsed-amalgam convention is stated as a scope limitation.
3. Under an epimorphism, the full preimage of a nearly maximal
   subgroup is nearly maximal. This proves the needed image property;
   no image-of-intersection equality is assumed.
4. A finite normal subgroup is contained in lambda for arbitrary
   ambient groups. The proof uses the finite index [SK:S], not a
   finite-generation or normality assumption on S.
5. In 19.3(b), core_G(H) is normal in each factor, so the
   intersection hypotheses force it to vanish before applying (a).
6. In 19.3(c), normality of mu turns containment in H into
   containment in core_G(H). Minimum condition is not maximum condition.
7. For the retraction argument, both factor maps fix the identified
   H pointwise. Their glued map is injective on H, which is where
   the upper-bound theorem places mu.
8. For 19.3(e), infinite-rank factors have an unused basis letter.
   In finite rank, the Hall complementary factor is nontrivial
   precisely because [A:H] is infinite. One trivial conjugate
   intersection suffices for the cited 2005 corollary.
9. The connected-sum argument uses meridian retractions. Trivial
   knot/cabling conventions that collapse the decomposition are
   explicitly excluded from the scope claimed by this deduction.
10. The cable argument uses the abelian pushout to check G' intersect B.
    Nontrivial mu makes B's image finite, and torsion-free
    abelianization then makes it trivial, giving the contradiction.
11. Finitely generated lambda=mu is proved by Zorn plus Schreier,
    with the finite-index union case ruled out. This equality is
    not extended to arbitrary groups.
12. Two errors in the 1994 proofs were checked in page images;
    neither argument is imported. Later published upper bounds
    and the stated knot decompositions are the actual dependencies.
13. All source reading limits are explicit. Full-text reading is
    distinguished from auditing cited earlier arguments. The 2005
    countable-case proof is not needed for these deductions.
14. These are prior-theorem consequences. No new candidate or
    outside review is claimed; 19.3(d) remains open in this work.
