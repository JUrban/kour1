# Problem 18.77 partial report

12 September 2026. Complete candidate count unchanged at 37.

The alternating-form lemma in `18.77-partial-proof.md` proves the
character-regularity conjecture for all finite p-groups of class less
than p, without an exponent-p restriction. `18.77-simultaneous.md`
strengthens this to simultaneous detection of any p-e nonidentity
elements by one largest-degree irreducible representation. The
class-two case has an elementary proof through a complex twisted
algebra, but was already proved in stronger form by Isaacs--Passman
(1968), Theorem 1.5. Priority of the small-class deduction is unresolved.

The ring proof is the main substantive argument: alternating normal
form gives a Pfaffian of degree r<=e; image bounds control its p-adic
valuation, and interpolation permits division of all its coefficients.
For one-element detection, the vanishing leading coefficient closes
the e=p-1 boundary. For simultaneous detection, a homogeneous polynomial
of degree at most p cannot vanish on the whole prime-field vector space.
The exact orbit-method input and the limits of its audit are documented
in `18.77-sources.md`.

The first controls check 63 deterministic random matrix pencils at
22,063 total parameter values, including four sharp threshold families
and all 15,625 derived-character parameters for UT_5(F_5). Of the random
pencils, 46 satisfy e<p and all pass the spanning conclusion. A separate
additive image enumerator agrees on 476 matrices. The UT_5 case has
e=4 and 10,000 maximum parameters spanning all six derived coordinates.
The degree-one coordinates do not affect its commutator form and are
free parameters; the calculation is not an enumeration of irreducible
characters themselves.

A second control checks 32 further pencils at 5,772 parameter values
and exhausts 578 sets of p-e forbidden hyperplanes in their two-dimensional
parameter spaces. All pass simultaneous detection. A targeted Z/25
family has Pfaffian 5x^2+5xy although its entries need not be divisible
by 5. Its 625 parameters check the nontrivial division step, with four
additional direct image enumerations. These are exact algebra controls,
not a finite test of the imported general orbit-method theorem.

The initial controls and their outer runner actually exited zero in
5.23 seconds, with a clean sentinel. The simultaneous controls and
runner also exited zero. Individual tool completion records, hashes
and replayable code are retained in the packet.

No general-group alternating-form parametrization is asserted. Groups
outside the proved class hypotheses remain untreated, and the work
adds no complete answer to the Notebook count.
