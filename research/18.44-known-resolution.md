# 18.44: excluded because of an existing full resolution

Jones--Keller, *Orbit sizes and the dihedral group of order eight*,
Annali di Matematica Pura ed Applicata199 (2020), 2323--2340,
[DOI 10.1007/s10231-020-00970-x](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10231-020-00970-x),
Theorem1.2, gives precisely the affirmative assertion of Problem18.44.
The article explicitly identifies this Notebook problem immediately
after the theorem. It was published online on 11 March2020.

The hypotheses are a finite nonabelian G, a finite faithful irreducible
module V, largest orbit size M=|G/G'|, and exactly two orbits of size M.
The conclusion is G=D_8 and V the two-dimensional space over F_3.
There is no extra solvability hypothesis in the theorem.

Reading scope: the publisher's abstract, introduction, Theorems1.1--1.2,
and the following remarks were read. The full article was retrievable
as publisher HTML and is cached, but its long case-by-case proof has
not been audited. The introduction notes a correction to an earlier
paper's proof; no assessment of that correction is claimed here.

Further generalizations were located in Çınarcı--Keller,
*Linear group actions with at most three orbits of the largest size*,
[DOI 10.1007/s00605-023-01850-1](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00605-023-01850-1),
and *The largest orbit sizes of linear group actions and abelian
quotients*, J. Algebra654 (2024), 1--24,
[DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2024.05.012](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021869324002473).
Only their abstracts, initial introduction material, and available
publisher snippets were inspected; their full classifications are not
needed for this exclusion and were not audited.

No new orbit search was launched once the exact prior result was
identified. Problem18.44 is excluded from new-candidate priority and
from our complete-candidate count.
