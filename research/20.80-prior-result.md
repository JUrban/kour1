# 20.80: prior affirmative result of Lu

11 September 2026. Excluded from the 25 possible-new candidates.

Jiakuan Lu, *On permutation characters of finite groups*,
[arXiv:2511.20171v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.20171v1),
25 November 2025, Theorem A, proves the missing sufficiency:
if every irreducible constituent of every maximal-subgroup coset
permutation character is monomial, the finite group is soluble.
Together with the necessity already recorded in the Notebook,
this answers 20.80 affirmatively.

All five preprint pages were read, including the full Theorem A
proof. Its quotient reduction, unique minimal normal subgroup,
Frattini argument, faithful constituent and strict subgroup-index
comparison were checked. The terminal kernel statement in the
first full paragraph of page 3 has a reversed sign: it must say
ker(psi)=1. The preceding contradiction proves precisely this,
and the ensuing argument uses it correctly. This is a repairable
typographical error, not a missing inference.
