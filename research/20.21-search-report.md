The only preliminary filter is exact: a finite group admits a cyclic
quotient of order12 if and only if the exponent of its abelianization
is divisible by12. To see sufficiency, use the invariant-factor
decomposition of a finite abelian group: its last cyclic factor has
order equal to its exponent, and hence has a quotient of order12.
Necessity follows because any map to an abelian group factors through
the abelianization. GAP takes the least common multiple of its
abelian invariants to compute that exponent.
