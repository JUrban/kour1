# Problem19.61: finite-ring C2 carpet pilot

12 September2026. General question unresolved.

The question concerns the closure of an initial elementary carpet,
not the root intersections of an arbitrary root-generated subgroup.
The latter can fail the carpet conditions and is not a counterexample.

Troyanskaya2024, *To the Question of the Closure of the Carpet*,
J. Sib. Fed. Univ. Math. Phys.17(5),684–688, gives prior positive
cases in odd characteristic, excluding characteristic divisible by3
for G2. The primary five-page PDF was downloaded and the mathematical
text read; no page images have yet been viewed. The B2 sign-separation
argument was inspected, but the G2 calculation is not independently
certified. Source:
https://elib.sfu-kras.ru/bitstream/2311/153269/1/Troyanskaya.pdf .

Plan a direct search in type C2 over F2, F4, F2[t]/(t^2),
F2 x F2, and Z/4. Use the standard4-by-4 symplectic root matrices
on the eight roots. Derive each commutator's individual root
factors by exact matrix multiplication and unique factorization
over the small ring. This retains the actual signs and factors2
in characteristic4. Exclude opposite-root pairs, where the
Chevalley commutator formula under consideration does not apply.

Enumerate every additive subgroup of each ring, then every
assignment to the eight roots satisfying all the initial carpet
conditions. Export faithful permutation actions on all ring
vectors to GAP. For each valid carpet, generate its subgroup,
test membership of every root element, and check the resulting
closure against every carpet implication. Export any failure
with an explicit failed pair, root factor and subgroup data.

A hit will require independent matrix reconstruction, verified
initial carpet conditions and a proof that the disputed root
factor is outside the generated group. A negative pilot is only
bounded evidence. Keep the source's stronger odd-characteristic
claims and the full general question separate. Begin with one
small worker under the current CPU/memory reservation.
