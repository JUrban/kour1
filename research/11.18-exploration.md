## Permutation composition convention

Example 5 of Slanina--Tomaszewski, printed p.487, lists three pairs
generating A7. That page was visually checked. GAP interprets cycle
products as right actions. Copying the second printed pair directly
gives a nonperiodic initial orbit in the second commutator direction.
Inverting both printed generators translates the opposite composition
convention to GAP's convention and makes all three examples mep pairs,
of respective minimal periods 49,16,18 in both directions.

An independent tuple-based Python implementation checks all 24 orbits
from the three pairs and four inversion choices. It exactly matches
the retained JSON. In particular, the second pair as copied has
transient 12 and cycle length 16 in its second orbit; after both
inversions its two transients are zero and periods are 16. The raw
GAP message SOURCE_PAIR_NOT_PERIODIC concerns the copied convention;
it is not a claimed mathematical counterexample to the source.

## Source boundaries

Heineken (2000), *Groups generated by two mutually Engel periodic
elements*, Boll. UMI (8)3-B, 461--470:
https://www.bdim.eu/item?fmt=pdf&id=BUMI_2000_8_3B_2_461_0
All main statements and the text of their proofs were read. Printed
pp.463,467,468 were additionally visually checked for the canonical
matrices and the coordinate recurrences. Original Dickson and
Coxeter--Moser arguments cited there are imported, not reverified.

Writing b_i,d_i for the upper-right and lower-right entries, the paper
sets e_i=lambda b_i+(lambda^2-1)d_i and obtains

    b_(i+1) = b_i e_i,
    e_(i+1) = lambda^4-lambda^2
              +(lambda^-2-lambda^2)e_i+lambda^-2 e_i^2.

Its auxiliary period-two condition gives e_0 e_1=lambda^2 and
z^3-2z^2-z+1=0 for z=lambda+lambda^-1. In characteristic zero that
cubic has a root between 2 and 3, so the corresponding lambda need
not be a root of unity. The changing b-coordinate prevents reading
this as a complex mep construction. Finite-field periodicity does
not provide unbounded finite quotients of one fixed G(a,b).

Slanina--Tomaszewski (2007), *Groups Generated by (near) Mutually Engel

## A structural reformulation for possible later work

For b>=2 put u=[x,y]. The relation x=[u,y] is equivalent to

    x^y=xu, u^y=ux.

Since [y,x]=u^-1, the other relation is
y=w(x,u):=[u^-1,_(b-1) x]. Eliminating y gives the exact Tietze form

    G(2,b) = <x,u | x^w=xu, u^w=ux>.

Indeed, adjoining u=[x,y] to the original presentation gives these
relations. Conversely x^y=xu recovers u=[x,y] and u^y=ux recovers
x=[u,y]; substituting y=w recovers the remaining defining relation.
The one-relator group before imposing y=w has the ascending-HNN
presentation for the substitution x->xu, u->ux. No inference about
the size of the further quotient is made. Global Thue--Morse word
identities in all pairs would be different hypotheses.
