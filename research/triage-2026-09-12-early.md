# Early12 September: bounded leads and reasons not to count them

This records mathematical triage so unfinished ideas are not silently
promoted into results or repeatedly rediscovered. No complete candidate
is added by this note. Separate packets contain20.1,20.3,20.59 and20.21.

- **20.103:** residual torsion-free nilpotence of a RAAG does not by
  itself make all its derived-series quotients torsion-free. Possible
  torsion in higher derived-subgroup abelianizations remains the issue.
- **20.124:** a Rota--Baxter map B corresponds to the subgroup
  {(gB(g),B(g))} of F x F with bijective difference map (u,v)->uv^-1.
  Asking for image F' suggests a subgroup parametrized by
  {(psi(b),b):b in F'}, with psi(b)b^-1 bijective onto F. No such
  homomorphism psi was constructed. Failure of a splitting complement
  to F' does not exclude a general Rota--Baxter map. Goursat kernels
  are normal in their projections, not automatically in F.
- **20.126:** the question is about ordinary braces with finitely
  generated abelian additive group. A regular subgroup of the integral
  affine group need not act properly on real affine space. The usual
  multiples mA need not be brace ideals merely because they are left
  ideals. Neither shortcut proves the desired solubility. Infinite-rank
  Magnus-ring brace constructions do not settle this finite-rank case.
- **20.120:** simple modules over Z[t,t^-1] are finite, excluding the
  abelian construction. Obvious translation/dilation automorphisms of
  McLain-type groups preserve interval or cut ideals. No nonabelian
  locally nilpotent example with the required simplicity was obtained.
- **20.85:** the standard almost-primitive definition requires the
  element itself to be nonprimitive. Primitive words are not a valid
  loophole. The candidate a^2 b^3 is not tame under the intended
  definition; different notions of tameness must not be conflated.
- **19.39:** a minimal-Frattini argument cannot assume that
  H intersection Phi(G) is contained in Phi(H).
- **20.75,20.76:** self-centralizing abelian subgroups give embeddings
  into automorphism groups, but that bound alone is too weak. Large
  commuting nilpotent matrix spaces do not prove the desired factor2
  comparisons for maximal abelian normal subgroups.
- **20.84:** small remembered dimension-subgroup counterexample orders
  were unreliable. No finite-group search was launched on that basis.
- **21.136:** passing to a countably based profinite quotient can
  preserve an element of infinite order. A relative isolated point in
  the space of infinite-order conjugacy classes does not imply positive
  Haar measure: torsion may be dense. The proposed Polish-space argument
  therefore stops at that step.
- **21.114:** adding an abelian direct factor cannot repair failure of
  weak ab-maximality; a violating subgroup can be padded by the same
  factor. Congruence-group ideas did not give a valid example.
- **21.140:** familiar Thompson variants tend to contain F, while
  simple wreath-product attempts lack the required finiteness type.
- **21.118:** no argument from residual finiteness or soficity was
  established that decides the amenable-kernel quotient question.
- **20.25:** parity of character values on odd-cycle columns has
  constraints from self-conjugate partitions, but those do not control
  all other partitions or prove the proposed asymptotic density.
- **20.36:** a finite p-quotient of a presentation is not evidence that
  the presented group itself is finite. No suitable rewriting
  certificate was constructed.
- **20.113:** modern ANUPQ might help, but four-generator class13
  dimensions and memory costs were not bounded. No computation was
  launched while the large certificate verification reserves memory.
- **20.73:** a finite interpretation of “countable” is not used to
  evade an intended infinite construction.
- **20.104:** recovering factors from the first-order theory of a
  direct product is obstructed by centres; no interpretation supplied.
- **20.55:** no iteration of character-table coincidences was found
  that gives the requested separation in derived length.
- **21.98:** finite cyclic coverage of word values gives some finite
  generation reductions, but a normal set contained in finitely many
  cyclic groups has not here been proved to have finite conjugacy
  classes. This missing assertion cannot be assumed.
- **21.103:** completeness and minimality do not justify every claimed
  inheritance or product property without the relevant topology
  theorem. No general arbitrary-product proof was obtained.
- **21.148,21.149:** the distributive-lattice analogy with rational
  subspaces does not prove the general convex-subgroup assertion.
  Dlab terminology requires its actual ordered-group definition.
- **17.114:** degenerate amalgams identifying whole factors are not
  counted as answers to a question intended for proper amalgams.
- **18.3,18.5:** finite unions of products of abelian subgroups do not
  immediately produce a finite-index metabelian subgroup; similarly,
  Hirsch-rank additivity for an arbitrary soluble factorization was
  not proved by a Lie-hull analogy.
- **21.102:** finite word-function groups suggest a chief-factor or
  module approach to exponential growth. Finite many critical groups
  alone do not establish an integral growth base. The newly located
  Olshanskii2606.04577v3 gives substantial prior cases but explicitly
  leaves the general limit and integrality questions open. See the
  separate source note; no general proof is claimed.

These are obstacles and unproved leads, not claims of impossibility.
Only sources explicitly audited in separate notes are imported.
