The [characteristic-zero](20.89-char0-proof.md) and
[bounded-finite-subgroup](20.89-bounded-torsion-proof.md) partial proofs
for 20.89/19.83 were also read completely. The torsion subgroup under
the local finite-by-nilpotent property is constructed before being
used; its finiteness follows from the separate lifting or boundedness
hypothesis. The final common sink is relative to the entire ambient
group, by taking a first commutator into the normal generated subgroup.

For that source check, the retained Shumyatsky text was read from its
linear-group preliminaries through Lemmas 3.1--3.7, Theorem 3.3,
Lemma 4.1, and Theorem 4.2. This includes the proof using a minimal
infinite centralizer in the locally finite case. The proof there
requires that an element chosen in an infinite abelian centralizer
is almost Engel. In the present unresolved positive-characteristic
closure problem, the subgroup initially generated by almost Engel
elements has not been proved to consist entirely of such elements.
That source theorem therefore cannot be applied at that stage.
No unrestricted positive-characteristic closure result is claimed.
