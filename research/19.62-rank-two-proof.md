# Problem 19.62: a short rank-two proof

12 September 2026. This strengthens and shortens the complete candidate
frozen in `19.62-proof.md`. Outside review and priority remain pending.

**Theorem.** If A is an elementary carpet over a commutative ring K
and B is its derived carpet, then

    A_p^2 B_-p is contained in B_p, for every root p.                (1)

Here squares are pointwise squares. The root system can be any reduced
irreducible crystallographic root system of rank at least two. Therefore
every derived carpet is closed, answering 19.62 affirmatively.

We give a rank-two reduction and nineteen explicit short derivations.
All 104 targets, including every root rather than only orbit
representatives, also have independently checked integer certificates.
The short certificates contain 655 nodes in total, with at most nine
nodes, including three initial variables, in any individual derivation.

## The reduction

Write c=|C_ij,rs| for an absolute Chevalley commutator constant, with
factors ordered by increasing (i+j,i,j). The group B_-p is additively
generated by the elements

    c u^i v^j,  u in A_r, v in A_s, i r+j s=-p.                    (2)

Signs do not affect the generated additive subgroups. Fix x in A_p.
Multiplication by x^2 is additive, so (1) reduces exactly to

    c x^2 u^i v^j in B_p                                         (3)

for each generator (2). There is no expansion of a square of derived
summands and no assumption about division by integers in K.

The roots p,r,s lie in a plane U. The intersection Psi=Phi intersect U
is a reduced crystallographic root system: reflection in any of its
roots preserves U. It is irreducible, since roots in two orthogonal
components cannot have a positive linear combination that is a root.
Thus Psi has type A2, B2 or G2. All root strings between roots of Psi
remain in U, so restriction of A gives a carpet with the same absolute
commutator constants. Every derived summand in the restriction is also
a derived summand of A. It therefore suffices to prove (3) in these
three systems. Restriction need not commute with the full derived
operation, and the argument does not require it to do so.

## The rank-two derivations

Use simple roots a,b, with a short in B2 and G2. The positive roots are

    A2: a, b, a+b;
    B2: a, b, a+b, 2a+b;
    G2: a, b, a+b, 2a+b, 3a+b, 3a+2b.

Together with their negatives these lists give every possible source
and destination. They also show that every positive pair i,j in a
commutator term has i,j at most three. The independent coverage checker
solves the two-by-two root equation by integer determinants, without
assuming this bound in its enumeration.

There are six targets in A2, twenty in B2 and seventy-eight in G2,
after identifying the harmless interchange of independently named
variables. If an identical target occurs with different requested
integer coefficients, requesting their gcd suffices for both. The
target dictionary is reconstructed independently from every ordered
source pair and compared exactly.

It is enough by Weyl transport to display targets at a+b in A2; at
a+b and 2a+b in B2; and at 2a+b and 3a+2b in G2. These account for
one, five and thirteen targets respectively. Transport relabels the
whole carpet; it does not assume that A itself is Weyl invariant.
Simple reflections preserve all absolute rules and transport every
target to a displayed one. Both facts are checked on the complete
Weyl groups of orders six, eight and twelve.

All nineteen derivations are written out in
`19.62-rank-two-derivations.md`. Each line is either an initial variable
in its assigned A subgroup, an application of a Chevalley carpet rule,
or the integer gcd of two such applications with the same monomial.
Every noninitial line belongs to the appropriate B subgroup, and hence
also to A. The last line has the target monomial and a coefficient
dividing the requested c. This proves (3) using only addition, integer
multiplication and the carpet conditions.

For example, consider G2 with p=2a+b, x in A_p, u in A_a and
v in A_(-3a-b). The relevant opposite derived summand is uv. The rules
give

    3xu in A_(3a+b),   xv in A_-a,   x^2v in A_(a+b).

Applying rules to the first two expressions gives 3x^2uv in B_p;
applying the rule with constant two to x^2v and u gives 2x^2uv in B_p.
Their difference is x^2uv in B_p. This illustrates how the argument
works even when two or three is a zero divisor. The remaining displayed
derivations use the same elementary logic.

## From (1) to closedness

The original carpet conditions imply B_p is contained in A_p. Moreover
B is a carpet directly: for a rule with destination i r+j s,

    C_ij,rs B_r^i B_s^j
      is contained in C_ij,rs A_r^i A_s^j
      is contained in B_(ir+js).

Now (1) implies B_p^2 B_-p is contained in B_p. The square closedness
criterion says precisely that a carpet satisfying these inclusions is
closed. A direct proof of this known criterion is supplied in
`19.62-closedness-criterion.md`, including all symplectic ranks over
arbitrary rings. It uses integral Lie lattices and symplectic matrices,
without division by two. Thus B is closed in every type and every
characteristic, as asserted.

Polarizing (1) also gives the stronger auxiliary inclusion

    2 A_p A_p B_-p is contained in B_p,

where the two factors A_p can be chosen independently. This follows
from (x+y)^2-x^2-y^2=2xy; it is not needed to conclude closedness.

## Verification and prior work

The mathematical inputs are the standard Chevalley construction, the
rank-two root-system classification and their commutator identities.
Exact polynomial matrix calculations over Z[t,u] verify every absolute
constant used. Integral Cartan data and root reflections identify the
three models. The ordinary-integer checker validates roots, exponents,
parent availability, products, powers and gcds at every step. Twelve
corruptions are rejected. Removing a redundant gcd edge and its unused
ancestors shortens 763 original nodes to 655 checked nodes. The printed
nineteen derivations are reproduced from those short certificates.

The earlier 71,056-target proof and all its evidence are preserved as
an independently audited route. Neither finite-ring experiments nor
the C++ optimizer's search procedure are assumptions of this proof.

by Carpets of Symplectic Type*, Siberian Math. J. 67(1) (2026), 110–119,
Theorem 2 (Russian original p.136):
