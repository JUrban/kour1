# 15.36: an exact published negative answer

Status: excluded from new-result priority. The primary publisher abstract

The supplied Notebook page87 was visually checked on11September2026.
For a group class M, L(M) consists of groups in which every element's
normal closure belongs to M. The question asks whether L(M) must be
finitely axiomatizable when M is generated as a quasivariety by a finite
group.

S. A. Shakhova, [*The Axiomatic Rank of Levi Classes*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10469-018-9510-9),
Algebra and Logic57(5) (2018),381--391, explicitly states that some finite
groups G have L(qG) of infinite axiomatic rank and identifies this as the
answer to Notebook15.36. The quantifiers and the Levi-class definition
match the printed question. Infinite axiomatic rank rules out a finite
axiomatization.

The publisher records publication on1December2018 and translation from
Algebra i Logika57(5),587--600. Its additional positive assertion concerns
a restricted family of quasivarieties and does not contradict the general
negative answer. The accessible publisher preview does not expose the
counterexample or its proof; no independent verification of those details
is claimed. This is prior work, not another candidate solution from this
project.
