## Explicit check at the stronger question's obstructed pair

Our standard-library checker reconstructs the printed 8-by-8 matrices
over F_2[w]/(w^3+w+1). It checks all 512 field associativity triples and
512 distributivity triples, the displayed matrix identities, the
52-element subgroup H=<s,y>, and the 48 thirteenth-power relations.
These source identities are prior and add no new result.

The following direct inference explains the distinction relevant to our
task. The checked data have x not in H, y=x^t in H, and y^x=y^(-1).
Thus y fixes the two distinct right cosets H and Hx. Its conjugate x
also fixes two distinct cosets, explicitly Ht^(-1) and Hxt^(-1).
Meanwhile x takes H to Hx. The two claimed fixed cosets, their
distinctness and their stabilizer conditions are verified directly
with matrices. Thus x is a valid witness for 21.99 at the obstructed
pair in the source's stronger question.

The full generated group's identification with the Steinberg triality
group, its order, maximality of H, the remaining millions of coset
pairs and the source's full search are not independently certified by
this matrix check. The positive two-coset witness itself needs none of
those imports. The group's finiteness follows from its realization
inside GL_8(F_8).

## Catalogue and search algorithm

GAP's installed transitive-group catalogue supplies the group generator
permutations and the complete counts in the selected degrees. Those
catalogue classifications and labels remain imported. Every positive
property of every supplied permutation model is instead checked by an
independent word evaluator. No GAP conjugacy-class, group-order,
stabilizer or character algorithm is trusted for that verification.

An exploratory call to `Factorization` on TransitiveGroup(24,24998)
hit the wrapper's 4 GiB workspace cap; GAP returned zero despite the
error. Its exact command and tool observation are preserved, and no
successful result from it is claimed. Local GAP documentation in
`lib/grp.gd` warns that this routine can enumerate all group elements.
The accepted producer builds words directly and never calls it.

The prime-power-degree proof and semiregular-normal-subgroup reduction
are included in full, with priority unclaimed. The source searches did
not establish a novelty claim or a proof for arbitrary degree.
