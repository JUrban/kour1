# 20.108(c): already answered affirmatively by Caranti--Tsang

Status: prior published affirmative result, excluded from new-priority
claims. This corrects our earlier provisional status for part(c).

Caranti and Tsang,
[Finite p-groups of class two with a large multiple holomorph](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.15205v2),
Theorem1.1, gives for every odd prime p and every n>=4 a group G of
order p^(n+binomial(n,2)) such that T(G) contains
GL_n(F_p) x GL_(binomial(n,2)-n)(F_p).

Take p=3 and n=4. Then |G|=3^10, and GL_4(F_3)<=T(G).
Since5 divides3^4-1, it divides |GL_4(F_3)| and hence |T(G)|,
whereas5 does not divide(p-1)p=6. This is exactly the requested
affirmative example. Corollary1.2 gives the stronger assertion that
every finite group embeds in T(G) for a suitable such p-group.

The paper first appeared30 May2022; v2 is dated4 December2022,
and it was published in J. Algebra617(2023),476--499. The primary
introduction, Theorem1.1 and Corollary1.2 were read. The full bilinear
construction and its proof were not independently audited.

The centreless order605 construction for(a),(b) remains a separate
candidate in `20.108-proof.md`; these p-group examples cannot be
centreless and do not by themselves settle(a) or(b).
