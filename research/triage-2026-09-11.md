## 14.22: complete candidate from finite coefficient support

The one-variable rational line is irreducible, but no finite equivalent
system can have its radical equal to its normal isolated closure. A
finite system involves a cyclic rational coefficient subgroup C;
Q *_C (C x Z) is a torsion-free separating target, and a commutator
with a rational root outside C witnesses the strict containment.

The same proof extends to the nonabelian linear coefficient group
Q * Z, with an explicit SL_2(Q(t)) embedding and an elementary
polynomial specialization proof of irreducibility of the centralizer
line. This removes any concern about restricting the general geometry
to nonabelian CSA coefficient groups. Neither example is finitely
generated; no such hypothesis appears in the printed question.

The proof and audit are in `14.22-proof.md` and `14.22-review.md`.
All exact controls pass. No prior matching resolution was located in
the initial literature search; novelty and outside review remain
pending. This adds an eighth candidate after the separate exclusion
of 17.34.

## 21.130: adjacent sums on arbitrary odd-order subsets

The problem asks for a cyclic ordering of every subset A, |A|>2, of an
odd-order abelian group, with distinct adjacent sums. Existing harmonious
group results for A=G do not settle the arbitrary-subset question.

Read Section 3.2 of Matt Ollis's 2025 sequencing survey,
[*Sequenceable Groups and Related Topics*, EJC DS10v3](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/DS10/pdf/).
Theorems 22 and 24 cover full groups and groups minus the identity,
respectively. Its reference 94 is Javaheri--de Wolf,
*Harmonious sequences in groups with a unique involution*,
arXiv:2408.16207, concerning a different particular subset.

Also located Arya Mosahab,
[*A Classification of Cyclic Orderings with Distinct Adjacent Sums*](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=7166164),
posted August 2026. The abstract classifies initial intervals in cyclic
groups. Only the abstract was screened, not its full proof.

Sun's original additive-combinatorics paper is
[arXiv:1309.1679](https://arxiv.org/abs/1309.1679).
The author-hosted published PDF could not be downloaded in this attempt.
No general resolution or substantive new result was established, and no
new exhaustive subset search was launched before checking its prior scope.

## 19.15: fixed finite cogenerator remains an essential restriction

For a fixed finite nilpotent A, the question concerns groups whose every
cyclic normal closure belongs to qA, not merely groups whose cyclic
normal closures have uniformly bounded nilpotency class or exponent.
An example in a nilpotent variety need not be residually A and therefore
does not automatically belong to qA.

Read the introduction of Budkin,
[*On groups whose normal closure of 2-generated subgroups are 3-nilpotent*](https://math-semr.ru/en/content/23-1/0420),
Siberian Electronic Math. Reports 23 (2026), 420--430,
DOI 10.33048/semi.2026.23.027. It discusses Levi classes and a different
two-generator-normal-closure condition. No implication for the fixed
finite qA question was established from that paper. The full proof has
not been audited.

Potential Burnside-exponent-four or matrix constructions with unbounded
nilpotency would still need the strong residual-A property for each
normal closure. No such construction has been verified. Finite group
enumeration by itself cannot find a nonnilpotent example when the given
normal closures are nilpotent; this lead requires an infinite construction
or a general uniform bound.

## 14.26: avoid a wreath-product convention trap

An unrestricted wreath product Q wr Z has an exponential-coordinate
element on which the shift acts by doubling, giving a relation forbidden
in torsion-free nilpotent groups. This does not address a restricted
wreath product. The printed phrase “direct wreath product” must be checked
against the author's convention before using that observation. No answer
is claimed from it.
