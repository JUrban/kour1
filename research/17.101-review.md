# Internal audit of the candidate for17.101

Date: 2026-09-11. This is an internal mathematical audit. No outside
review or confirmed priority claim has taken place.

## Statement and category

The supplied printed page115 was inspected as an image. It asks whether
every group representation embeds in a homogeneous one, where finitely
generated means a finitely generated acting group and a finitely generated
module over its group algebra. The module need not have finite dimension.

The usual category is Rep-k for a fixed commutative ring k with identity.
Both group and module are allowed to enlarge; morphisms have compatible
components on these two sorts. This is verified in Gvaramia--Plotkin2004,
Section1.1, and Aladova--Gvaramia--Plotkin2012, Section2.1. The latter
paper's Definition5.8 and Problem5.11 confirm the exact notion of
homogeneity. No metric, topology, norm, or finite-dimensionality is
required. Our construction preserves the coefficient ring throughout.

## Main mathematical risks

1. **Preserving the group.** The group is the actual multiple HNN
   extension on the specified subgroup isomorphisms. Its canonical base
   group map is injective by the HNN theorem. An arbitrary overgroup
   implementing the group maps would not suffice for the proof, since
   extra stable-letter relations could create cycles in the module diagram.
2. **Submodules are only subgroup-invariant.** U_i is a kA_i-module,
   not necessarily a kG-module. Accordingly each edge term is induced
   over kA_i, and the vertex term is induced over kG. The formula
   delta_i(h tensor u)=h tensor u-h t_i^(-1) tensor f_i(u) respects
   precisely the kA_i balancing relation. The equality a t_i^(-1)=
   t_i^(-1)theta_i(a) and equivariance of f_i prove well-definedness.
3. **Cosets and endpoints.** The vertex spaces are indexed by hG,
   and the type-i edge spaces by hA_i. The edge endpoints hG and
   h t_i^(-1)G agree with the same balancing calculation. The graph
   is the Bass--Serre tree for the multiple HNN group. Each endpoint
   map is an inclusion or f_i, composed with an invertible group action.
4. **No collapse from infinitely many relations.** The quotient is by
   an algebraic submodule. Every individual relation combination has
   finite support in the edge direct sum, even when the whole index
   set is infinite. A nonempty finite forest has a leaf away from any
   prescribed single vertex. Its nonzero coefficient cannot disappear
   through an injective edge map. Hence the relation submodule meets
   every individual vertex module trivially. No topological closure,
   infinite sum, splitting of submodules, or flatness assertion is used.
5. **The prescribed maps really extend.** The boundary relation implies
   t_i j(u)=j(f_i(u)), while HNN conjugation gives t_i a t_i^(-1)=
   theta_i(a). The pair (w->t_i w,h->t_i h t_i^(-1)) is an automorphism
   of the entire new representation, with inverse defined by t_i^(-1).
6. **The extension survives later enlargements.** At the next stage,
   the representation embedding preserves the action of every existing
   group element, including t_i. The same inner pair remains an
   automorphism of the final union. This avoids attempting to extend
   an arbitrarily chosen automorphism separately at every later stage.
7. **Finite generation is used correctly.** A finite tuple of group
   generators and a finite tuple of module generators occur together
   at one stage. Their whole subrepresentation then lies in that stage.
   An isomorphism between two such subrepresentations is among those
   processed at a later stage. Infinite k-dimension is harmless.
8. **Set size and coefficients.** All isomorphisms within one stage form
   a set. At an infinite cardinal bound kappa including |k|, their
   number is at most kappa because images of finite generating tuples
   determine them. Induction, HNN formation, quotient, and countable
   union preserve that bound. The proof also works for modules with
   torsion and non-split inclusions over a commutative ring.

## Computation and its limits

`python3 scripts/verify_17_101.py` passes400 finite tree cases over
F2,F3,F5,F7 and3,391 vertex-injectivity tests. Another120 trees of
cyclic modules pass all1,191 edge-coefficient combinations. These
include non-split subgroup inclusions, so the test does not silently
assume direct summands. A three-edge cycle over F3 deliberately kills
the vertex modules, confirming why the tree hypothesis is essential.

There are503 controls of the enlarged unipotent representation and87
equivariance controls on a proper, non-G-invariant S3 submodule. The
verifier also proves by linear-system inconsistency that the chosen
partial isomorphism has no commuting linear extension on the original
three-dimensional space. It constructs an extension in dimension four.

`bin/gap -q -b -T -m 128m -o 4g scripts/verify_17_101.g` independently
checks503 vectors in the exact Laurent-polynomial realization over the
same four prime fields, and the group/stable-letter relations. A first
run revealed that GAP distinguishes a finite-field zero from a constant
polynomial zero in equality tests. All constants were explicitly moved
to the polynomial family; the corrected run passes. This was a verifier
type mismatch, not a change to the mathematical construction.

These finite checks support the algebra and conventions. They do not
certify infinitely many HNN words or the final homogeneous union. Those
assertions rest on the normal-form theorem and the written proof.

## Primary literature and novelty scope

- Gvaramia--Plotkin, *Action-type axiomatizable classes of group
  representations*, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406544 . Section1.1
  and the following basic logical definitions were read. The rest was
  screened for homogeneity/HNN/amalgamation terms, not fully audited.
- Aladova--Gvaramia--Plotkin, *Logic in representations of groups*,
  Algebra Logika51:1 (2012),3–40; Algebra and Logic51:1 (2012),1–27,
  DOI10.1007/s10469-012-9167-8,
  https://alglog.org/51-60/51/51N1-1.pdf . Read the introduction,
  Section2.1, Definition5.8, Propositions5.9–5.10, Problem5.11 and
  its discussion. The latter explicitly leaves the embedding problem
  open and identifies the module-map difficulty after ordinary induction.
  The rest of the paper was not fully audited.
- Plotkin1977, https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/rm3237 : primary abstract
  confirms the same fixed-ring category. English and Russian full-text
  downloads returned403; no full-paper reading is claimed.
- The standard HNN and tree results were checked in Bridson--Nyberg-Brodda,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10800 : introduction, TheoremII and its
  proof sketch, the Britton-lemma statement and proof sketch, and the
  Bass--Serre theorem. The rest of the survey was not audited. The
  original1949 HNN paper was located and its metadata checked.
- Doucha, https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.09448 : primary abstract only.
  This concerns universal actions of locally finite groups on metric
  spaces and linear isometries. It does not supply the arbitrary-input,
  fixed-ring algebraic extension proved here. Full text not audited.

Exact-problem and English/Russian searches for homogeneous group
representations, HNN extensions of representations, and amalgamation
in Rep-k did not reveal a prior solution. The search was limited and
some results were unrelated uses of the word homogeneous. Further
review should investigate older module and representation amalgamation
theorems. No claim that the construction is new is established by a
negative search alone.

Complete internally audited candidate affirmative answer. Add as the
