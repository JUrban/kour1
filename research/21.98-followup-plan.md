# 21.98: cyclic coverage follow-up

12 September 2026. The full multilinear-commutator question is unresolved
here. No additional complete candidate is claimed.

The finite-generation/FC reduction missing from the earlier triage has
now been located exactly in Cutolo--Nicotera2010, Lemma 1.1. It is a prior
theorem for every group word, not a new solution. After its finite torsion
subgroup is removed, the verbal subgroup is finitely generated free
abelian and has finite conjugation action. The remaining task is to
bound its rank by one for a multilinear commutator word. Conciseness
alone has not supplied that step.

An independent route may settle the locally finite-by-nilpotent case for
every word, using a faithful unipotent representation of a finitely
generated torsion-free nilpotent group. The Zariski closure is connected,
and a word map from a connected algebraic group cannot have its image
distributed among finitely many distinct one-dimensional unipotent
subgroups. This must be written carefully over Q so that the resulting
finitely generated rank-one subgroup is actually cyclic. Check the
precise nilpotent representation input and existing literature before
assigning even partial priority.

Also audit the newly retrieved full Poss1975 source for17.115. Its
constant-embedding results do not cover every locally free group.
