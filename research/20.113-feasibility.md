# 20.113: ANUPQ feasibility and a stopped class-eight probe

September 12, 2026. Neither subpart is resolved. This records a bounded
probe and distinguishes the installed runtime from the arithmetic limit
mentioned in the problem.

The installed ANUPQ is 3.3.3, dated November 25, 2025. Its change log
says 64-bit builds have been supported since 3.2.1; this is not a change
of its presentation workspace to arbitrary-width indices. In the installed
sources, `y_address` is an `int*`, the presentation and workspace offsets
in `struct pcp_vars` are `int`, and `WORD_LENGTH` is
8*sizeof(int)-1. Default packed generator/class fields and the optional
RUN_TIME field allocation are explicit in the storage headers and main.c.
GMP support for selected calculations does not widen every such field.
No vendor code was changed and no new 64-bit-index implementation is claimed.

The manual's `ClassBound` means lower exponent-p class. With an imposed
exponent-seven law this agrees with ordinary lower central class, so it
can be used directly for the H(7,c) family. It cannot be silently read
as an ordinary class bound for the exponent-eight family.

The probe uses the original four generators and relation
[b,a][d,c]^-1=1, Prime=7, Exponent=7, ClassBound=8, and a ten-million-word
pq workspace. GAP had a 3 GiB workspace ceiling, with an 8 GiB address
space limit per process and a 180-second bound on the entire process
group. Its progress log reports successive class quotients with log_7
orders 4,9,25,70,214,654,1978,6238. These are progress markers, not a
completed independently checked group certificate.

After the class-eight marker, the native pq child had terminated and
GAP remained busy. The probe did not instrument the return from Pq, so
the exact expensive GAP operation was not identified. At the specified
180-second limit the wrapper terminated the process group and collected
actual exit 1. Stderr was empty, but the final result and completion
sentinel were absent. The wrapper correctly records
`accepted_complete_probe=false`. The process is terminal and was not
restarted. No Schur multiplier was computed.

Vaughan-Lee's primary paper
[*Schur's exponent conjecture II*, arXiv:2111.11098v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.11098v3)
was obtained and cached. Its abstract, introduction, statements on pp.1–4,
and the short proof of Theorem 1 were read. It explicitly presents the
H(8,12) multiplier exponent 32 as an expected result requiring proof,
alongside calculations for two-generator covers. Those two-generator
calculations do not by themselves settle H(8,12) or H(7,13). The rest
of the 22-page proof was not audited; no theorem from it is used to
claim a new bound or completed subpart here.

Given this probe and the remaining research time, a large H(7,13)
calculation is deferred. The new full counterexample to 11.115 took
priority. The exact probe code, output, bound and actual exit record
are retained so this feasibility check need not be repeated unknowingly.
