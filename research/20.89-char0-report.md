## Mathematical result

In every characteristic-zero linear group G, the almost Engel elements
form a characteristic subgroup L. Moreover L has a finite characteristic
subgroup K with L/K locally nilpotent, and K is a common finite sink
for all elements of L relative to G. No finite-generation or solubility
assumption is made on G.

The key new step is an abstract lift: if every finitely generated
subgroup of X is finite-by-nilpotent, N is torsion-free and normal,
and X/N is finite-by-(locally nilpotent), then X is finite-by-(locally
nilpotent). The torsion subgroup in the preimage of a finite quotient
kernel injects into that finite kernel. Taking two sufficiently late
lower-central terms proves that the remaining quotient is locally
nilpotent. Choosing the quotient kernel characteristic preserves
normality under ambient conjugation.

The characteristic-zero Tits theorem and the unipotent radical of the
Zariski closure give the required torsion-free kernel and virtually
abelian quotient for L. The already justified virtually abelian
closure theorem is used before applying Shumyatsky's theorem for
all-almost-Engel linear groups. The final first-commutator argument
then proves closure relative to G without a circular all-elements
assumption. The positive-characteristic gap is stated explicitly.
