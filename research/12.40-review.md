# Problem 12.40: internal review

This is an internal mathematical audit, not an independent referee report.

1. The printed statement concerns the p-part of the degree, not the
   full degree, its p'-part, or an ordinary character. All constructed
   characters are irreducible Brauer characters in characteristic two.
2. Bray's Main Theorem applies with n=6>=3, p=2 dividing n, and q odd.
   The extracted text rendered >= as >; both relevant pages were
   viewed and the distinction is harmless here.
3. The heart dimension subtracts TWO: the constant line lies inside
   the augmentation kernel because the projective degree is even.
4. Irreducibility is absolute. The assertion is over an arbitrary
   characteristic-p field in the primary theorem, and its proof works
   after extension to an algebraic closure. Mere irreducibility over
   F2 would not by itself suffice to identify one Brauer character.
5. In the recalled proof, the ordinary permutation dot product has
   W-perpendicular equal to U, so its form on W/U is nondegenerate.
   The at-most-one-nontrivial-factor argument needs both the bottom
   and the top to be nontrivial, not just a dimension bound.
6. The group representation descends from SL to PSL because scalars
   fix all projective points. Passing to PSL subtracts one further
   factor of two from the SL order. For odd a, gcd(6,5^a-1)=2 exactly.
7. No exceptional small group occurs: n=6 and q>=5. Simplicity is a
   strengthening of the conclusion, not necessary for the inequality.
8. The field orders are powers of five throughout. A polynomial root
   modulo 2^k alone would not suffice without prime-power realization.
9. The exponent increment is 2^(k-2), not 2^k. Formula (3) verifies
   precisely the induced change in q. Since k>=3 the increment is
   even, so all exponents stay odd and the Sylow order stays fixed.
10. Exact valuation k uses the rejected lift b_k, whereas the selected
    lift a_(k+1) continues the induction. Confusing them reverses the
    desired congruence. Both are tested independently.
11. The argument requires arbitrarily large k, not an increasing list
    of q. Exact valuations nevertheless imply infinitely many distinct
    field orders among the witnesses.
12. The hypothetical bound need not be monotone or computable.
    Its single finite value at 8192 is already contradicted.
13. Known upper bounds on |G:O_p(G)|_p in terms of the largest degree
    p-part point in the opposite direction and do not settle this
    question. Cyclic-defect results do not apply to this family.
14. Primary source scope: the short Bray paper's mathematical text
    was read; pages 2 and 3 were viewed locally. Only the irreducibility
    assertion is imported. The cohomological exact-sequence argument
    is not required or claimed as an independently verified premise.
15. GAP checks on small modules are controls on the construction and
    dimensions. No GAP calculation is used to assert irreducibility
    for all q. Exact integer calculations similarly do not replace
    the lifting proof.
16. Searches for the problem number, Kondratiev, Brauer-degree p-parts,
    bounded Sylow orders and the PSL family did not locate an exact
    prior answer. Searches are not exhaustive; priority remains pending.

Disposition: the deduction has no identified mathematical gap.
The imported representation result is explicitly credited and checked
against its hypotheses. Complete candidate status is distinct from
new-result priority and independent review.
