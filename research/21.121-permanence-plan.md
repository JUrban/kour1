# Development checklist for exponent permanence

12 September 2026. The source comparison after the soluble and A5
packets suggested testing whether the characteristic construction
inside the p'-radical needs any solubility hypothesis.

1. Separate the two uses of the original Jordan bound: bound the
   index of O_(p')(G), then apply the bound again inside this finite
   p'-subgroup. This gives a characteristic subgroup with the original
   exponent and a changed constant.
2. Apply characteristic invariance to the intersection of a finite
   subgroup with a normal subgroup of the ambient group. Retain an
   explicit constant if negative exponents are allowed.
3. Check finite-index subgroups that are not normal by taking their
   core. Compare entire sets of admissible exponents, which also
   settles preservation of attainment.
4. Test quotienting by a finite normal p-subgroup using the already
   proved normal-Sylow estimate. Check the reverse direction on full
   preimages of finite subgroups.
5. Give a boundary example for replacing the p-kernel by an arbitrary
   finite kernel. Record exactly which primary statements and proofs
   were read, and archive the new source bytes and report.

These are supplemental structural results for 21.121, not a complete
answer to part (b) or an additional candidate count. The finite
controls of the imported elementary lemmas are already recorded;
do not rerun them unchanged or claim a new independent human review.
