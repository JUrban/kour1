# Problem 8.3: matrix p-group regularity literature update

Status: general problem unresolved here; no computation launched.
Checked 11 September 2026.

Kolesnikov and Leontiev,
[*One necessary condition for the regularity of a p-group and its
application to Wehrfritz's problem*](https://doi.org/10.33048/semi.2022.19.013),
Siberian Electronic Mathematical Reports 19:1 (2022), 138--163,
Theorem 2, proves irregularity of P_n(Z/p^m) when p is odd,
p is congruent to 1 modulo 3, n >= (p+2)/3, and m >= 3.
The primary PDF's abstract, introduction, theorem statements and beginning
of the proof of Theorem 1 were read. Theorem 2's proof has not been audited.
The PDF states publication on 5 March 2022; aggregator dates differed.

This strengthens the exclusions listed in the supplied Notebook. It does
not classify all triples. The same source notes that some classical
necessary conditions for regularity hold throughout a wider range, so
checking only those conditions cannot settle the remaining cases.

GAP's IsRegularPGroup implementation was inspected. Its elementary
sufficient conditions and its central-quotient recursion are available for
small examples, but the unexplored matrix groups grow quickly. The exact
order is

    p^(n^2*(m-1) + n*(n-1)/2).

This follows by counting independent entries: m-1 base-p digits on and
below the diagonal, m digits above. Every such matrix is invertible
because its reduction modulo p is upper unitriangular. A future search
should first isolate parameter triples outside the published positive and
negative ranges and establish a practical faithful representation.

Potency or a regular power structure must not be substituted for
regularity of a p-group. No such shortcut has been used.
