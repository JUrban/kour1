# A direct proof of the known square closedness criterion

This is an independent derivation of the criterion already proved by
Nuzhin (2023, 2026), not a new answer to 19.63. It supplies a reviewable
bridge from the arithmetic certificates for 19.62 to group closedness.
We use the standard integral Chevalley Lie lattice, its root-element
action, and the classification of reduced crystallographic root systems.

Let A be a carpet over a commutative ring K, and assume

    A_r^2 A_-r is contained in A_r for every root r.                 (1)

All Lie lattices below are tensored with K; their Chevalley bases remain
free K-bases, including in bad characteristic. Put

    M = sum_r A_r e_r + sum_r <A_r A_-r>_Z h_r.                      (2)

The second sum is in the Cartan part and the first sum has independent
root coordinates. In particular M intersect K e_r equals A_r e_r.

## Lie closure and invariance

The additive group M is a Lie subring. Brackets of two root summands
lie in the appropriate root summand by the (1,1) carpet condition,
or in the Cartan part for opposite roots. Cartan summands commute.
For a Cartan generator u v h_r and a root generator w e_s with
s different from r and -r, apply Jacobi to

    [[u e_r, v e_-r], w e_s].

Each inner bracket on the right side of Jacobi is zero or a root
summand, and a second application of the (1,1) carpet condition puts
each outer bracket in A_s e_s. For s=r or -r the required coefficient
is respectively 2uvw or -2uvw. It belongs to the appropriate subgroup
by polarization of (1):

    2uvw = (u+w)^2 v - u^2 v - w^2 v

when u,w belong to A_r and v belongs to A_-r, with the opposite-root
version for s=-r. No division by two is used. Thus (2) is closed under
all brackets. It is exactly the Lie subring generated by the A_r e_r,
because its Cartan summands are brackets of opposite root summands.

Every x_s(t), t in A_s, preserves M. It suffices to check its action
on the Lie generators u e_r. For r different from s and -s, the root
string action has coefficients C_(i,1), so every term belongs to M by
the carpet conditions. The vector e_s is fixed. On u e_-s the action is

    u e_-s + tu h_s - t^2 u e_s,

whose three terms belong to M by (1) and (2). The inverse generator
x_s(-t) gives equality. Hence the carpet group H preserves M.

## Detecting roots by a Cartan vector

Suppose a root p admits a root q with Cartan integer <p,q^vee>=±1.
For every generator x_s(u) of H, its action on h_q differs from h_q
by an integer multiple of u e_s, which lies in M. Invariance gives

    H h_q is contained in h_q + M.

If x_p(t) belongs to H, its action on h_q now shows ±t e_p belongs
to M. The root coordinate in (2) forces t in A_p.

Every root has such a q in simply laced irreducible systems of rank
at least two: move to a simple root and take an adjacent simple root.
For B_n, n>=3, use the usual coordinates. A short root e_1 pairs with
the coroot of e_1+e_2 with value one; a long root e_1+e_2 pairs with
the coroot of e_2+e_3 with value one. Weyl conjugacy within each length
covers all roots. The same choices work in F4. In G2 each length
class forms a hexagon, and another root of that length with angle
60 or 120 degrees gives value ±1. This covers the nonsymplectic
cases; B2 is isomorphic to C2.

## Symplectic roots without dividing by two

For C_n use its natural integral symplectic matrices, indexing rows
and columns by 1,...,n,-1,...,-n. The Chevalley Lie root vectors are

    e_(e_i-e_j) = E_(i,j) - E_(-j,-i),
    e_(e_i+e_j) = E_(i,-j) + E_(j,-i),
    e_(2e_i)    = E_(i,-i),

with the corresponding negative-root vectors and Cartan vectors
H_i=E_(i,i)-E_(-i,-i). They form a free K-basis of the symplectic Lie
algebra, so the root-coordinate assertion about M remains valid in
these matrices. Each root vector N satisfies N^2=0, and the natural
root matrix is I+tN, up to the harmless Chevalley basis signs.

Choose d_i in {0,1} and let D be the diagonal matrix with entries
d_i at i and 1-d_i at -i. For every root r,

    [D,e_r] = lambda_r e_r,

where lambda_(e_i-e_j)=d_i-d_j,
lambda_(e_i+e_j)=d_i+d_j-1, and lambda_(2e_i)=2d_i-1.
Negative roots have the negative coefficients. For any prescribed
root p one can choose the d_i so that lambda_p=±1. Moreover
e_r D e_r=0, because [D,e_r] is a scalar multiple of e_r and e_r^2=0.
Consequently

    (I+t e_r) D (I-t e_r) = D - t lambda_r e_r.                     (3)

For every initial root generator the difference in (3) lies in M.
Since H preserves M, every lifted word sends D into D+M. If the
group is defined in the natural symplectic representation, (3)
immediately detects every new root parameter, exactly as above.

The same argument works for any standard Chevalley isogeny form.
An equality of root words in that group gives the corresponding
equality of actions on the abstract Chevalley Lie algebra. Lift the
word to natural symplectic matrices. The quotient between this lift
and I+t e_p therefore centralizes the whole symplectic Lie algebra.
Such a matrix is scalar over every commutative ring: commuting with
each E_(i,-i) and E_(-i,i) first makes it scalar on each two-coordinate
block and zero between blocks; commuting with the short-root matrices
makes all block scalars equal. This uses entries equal to one, so
zero divisors cause no exception. Scalar matrices commute with D.
Thus the lifted word and I+t e_p have the same action on D. Equation
(3), with lambda_p=±1, puts t e_p in M and hence t in A_p.

All roots in every type are now detected, proving the criterion.

The already published all-type statement is Nuzhin, *Lie Rings and
Groups Defined by Carpets of Symplectic Type*, Siberian Math. J. 67(1)
(2026), 110–119, Theorem 2 (Russian original, p.136). Its full text
was read, and that theorem page was inspected as an image:
https://math-smz.ru/sites/math-smz.ru/files/2026-01/67-1-135.pdf .
The argument above makes its use in the 19.62 packet explicit and
retains only standard Chevalley identities as mathematical inputs.
