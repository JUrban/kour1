# Internal audit of the candidate for 18.76

Date: 2026-09-11. This is an internal audit, not an outside review or
a confirmed priority claim.

## Exact hypotheses

The supplied PDF's printed page 125 was inspected as an image. It
asks about an arbitrary division ring A, an arbitrary subgroup G of
its multiplicative group, and an extension with the multiplication
action on (A,+). No finite-dimensionality, commutativity, solvability,
normality of G in A*, or finite generation of the extension is required.
The accompanying finite-G positive result is not an extra hypothesis.

Our D and E are countable; G=F2 x F2 is finitely presented, torsion-free,
and centreless. These extra properties are not needed by the question.
The action is explicitly left multiplication. If the convention uses
right actions, translate through opposite groups and the opposite
division ring; the splitting question is unchanged.

## Checks of the construction

1. **Actual division ring.** Bi-orderability of free groups and the
   Mal'cev--Neumann embedding give a division ring containing the entire
   group algebra of F2 x F2, faithfully. This is a two-sided order,
   not merely a right order. Closing the group algebra under division
   inside this established division ring is legitimate and countable.
   We never assume an arbitrary noncommutative domain has fractions.
2. **Only the required commuting elements are used.** Elements from
   different factors commute, as do their sums and inverses when
   defined. The two generators inside either free factor do not
   commute. In the elimination, B0 A0^(-1) is always in that order.
   No scalar-commutative linear algebra is being imposed on D.
3. **The derivation is well-defined.** Its prefix formula is unchanged
   by inverse-pair deletion. For a negative b letter the contribution
   is minus the prefix times b^(-1), which is essential. Its values
   lie in kF, so they commute with the other free factor and its
   group algebra. No extension of the derivation to all of D is needed.
4. **The automorphisms exist on the full kernel.** Left multiplication
   by c or d is additive and invertible on all D. The derivation
   identity proves that gamma_d preserves the N multiplication. Its
   explicit inverse proves bijectivity. K is free, so there are no
   additional relations for these two automorphisms to satisfy.
5. **The extension is an actual group.** The iterated semidirect product
   constructs E before any twisted commutation relation is used. Its
   projection has exactly D_add as kernel and quotient F x K. The
   conjugation action on the kernel is x h times the kernel element,
   even for arbitrary nonzero kernel coordinates of a lift.
6. **Sign and identity conventions.** The natural lifts satisfy
   d_tilde b_tilde=(1_D in the additive kernel)b_tilde d_tilde. The
   additive kernel element 1_D is not the identity of E. Arbitrary
   commuting lifts therefore give B0 z-D0 v=1_D, with the sign printed
   in the proof. The other three cross relations have zero right side.
7. **Every possible section is covered.** A section's four generator
   images have arbitrary coordinates u,v,w,z in D. The first three
   commuting relations uniquely express v,w,z in terms of u, and
   force the fourth left side to vanish. Both inverted elements are
   nonzero by the faithful group-algebra inclusion. This contradiction
   holds in every characteristic, including 2, and for all of D,
   not just the finite-support group algebra used in computations.
8. **No irrelevant splitting is substituted.** E splits over N, and N
   splits over D_add with quotient F. These facts do not give a
   complement to D_add in E with quotient F x K. The latter is the
   particular extension the question asks about.

The cohomological paragraph is optional. The cellular degree-one
coboundary has a one-dimensional right-D kernel, hence rank 3 in D^4.
There are no 3-cells in the product-of-roses classifying space, giving
one-dimensional H^2. The elementary section proof already establishes
the nonzero extension class without any cohomology theorem.

## Exact computation and its limits

Run:

    python3 scripts/verify_18_76.py > results/18.76-controls.json
    bin/gap -q -b -T scripts/verify_18_76.g > results/18.76-gap-controls.log

The Python script computes in finite-support Z[F2 x F2] and in
F_p[F2 x F2] for p=2,3,5,7. The characteristic-zero controls use
integer coefficients, which include every coefficient occurring in
these checks. No formal series is truncated or numerically approximated.
In each of the five cases, the checks pass:

- 5,618 derivation identities on all ordered pairs of reduced words
  of length at most 3 in each factor;
- 10,922 checks of invariance under free reduction on all raw words
  of length at most 6, for both derivations;
- 2,400 generator-automorphism checks, including inverses and the
  homomorphism identity on N;
- 600 action-formula checks against letterwise composition of the
  actual automorphisms, also checking composition in K;
- 600 extension cases checking the explicit product against the
  semidirect construction, associativity, both inverses, identities,
  and the prescribed conjugation action;
- eight exact controls of the lift relations and noncommutativity.

The independent GAP script uses 4-by-4 Kronecker-product matrices over
Q and each of the 26 prime fields through 101. The two factors commute
with each other but are internally noncommutative. A0 and C0 are
invertible. In all 27 cases the first three block equations have rank
12, the full four-equation matrix has rank 12, and adjoining the
nonzero target in the last block raises rank to 13. GAP also checks
the correctly ordered relation, for q1,...,q4 the four left sides:

    q4 - B0 A0^(-1) q2 + D0 C0^(-1) B0 A0^(-1) q1
       - D0 C0^(-1) q3 = 0.

Matrix algebras are not division rings, and these representations are
not faithful representations of F2 x F2. They are controls of the
elimination identity, not finite counterexamples to the question.
The actual counterexample rests on the division-ring theorem and the
written construction. The first GAP invocation passed with global
variable syntax warnings; a function with local declarations removes
the warnings without changing the matrices or proof.

## Primary references and novelty scope

- Jacques Sakarovitch, *Rational and recognisable series*, author-hosted
  draft chapter of the Handbook of Weighted Automata, dated 20 April
  2009: https://perso.telecom-paristech.fr/jsaka/PUB/Files/HBWA.pdf .
  Read Definition 28, Theorems 21 and 22, their immediate application,
  and the historical notes on those results. These supply exactly the
  free-group order and series-division-ring ingredients; here
  “product” in Theorem 21 is a direct product of free groups. The rest
  of the chapter was not fully read. Cached PDF and text use the
  prefix `references/cache/sakarovitch-weighted-automata`.
- George M. Bergman, Journal of Algebra 535 (2019), 503–540,
  DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2019.06.021, author-hosted final preprint:
  https://math.berkeley.edu/~gbergman/papers/kG_in_D.pdf . Read Section
  1's background on the two-sided order theorem, the included group
  algebra, and the distinction from right-orderability. The later
  embedding machinery was not audited or used. Cached PDF/text prefix:
  `references/cache/bergman-division-rings-2019`.
- B. H. Neumann, *On ordered division rings*, Transactions AMS 66
  (1949), 202–252, DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1949-0032593-5. Metadata
  checked; the publisher PDF returned 403. The original proof was
  not read. This is distinct from his 1949 paper *On Ordered Groups*.

Exact-number, English, and Russian searches involving Palyutin,
division-ring additive extensions, splitting, free groups, and the
Notebook found the stated question but no prior exact resolution.
General nonsplit-extension and division-ring papers appeared; their
full contents have not all been audited. The literature search does
not establish that the example is new. In particular, future review
should check older group-cohomology treatments of division closures
and nonsplit extensions with fixed-point-free actions.

Complete internally checked candidate negative answer to 18.76.
