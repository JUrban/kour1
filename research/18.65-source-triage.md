# Problem 18.65: source and unresolved reduction

12 September 2026. No new result; general conjecture unresolved here.

The exact printed question is Conjecture1.3 of Guralnick--Malle,
*Variations on the Baer--Suzuki theorem*: if p!=5 and every commutator
of two members of a p-element conjugacy class is a p-element, must
the class lie in O_p(G)? Theorem1.2 of the same paper instead assumes
a normal set closed under taking commutators. Merely having p-element
commutators does not supply that stronger closure condition.

The author PDF https://agag-malle.math.rptu.de/~malle/download/baersuz.pdf
is dated22 June2014 (arXiv:1310.5909 initially2013), published in
Math. Z.(2015), DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-014-1399-y.
It is cached as `references/cache/guralnick-malle-2013-baer-suzuki.pdf`
and layout text; the filename's year is not the version date.
The introduction on pp.1--2 and the first portion of p.3 were read;
the displayed definitions, reduction discussion, and Conjecture1.3
on p.2 were visually checked. Theorem4.2 around pp.5--6 and the
counting argument in Section6 on p.22 and the start of p.23 were
also inspected in text. The full paper and all classification inputs
have not been independently read or checked.

The introduction asserts that the conjecture reduces to the small
Ree groups ^2G2(q^2), q^2=3^(2a+1)>27, with p a primitive prime
divisor of q^2+sqrt(3)q+1. It states the positive cases p=2,3,7.
The full reduction for this weaker conjecture is not separately
proved there, so it remains an imported author assertion if used.

A possible pigeonhole strategy uses the doubly transitive Ree action.
If two permutations send the same ordered pair (alpha,beta) to the
same (beta,gamma), their quotient relation forces their commutator
to fix a point. Anisotropic torus elements are derangements, so a
nontrivial such commutator could violate the p-element condition.
The large-torus class size is too small for the direct count against
the ordered-pair degree bound. No argument overcoming that obstruction
was found. This is a lead, not a proved new reduction or theorem.

The2025 article *Weakly subnormal subgroups and variations of
Baer--Suzuki*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70057, was also inspected
in primary publisher HTML for its stated conjecture and some structural
cases. Its hypothesis on [x,g] for every g in G is different from
commutators with conjugates of x. No full PDF was read and no result
from it is imported here. It does not supply a resolution of18.65.
