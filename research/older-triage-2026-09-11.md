# Older-issue triage,11September2026

These are research leads and scope checks, not additional solutions.
The successful lead in this pass is11.116, now written separately with
a complete candidate affirmative proof and an internal review.

- **9.45:** The rational overlattice Za+Z^n has cyclic quotient over Z^n.
  Its dual is the congruence lattice {z in Z^n : r.z=0 mod m} when a=r/m.
  Orthogonal integer bases are not limited to coordinate axes: primitive
  Pythagorean two-dimensional frames already give nontrivial examples.
  A finite basis enumeration would be an algorithm but does not yet supply
  the useful coordinate characterization requested. No candidate counted.
- **9.76:** The centralizer of a nilpotent element in an infinite-dimensional
  nil algebra can be infinite-dimensional; this alone does not establish
  that its intersection with the finitely generated adjoint Golod subgroup
  is infinite. Passing from algebra centralizers to group centralizers is
  the unresolved step. The earlier finite-center construction21.132 does
  not resolve it.
- **9.72:** Enlarging the ambient topological group without requiring it
  to be generated by the two indicated Lie subgroups makes a superficial
  counterexample possible. The intended amalgam condition needs a source
  audit before this observation could be treated as meaningful. No such
  formulation loophole is counted as a new resolution.
- **10.13:** Ordinary uniform small-cancellation constructions do not
  immediately provide an independent set of relators with the requested
  full exponential rate3. No massiveness proof was obtained.
- **10.50:** Cyclic induction gives an explicit integral character lattice,
  but restating the desired property as a lattice-index condition is not
  yet a structural classification. Simple abelian tests must distinguish
  elementary abelian groups of different ranks and primes. No classification
  or computational catalogue was claimed.
- **10.71:** Existing finite-subgroup centralizer results do not directly
  cover the centralizer of an arbitrary infinite-order automorphism or of
  an arbitrary finite set of automorphisms. No broad finiteness conclusion
  was imported from that special case.
- **11.5:** Taken with completely arbitrary coefficient rings, familiar
  nonfree projective modules over Dedekind domains create an apparent
  formulation issue. This needs checking against the original intended
  coefficient assumptions before counting any elementary ring example.
- **11.124:** A proposed recovery of R from its derived subgroup inside a
  free group needs a proof, especially for infinitely generated R. Arguments
  using finite core graphs or finite-index commensurators do not by themselves
  cover the stated arbitrary subgroup. No normality theorem established.

For11.116, the decisive observation was to encode every coset of every
quasicyclic subgroup in one common interval ordering, then retain every
fiber of every subgroup in the finitely many cosets of the normal divisible
subgroup. This avoids any need to choose compatible realizers separately
for a sequence of finite subgroup lattices.
