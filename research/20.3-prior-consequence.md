# Problem 20.3: negative consequence of a prior theorem

12 September 2026. **Prior negative consequence; excluded from the new-candidate count.**

The closure numbers of nonalternating finite simple groups are unbounded.
In particular, the family PSL_n(3), with odd n at least five, has closure
number at least n. These are nonalternating simple groups (the standard
simplicity theorem and exclusions of exceptional alternating/classical
isomorphisms are used here). Thus no fixed k works in Problem 20.3.

The relevant prior result is Gong--Yang--Zeng, *Total 3-closure for
projective special linear groups*, [arXiv:2608.17878v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17878),
submitted 18 August 2026. **Theorem 5.3**, PDF pp.21--22, states the stronger
local matching assertion for every k<n, despite the paper's focus on
3-closure. Its inclusion of a proper general linear overgroup in the
k-closure directly gives the lower bound. The paper explicitly identifies
20.2; the inference to 20.3 is recorded here as a consequence of its prior
theorem, not as a new solution.

Here is the elementary specialization, also considered locally before
finding Theorem 5.3. For odd n, SL_n(3) has trivial centre and equals
PSL_n(3). Let it act faithfully on the nonzero vectors of F_3^n, and
let A be any invertible matrix of determinant -1. Given k<n vectors,
their span W has dimension less than n. Extend a basis of W to a basis
of the whole vector space, and define D to be the identity on W and
all but one complementary basis vector, negating that remaining vector.
Then det(D)=-1, so AD is in SL_n(3), and ADv=Av on the entire tuple.
Thus A is in the k-closure for every k<n. It is not in the original
permutation group: equality on all nonzero vectors would force equality
of the linear maps, contradicting their determinants. Consequently
SL_n(3) fails total k-closure for every k<n.

actions of finite nonabelian simple groups*,
