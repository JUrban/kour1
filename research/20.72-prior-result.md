# Problem 20.72: prior affirmative theorem

12 September 2026. Not a new candidate resolution.
The original question on printed/PDF page 157 was read and viewed;
`references/cache/20.72-notebook-p157.png` retains that observation.

The variety defined by exponent dividing four, the metabelian identity,
and nilpotency class at most four has an outer category automorphism.
This is the direct content of Fernandes--Tsurkov,
[arXiv:1909.05955v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.05955v1), Theorem 9.1:
the automorphism group of its category of finite-rank free groups,
modulo inner automorphisms, has order two.

The exact defining identities on page 4 and theorem proof on pages
29–30 were read and viewed. The intermediate calculations constructing
and classifying the word operations were not fully audited, so the
existence theorem is imported, not independently proved here. The
source, images, and reading limits are bound into the 15.76 packet;
see [the shared source audit](15.76-sources.md).

This also prevents extending the positive S_d result of 15.76 to every
proper metabelian subvariety. It does not contradict that result:
the affine semidirect products used there have unrestricted exponent.
