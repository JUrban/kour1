# Problem14.26: internal mathematical review

This is an internal audit, not an independent referee report.

1. **Target convention.** Budkin1999 p258 explicitly uses finite
   support. The positive result answers that convention. The earlier
   unrestricted-wreath obstruction is irrelevant to this target.
2. **Class bound.** C includes all nilpotency classes. The model's
   bound c(cm+1)+1 depends on the finite subset and its coordinate
   representation. There is no claim of closure at fixed class.
3. **Finite algebra.** The ordinary degree cutoff makes A finite
   dimensional and kills no additional degree at most c. All defining
   relations are homogeneous of ordinary degree two.
4. **Derivation stability.** Differentiating the entire polynomial
   f(z)f(z+d) gives D applied to it. Coefficients of that derivative
   lie in the span of the same defining coefficients. The Leibniz
   rule extends stability from generators to their ideal.
5. **Nilpotent derivation.** Index sum bounds D on the free truncated
   algebra before taking the quotient. This proves its nilpotence
   on the quotient without asserting a quotient index grading.
6. **Shift automorphism.** The exponential is finite and the iterated
   Leibniz formula proves multiplicativity. Positive, negative and
   zero integer shifts all work. No convergence issue enters.
7. **Orthogonality.** Positive differences1,...,R are imposed;
   commutativity gives negative differences. Difference zero is
   deliberately excluded. Nonzero powers at one position survive.
8. **Duality.** Arbitrary symmetric polynomials of degree at most m
   in each variable correspond exactly to linear functionals on
   Sym^k(V), by the factorial coefficient formula. No missing k!
   factor occurs because the variables label the ordered factors.
9. **Whole ideal.** Vanishing with two specialized arguments kills
   every relation multiplied by arbitrary k-2 factors: f(z) spans V
   and coefficient extraction gives the defining relation vectors.
   It is not merely vanishing on a few integer evaluations.
10. **Separation.** Delta_k has nonzero constant diagonal value.
    The Lagrange factor uses the average of k variables, permissible
    over Q, and adds at most M-1 to each variable's degree. This
    proves simultaneous linear independence for every1<=k<=c.
11. **No false grading of the base.** Arbitrary nilpotent bases need
    not be graded. They first embed into ordinary unitriangular
    matrices; superdiagonal distances provide the additive exponents.
12. **Cross-products.** For different nearby positions, the products
    of the two strictly upper matrix parts vanish in both orders.
    Consequently products of finitely many copies are precisely sums
    of their strictly upper parts, with no surviving cross terms.
13. **Injectivity.** At each matrix entry, the independent f(i)^(b-a)
    recover all scalar coordinates. This is stronger than merely
    knowing each rho_i is injective.
14. **Nilpotence of H.** An extension of nilpotent groups need not
    be nilpotent. Here an actual H-central series is supplied by
    refining each superdiagonal factor with powers of alpha-1.
    U acts trivially on each original superdiagonal factor; t moves
    each refinement down one step. This verifies the needed stronger
    condition explicitly.
15. **Normality and endpoints.** The refinements are inverse images
    under homomorphisms U_j->U_j/U_(j+1); they are H-normal because
    U acts trivially and alpha preserves each delta-power image.
    The last image is zero, giving U_(j+1), and H/U is abelian.
16. **Torsion.** Projection to the infinite cyclic quotient eliminates
    its exponent. A first nonzero superdiagonal in the kernel cannot
    vanish under a nonzero integer multiple. Zero divisors in A do
    not affect this additive characteristic-zero argument.
17. **Embedding input.** Jennings Theorem5.2 supplies a faithful
    finite augmentation quotient for the finitely generated base.
    The passage to a unitriangular representation is explained in
    the proof. Earlier augmentation separation is an imported
    classical theorem; it has not been independently reproved.
18. **Finite support window.** I includes all supports of S and every
    shifted support needed for an actual product relation inside S.
    Hence all rearrangements used in that relation occur among
    commuting copies. A global homomorphism is neither needed nor
    asserted.
19. **Identity and empty supports.** The construction maps1 to1.
    If every support is empty, I={0} and the infinite cyclic
    projection alone separates S. c may be increased to1 for a
    trivial base representation.
20. **Quasivariety reduction.** A solution separating each individual
    pair exists by the definition of generated quasiidentities.
    A finite product combines these solutions; this uses an actual
    closure property of C. It does not assume an embedding into a
    single original nilpotent group without proof.
21. **Replacing coordinates.** The finite base table contains both
    factors and products for every required coordinate multiplication.
    Injectivity preserves identities, nonidentities, and differing
    coordinates. Thus it gives an injective partial-table map before
    the nilpotent-base construction is applied.
22. **Word evaluations.** The converse local criterion includes
    intermediate products, inverse values and1 for the finitely
    many words in a quasiidentity. It therefore checks arbitrary
    quasiidentities, not only equations of length two.
23. **Controls.** Exact rational row reduction tests the whole
    truncated defining ideal in four small algebras. Independently
    expanded interpolation polynomials test annihilation and
    separation. An independent Heisenberg-triple implementation
    supplies a37-element wreath table with319 relations, including
    noncommuting products, inverses, cancellations and shifts.
24. **Limits of evidence.** These finite tests do not prove the
    parameter-uniform theorem. Lemmas2–3 and the finite-table argument
    do. Limited source searches do not establish novelty. No outside
    referee or independent-agent review has occurred.

Disposition: no mathematical gap identified. Complete affirmative
candidate, with independent review and priority still pending.
