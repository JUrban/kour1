## 21.70 and 21.71

Re-read the existing integral-finiteness reductions and source audit.
The required integral FP hypothesis remains missing. Ian Leary's
primary abstract for *The Euler class of a Poincare duality group*
([Southampton repository](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/29837/), 2002)

an example that is FL over two fields with different Euler
characteristics. Only the primary abstract and bibliographic record
were inspected in this follow-up; no full-proof audit is claimed.

## 21.148

The rational-subspace analogy settles neither arbitrary relatively
convex subgroups nor their joins. Even when g and h lie in two chosen
subgroups, replacing a relatively convex join by the subgroup generated
by them is unjustified. The prior negative answer to 21.147 explicitly
warns against that substitution. No proof that distributivity forces
the whole lattice to be a chain was obtained. Statements for a fixed
ordering, whose convex subgroups already form a chain, do not answer
the question about all right-relatively convex subgroups.

## 20.89

The characteristic-zero torsion-free lifting step cannot simply be
used for an infinite unipotent subgroup in characteristic p. Also,
being Engel on an abelian normal subgroup alone does not control
commutator orbits starting in other cosets. The existing S_3-module
obstruction remains relevant. No virtually soluble theorem in full
positive-characteristic generality was established in this triage.

There is, however, a short further sufficient condition: uniformly
bounded orders of finite subgroups. The local finite-by-nilpotent
property makes the torsion elements of the generated subgroup locally
finite; the bound then makes that torsion subgroup finite. The detailed
proof and the virtually torsion-free corollary are in
`20.89-bounded-torsion-proof.md`. They are partial coverage, with no
priority or complete-count claim.
