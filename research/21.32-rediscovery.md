# 21.32: an elementary bounded-integral construction (not a new resolution)

**Status:** independent argument found during the session, then excluded from the new-solution count after locating Kalithasan–Thomas, arXiv:2602.18829v1, *On the decidability of the integrability of finite groups* (21 February 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.18829 . Their paper already proves decidability with a quantitative bound. The supplied September 2026 Notebook still has an unstarred entry. Retain this argument as a potentially useful alternative proof, not a new solution to the Notebook.

## Construction

Let G be finite of order n>1, and suppose H'=G. Identify G with that derived subgroup. There are at most floor(log_2 n) commutator values that generate G: greedily choose a commutator outside the subgroup so far generated, and each choice at least doubles its order. Keep only the elements appearing in those commutators. The resulting subgroup H_0 still has derived subgroup G and has a generating list h_1,...,h_d with d <= 2 floor(log_2 n).

Put a=|Aut(G)|, b=exp Z(G), and M=ab. For h,k in H_0, h^a centralizes G because the conjugation action has image in Aut(G). The centralizer C=C_{H_0}(G) is normal, so [h^a,k] belongs to C intersect G=Z(G). Since h^a centralizes G, the commutator power identity gives

    [h^(ab),k] = [h^a,k]^b = 1.

Thus every M-th power in H_0 is central.

Let q:Z^d -> H_0/G map e_i to h_iG. It is a surjective homomorphism, since H_0/G is abelian. Form the pullback

    E = {(h,v) in H_0 x Z^d : hG=q(v)}.

Projection E -> H_0 is surjective; its restriction to E' is an isomorphism onto G. Indeed E' has zero second coordinate, projection is injective on the zero-second-coordinate subgroup, and surjectivity of E -> H_0 implies surjectivity of E' -> H_0'=G. Hence E'=G x {0}. The quotient E/(G x {0}) is Z^d.

Let x_i=(h_i,e_i). The elements x_i^M=(h_i^M,M e_i) are central in E. Their generated subgroup L projects isomorphically onto MZ^d, since the vectors M e_i are independent. In particular, L intersects G x {0} trivially. Consequently Q=E/L satisfies

    Q' is isomorphic to G,
    |Q| = n M^d <= n (|Aut(G)| exp Z(G))^(2 floor(log_2 n)).

This produces a finite integral with an explicit order bound, even if the original H was infinite. The trivial group has the trivial group as an integral.

## Effective bound and algorithm

An automorphism of a finite n-element group is determined by the images of a generating set of size at most log_2 n; hence |Aut(G)| <= n^(log_2 n). Also exp Z(G) <= n. Therefore a uniform coarse bound is

    |Q| <= n^(1 + 2(log_2 n)^2 + 2 log_2 n).

Enumerate all multiplication tables through this bound, retain groups, compute their derived subgroups, and check isomorphism to G by finite bijection search. This is a terminating decision procedure. No practical efficiency or optimal complexity is claimed.

The new preprint gives a different bound involving the maximum size of a minimal generating set and the rank of Z(G), using cohomological methods. A systematic comparison and any complexity improvement would be separate work; the basic decidability conclusion is already known.
