# Problem 19.62: finite derived-carpet screen

12 September 2026. Bounded result only; no new complete candidate.

Every derived carpet obtained from the following 193,805 original carpets
is closed in the saved matrix model. There are 6,999 distinct derived
carpets when types and rings are counted separately.

| Type | Ring | Original carpets | Distinct derived carpets |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| C2 | F2 | 64 | 18 |
| C2 | F4 | 2,515 | 171 |
| C2 | F2[t]/(t^2) | 6,717 | 244 |
| C2 | F2 x F2 | 6,915 | 341 |
| C2 | Z/4 | 1,398 | 114 |
| G2 | F2 | 174 | 39 |
| G2 | F3 | 217 | 52 |
| G2 | F4 | 10,479 | 615 |
| G2 | F2[t]/(t^2) | 59,921 | 1,457 |
| G2 | F2 x F2 | 47,345 | 1,589 |
| G2 | Z/4 | 17,955 | 963 |
| G2 | F9 | 40,105 | 1,396 |

For each original carpet and destination root, take the union of all
root-commutator coefficient sets from the frozen implication tables,
then its additive span. This is precisely the definition of the derived
subgroup at that root: the tables enumerate all parameters in every
source subgroup, not merely additive generators. The span lookup is
independently compared with repeated addition for every possible input
mask. Every resulting assignment occurs in the complete saved carpet
list. None occurs among the assignments that acquire new root elements
in the completed 19.61 closure searches. Original packet hashes, including
the four-shard F9 completion, are verified by the new auditor.

An additional direct scalar check verifies B_r^2 B_-r contained in B_r
for every root of every distinct derived carpet. Here B_r^2 means
pointwise squares. This supplies a useful symbolic target but does not
establish it beyond the listed finite rings. Z/9 is not included in this
screen; its separate packet already proves all original carpets closed.

The definition and the characteristic restrictions in the known partial
answer were checked against Nuzhin, *On the Closedness of Carpets of
Additive Subgroups Associated With a Chevalley Group Over a Commutative
Ring*, JSFU 16(6) (2023), 732–737. The full article text was read; printed
pages 732 and 736 were also viewed as images. The Lie-ring input cited
from his earlier work remains imported and is not re-proved here. His
Theorem 1 supplies the sufficient square condition for C2 and G2. The
finite screen's closedness conclusion instead uses the already completed
matrix searches. Primary source:
https://journal.sfu-kras.ru/sites/journal.sfu-kras.ru/files/fulltexts/J_MATH_2023_6_total2.pdf
