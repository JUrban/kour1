# Problem 18.84: bounded character-table screen

12 September 2026. No solution or counterexample found; no candidate count.

The table pilot tested14 minimal prime sets across11 named table pairs.
The exceptional follow-up checked F4(2), G2(3), and O8+(2).3 in their
indicated extensions. The broader catalogue inspected78 table pairs
and87 prime-set tests, with87 missing or class-capped entries logged.
Tables with more than150 classes in either member were skipped.
The78 pairs are distinct catalogue identifiers, not necessarily78
distinct simple isomorphism types: the catalogue has alternative
identifiers for some groups occurring as maximal subgroups elsewhere.

No test supplied the sufficient positive criterion for the ambient
BS_pi property. In every catalogue test, a nontrivial ambient involution
class remained whose square had only pi-element support. For involutions
this is a genuine pairwise pi-subgroup obstruction, since two involutions
generate a dihedral group. In an almost simple ambient group with a
socle that is not a pi-group, O_pi(G)=1: a normal pi-subgroup intersects
the socle trivially and centralizes it, hence is trivial. Conditional
on the table identifications and almost simple extension metadata,
such an involution class therefore also obstructs the ambient BS_pi
property. Thus none of these involution tests gives the desired pair.

The exceptional test of order-three classes is weaker. Class-square
support consisting of pi-elements does not show that every pair
generates a pi-subgroup; no such inference is made for G2(3) or the
triality classes. The Guralnick--Malle classification of some cross-pairs
of3-elements explains why graph fusion needs separate care; it is not
a validation of every subgroup claim suggested by character products.

These are exploratory computations with the GAP character-table library.
There has been no independent reconstruction of its tables or of the
normal embeddings. The results exclude a bounded route conditional on
those inputs; they do not establish a general theorem about18.84.
The exact algorithm and its sufficient criterion are in `18.84-plan.md`.
