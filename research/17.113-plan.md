# 17.113: audit of a prior affirmative answer

2026-09-12. The problem asks for two-generator finite p-groups of
deficiency zero and unbounded nilpotency class, for each p>3. Definition
8.12 requires an ordinary finite balanced presentation.

ProofForum PF:2026.000002 v1 predates this research session and gives
an explicit two-relator metacyclic family. The task is to audit and
record that answer, with no increase in the new-candidate count.

1. Preserve the primary PDF and TeX, download metadata and hashes.
2. Check collection, a faithful finite model, both generator orders,
   the complete lower central series and deficiency independently.
3. Compute finite p-quotients from the two relators in GAP; compare
   their orders, class, abelianization and generator powers with the
   proved formulas. Keep the universal proof distinct from controls.
4. Implement the central quotient in integer coordinates, and check
   its projection and defining relations independently of GAP.
5. Record the precise reading scope, prior attribution, actual process
   outcomes and replay instructions; commit locally.
