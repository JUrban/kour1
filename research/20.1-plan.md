# Problem 20.1: coprime direct products

12 September 2026. New candidate route, under audit.

The printed question asks for an insoluble finite totally2-closed group
with nontrivial Fitting subgroup. No direct-indecomposability restriction
appears. The known theorem of Arezoomand--Iranmanesh--Praeger--Tracey
states that J1 is totally2-closed. Try J1 x C13, since13 does not
divide |J1|=175560.

Prove that total2-closure is preserved under direct products of coprime
finite groups. In any faithful A x B action, the A-orbit and B-orbit
of a point meet only in that point: if alpha^a=alpha^b, then ab^-1
fixes alpha, and its cyclic subgroup contains both a and b by coprimeness.
The action of A on the set of B-orbits is therefore faithful, and
likewise with A,B interchanged. Each orbit equivalence relation is a
union of G-orbitals, hence preserved by every element of the2-closure.
On each quotient, such an element satisfies the pair criterion for
the factor's2-closure. Total2-closure of the factors gives one a and
one b globally; the singleton intersections then identify the original
permutation with ab.

This appears to settle the question directly, with Fitting subgroup
exactly C13. It bypasses an earlier considered computation for the
exceptional group SmallGroup(1440,4594), whose four core-free orbit
types also suggest a pairwise-action reduction. No computation on
that exceptional group has been started or is needed for this route.
