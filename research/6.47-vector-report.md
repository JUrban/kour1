## Completeness of the finite search

Let F be the group of binary word functions on G, generated by the coordinate
projections x,y. Restriction to automorphism-orbit representatives of G² is
injective: word evaluation commutes with every automorphism. The stored
transporters explicitly reconstruct every pair value. The original pilot
proves that the normalized operations, those with w(x,1)=x and w(1,y)=y,
are exactly xyF'. In particular, |F|=exp(G)²|F'|. Here that order is derived
from the proof, rather than computed by GAP's direct-product machinery.

Write c=[x,y]. The subgroup F' is the normal closure of c in F. Starting
from its identity, enumerate the orbit under right multiplication by c
and conjugation by x and y. Each operation preserves F'. A completed
finite orbit S is invariant under the inverses of these three permutations
as well: an injective self-map of a finite set is surjective. Conjugating
right multiplication by c by the conjugation permutations produces right
multiplication by every F-conjugate of c. Thus S is invariant under right
multiplication by generators and inverse generators of F'. Since 1 belongs
to S, F' is contained in S. Conversely, reachability gives S contained in F'.

The NumPy implementation evaluates the three moves coordinate by coordinate.
It records every distinct vector and its predecessor. The separate scalar
replay checks every predecessor edge and all three outgoing edges at every
node. These twelve complete certificates have 9,888 closure edges and 3,284
nonroot reachability edges. The cap is 4,096 vectors; all twelve finish below
it. A capped search certifies only its distinct reachable nodes and does not
claim closure or completeness.

## Independent controls and provenance

Before the new cases, the new implementation reproduced the full sets of
normalized word vectors and all associative multiplication tables in all
26 frozen GAP cases. Those cases contain 911 functions and 98 associative
operations. Each regression case also forces a cutoff one node below the
known derived order and checks that the result remains explicitly incomplete.
The final regression process exited zero with empty stderr. After the first
successful regression, the unsigned-byte conversion was changed to subtract
one in a signed integer array before conversion, supporting order 256 without
overflow. The final regression was then repeated successfully.

For the twelve new cases, the existing independent table checker tests all
856,686,592 associativity triples and 43,008 complete transporter permutations.
It also verifies unit, inverse, and every power up to the exponent for each
operation. All original and opposite operations are present. Every GAP input
export exits zero with a clean log and PASS_647_INPUT. The outer runner exits
zero with PASS_647_VECTOR_RUN. The exceptional-isomorphism GAP process exits
zero with a clean log and PASS_647_EXCEPTIONAL_GAP. Its explicit maps are
checked by scalar table arithmetic, so no trust in IdGroup is needed to
deduce isomorphism.

The packet auditor verifies all hashes, reconstructs each normalized export
from its certificate, reruns the scalar closure verifier and complete table
checks, repeats the 26-case regression, verifies the exceptional maps, and
audits the earlier frozen extension and pilot packets. The new packet has
separate names; none of the earlier timeout records or bounded-search results
has been overwritten.

The combined completed search now covers 38 distinct groups, 4,207 normalized
operations, 178 associative operations and 876,136,474 associativity triples.
The earlier capped (24,3) case remains outside this coverage. These finite
checks neither establish the general conjecture nor exclude larger examples.
