# Problem 6.47: direct coordinate enumeration

11 September 2026. New implementation plan; no added coverage yet.

The first GAP extension stalled after computing automorphism orbits,
while forming the word-function group inside a large direct product.
Avoid that representation: retain the small group table, representatives
and transporters, and perform coordinate operations directly in Python.
All existing GAP exports, failed runs and frozen packets stay intact.

Let x,y denote the two coordinate projections in the finite group F
of binary word functions, and let c=[x,y]. Its derived subgroup D is
the normal closure of c. Enumerate, from the identity vector, the
orbit under the following three permutations of D:

    d -> d c,       d -> x^-1 d x,       d -> y^-1 d y.

This orbit is precisely D. Each transition preserves D. Conversely,
conjugations by x and y preserve the completed finite orbit in both
directions; conjugating right multiplication by c yields right
multiplication by every F-conjugate of c. Their inverses also preserve
the finite orbit. The subgroup they generate is D, so the orbit
contains every element of D. A breadth-first search therefore gives
either all of D or a certified lower bound exceeding the chosen cap.

Each transition acts independently on the representative coordinates.
Precompute, for each coordinate, its three permutations of G. Store
vectors as byte strings and retain predecessor/transition pairs for
reachability. Check the three outgoing edges from every node when
complete. No generated permutation group on a large direct product
is needed. The normalized coset is xyD as in the original pilot.

1. Replay all 26 completed GAP cases and compare the entire unordered
   coset of vectors, then compare all associative output tables.
2. Export only group/orbit data for the 12 timed-out cases, with one
   bounded 4-GiB GAP process at a time beside the six n=7 verifiers.
3. Enumerate D and check all normalized operations using the existing
   table verifier and explicit variety-membership certificates.
4. Freeze actual exits, clean logs, completion sentinels, inputs,
   enumeration certificates, replay outputs and all omissions.

The new implementation must not inherit completeness merely from
matching counts. Full vector sets and the closure/reachability
certificates will be checked. Any capped enumeration remains incomplete.
