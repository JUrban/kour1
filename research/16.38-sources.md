## Closely related factorization literature

S. Franciosi and F. de Giovanni, *On periodic subgroups of factorized
groups*, Archiv der Mathematik 61 (1993), 313–318,
[primary PDF](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/BF01201446.pdf).
All six pages of extracted mathematical text were read; pages 313 and
315 were also viewed. The PDF has some OCR errors, including the first
author's initial, which was checked visually. The article studies
triply factorized groups G=AB=AK=BK with locally nilpotent factors.
Corollary 6 cites the known periodicity theorem for the group G=AB.
The paper does not state our assertion for arbitrary subgroups in a
set product. Its cited structural theorems and all internal details are
not independently reconstructed here, and none is used as a premise.
An attempted download from the author's numbered publication directory
returned 404; the publisher PDF was then obtained successfully.

B. Amberg, *Some Results and Problems about Factorized Groups*, in
*Infinite Groups 1994*, de Gruyter, 1996 (article copyright 1995),
[publisher preview](https://api.pageplace.de/preview/DT0400.9783110810387_A34803000/preview-9783110810387_A34803000.pdf).
The cache uses the conference-year name
`amberg-1994-factorized-survey-preview.pdf/.txt`. Mathematical pages
1–9 and the beginning of page 10 were read; page 3, PDF page 21,
was viewed. Theorem 2.3 records finiteness results for groups already
factorized as AB, including periodicity in the hyperabelian-by-finite
case. Section 3 explains the established relationship with modules and
triply factorized groups. Those general ideas are prior; the local
cocycle/orbit proof here is supplied in full. The remainder of the
survey and the proofs it cites were not fully audited.

The 1992 monograph *Products of Groups* by Amberg, Franciosi and de
Giovanni was identified through these primary references, including
Theorem 3.2.6 on soluble products of periodic groups. Its full text was
not obtained or read. No claim is made about the absence of an
equivalent intermediate lemma there.

The author's publication list was inspected at
[the primary site](https://www.advgrouptheory.com/degiovan/Pubblicazioni/IndexPub1.html)
and cached. The 1993 entry has no PDF link. Search results for later
mutually permutable factorizations were leads only; their full proofs
were not used.

## Dependencies and distinction from 9.65

The candidate proof uses Schreier's elementary finite-index generation
theorem, rationalization of torsion-free abelian groups, and averaging
over a finite group. It supplies the periodic-soluble local-finiteness
argument, the cocycle averaging calculation, and the entire induction.
No deep structural theorem from the cited factorization papers is
imported. The proof requires a finite derived length for the ambient
group; it does not answer the general locally soluble question 9.65.
