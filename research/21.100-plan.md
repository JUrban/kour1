# Problem21.100: coprime involutions on finite3-groups

2026-09-12. Exploratory counterexample search; no conclusion yet.

The question asks whether the number of A-invariant irreducible
characters of G nonzero everywhere on C_G(A) equals the number of
linear characters of C_G(A). Navarro's Problem6.3 in *Problems on
Characters: Solvable Groups* asks the stronger pointwise equivalence
with linearity of the Glauberman--Isaacs correspondent, and explicitly
notes that the nilpotent case was not proved there.

Start with groups of orders81,243,729 of class at least3. For each,
construct its full automorphism group and representatives of the
involutions in a Sylow2-subgroup. Every involution of the automorphism
group is conjugate to one of these; duplicates under the full
automorphism group are harmless. This searches cyclic actions of
order2, not arbitrary coprime actions or arbitrary finite groups.

For each action, calculate the fixed subgroup directly on elements.
Calculate all invariant irreducible characters using the induced
permutation of conjugacy classes, then evaluate them on every relevant
class of the fixed subgroup. Independently decompose each restriction
into irreducible characters of the fixed subgroup. For an involution
acting on an odd-order group, the unique odd-multiplicity constituent
is the Glauberman correspondent (classical theorem, to be source-audited
before relying on a potential hit). Compare its degree with the
nonvanishing test. Count equality and pointwise equivalence separately.

Preserve complete case rows, actual process exits, stderr and sentinels.
A hit must receive an explicit finite model, independent character
validation, and a rigorous proof before being counted as a solution.
No existing long-running worker is restarted or disturbed.

## Executed extension and validation plan

The pilot finished with 906 actions and no hit. Extend to orders 256 and
2187, retaining all groups of nilpotency class at least three. For every
prime r dividing the automorphism group but not the group, enumerate all
nonidentity conjugacy-class representatives of a Sylow r-subgroup. This
covers every nontrivial cyclic prime-power coprime action, with possible
duplicates under the full automorphism group. Four catalogue-index
shards per order use at most eight additional CPUs and 3 GiB each.

Preserve the actual exit of every shard and reconcile each complete row
file with its end-of-order and completion markers. Select controls with
nonabelian fixed subgroups, including every observed action order.

their group laws, homomorphisms, induced traces, irreducibility,
