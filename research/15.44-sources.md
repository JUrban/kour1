# Sources and audit for 15.44(a)

## Decisive prior source

Melvin Hochster, *Foundations of Tight Closure Theory*, lecture notes
based on Math 711, Fall 2007, University of Michigan, 276 pages.
Author-hosted PDF:
https://sites.lsa.umich.edu/hochster/wp-content/uploads/sites/1337/2024/10/fndtc.pdf

Pages 15–16 give the exact cubic-cone Segre example and the failed
parameter sequence used here. Both pages were rendered and viewed.
The proof of the example was read completely. Pages 7–16 were read in
extraction, including the proof of the six equivalent graded
Cohen–Macaulay conditions on page 12. That page was also rendered and
viewed. These standard equivalences are used as imported facts with
their proof checked; the rest of the 276-page course is not audited.

Two visible typographical slips are not copied: the displayed definition
of the unital Segre product starts at n=1 instead of n=0; the displayed
intermediate ring D lists zt where the following determinant presentation
requires yt. Our argument uses an explicit finite-dimensional parameter
quotient and does not depend on that intermediate ring.

## Review outcome

The elementary verification in `15.44-prior-counterexample.md` covers
reductivity, the exact induced-module filtration, irreducibility and
Cohen–Macaulayness of the input, finite generation of the invariant ring,
and its explicit nonregular parameter sequence. A separate Hilbert-series
argument gives the same failure. The construction adds no new candidate:
its essential example and obstruction are already in Hochster's notes.
No source or editor was contacted. Part (b), archived as solved in the
Notebook, is not a new target.
