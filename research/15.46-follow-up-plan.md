# Problem 15.46 after the 19.61 and 19.62 candidates

12 September 2026, 09:15 UTC. No resolution of 15.46 is claimed.

The Notebook asks whether closedness over a field reduces to closedness
of every opposite-root rank-one subcarpet. The locally finite field
case is already known, so the finite-ring searches do not resolve the
remaining field problem.

The new candidates give two useful facts: the derived carpet B is
closed, and the full root closure D of A is a carpet with derived
carpet B unchanged. These do not by themselves show D=A when all
rank-one subcarpets are closed. In particular, the square completion
C used for 19.61 can be strictly larger than the actual group closure;
the finite controls exhibit this in 20,300 cases. Do not replace C by
D in that argument.

Nuzhin 2011, *Factorization of carpet subgroups of the Chevalley groups
over commutative rings*, Theorem 2, gives a factorization of E(Phi,A)
as E(Phi,B) times the rank-one carpet subgroups, in any order. Its
statement and short collection argument were read through the primary
MathNet PDF interface:
https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=jsfu&paperid=211&what=fullt .
A direct requests download returned HTTP 403 (tool chunk d0eb5b), so
the PDF is not locally cached. The theorem states subgroup inclusions,
not normality of E(Phi,B). Neither normality nor root-coordinate
extraction from that factorization has been proved here.

Investigate whether one can show

    E(Phi,A) intersect X_r = E_r(Phi,A) intersect X_r,

using the closed derived core and the unchanged-derived result. This
would imply the desired equivalence. A proof must handle interactions
between the rank-one factors; a product of subgroups defined by closed
carpets cannot simply be assumed to remain closed.

Two primary-source leads concern the matrix-net case. Only their
abstracts/search excerpts have been inspected so far:

- Dryaeva--Koibaev, *Decomposition of elementary transvection in
  elementary group*, Zap. Nauchn. Sem. POMI 435 (2015), 33–41:
  https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/znsl6149 .
- Itarova--Koibaev, *Decomposition of Elementary Transvection in
  Elementary Net Group*, Vladikavkaz Math. J. 21(3) (2019), 24–30,
  DOI 10.23671/VNC.2019.3.36458. Primary full-issue PDF:
  https://www.vmj.ru/upload/uf/b5e/21_3_.pdf .

Both discuss a rank-one factor times a matrix group whose off-diagonal
levels are derived-net levels. Their full arguments and the distinction
between equality of root intersections and equality of whole rank-one
group intersections need checking before importing a consequence.
