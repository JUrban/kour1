# Internal review of Problem 15.92

Date: 2026-09-11. This is an internal adversarial audit, not independent
external review. The proof is in `15.92-proof.md`; the earlier conditional
route is retained in `15.92-bounded-support-route.md`.

## Logical audit

1. **Exact question.** The Notebook's PDF page 95 was viewed. Its final
   '=1' is missing in the presentation. Both readings are answered, since
   the literal group surjects onto the conventional triangle group.
2. **Parameter scope.** Every r>6 has a unique r=h+6d with 7<=h<=12 and
   d>=0. The selected p is fixed once r is fixed.
3. **Source strength.** Arbitrarily large alternating quotients alone are
   insufficient. The input used is the precise prime-cycle property of
   the same generating triples in Conder's construction.
4. **Ordinary versus extended group.** The reflection t occurs only in
   the derivation. The actual fixed word is a word in x,y alone because
   (xyt)^2=xyxy^-1. This equality follows from the defining relations.
5. **Uniformity.** The word exponent is 32760 or 27720; it never depends
   on n, the number of stock diagrams, or a subsequently chosen quotient.
6. **Exponent removal.** The 11- and 13-parts of 720720 each have exponent 1.
   Removing p kills every other permitted cycle length and leaves a
   nontrivial p-cycle. Removing just the prime would not suffice if other
   p-divisible cycles existed; the source explicitly excludes those.
7. **Parity.** U(8,d) and U(10,d) have odd x. The source construction
   corrects this using one W. S(8,d) and S(12,d) are each joins of two W
   diagrams and have even x. Every join adds two transpositions.
8. **Limit existence.** Countability of Gamma permits a diagonal
   subsequence with eventual constancy of the truth of every equation.
   R is normal. Nontrivial v never becomes trivial on that subsequence.
9. **Faithful permutation realization.** The set ultraproduct, not a
   metric ultraproduct that would kill bounded supports, is used. A moved
   point can be chosen for every nontrivial marked word. This distinction
   is essential.
10. **Bounded support survives.** S+1 distinct moved ultraproduct points
    would contradict the coordinate bound on an ultrafilter-large set.
    Thus vbar is a nontrivial finitary permutation.
11. **Normal closure.** Products of finitely many conjugates of vbar
    have finite support. N acts faithfully on Omega*, even if G's action
    restricted to Omega* has a kernel. Only N's faithfulness is needed.
12. **Uniform bound on finite N-orbits.** Omega* has at most S G-orbits.
    Normality of N makes all N-orbits inside one G-orbit equipotent.
    Consequently finite N-orbits have one uniform size bound M. Without
    the finitely many G-orbits, this conclusion would fail.
13. **Finite quotient kernel orbits.** H has finite index in N. If an
    H-orbit in an infinite N-orbit were finite, finitely many translates
    of it would make the N-orbit finite, a contradiction.
14. **Displacement.** H is locally finite as a group of finitary
    permutations. A finite subgroup with sufficiently large orbits on
    a prescribed finite set is obtained from finitely many elements of
    H. Uniform counting and the union bound produce a disjoint translate.
    No assumption of global transitivity of H is made.
15. **Central image.** Only x in L, the kernel on the finite N-orbits,
    is displaced. Conjugation by h in H preserves phi(x), so it commutes
    with every phi(y). The image phi(N)=A_m has trivial center; hence
    phi(L)=1. The proof does not claim all finite quotients of N vanish.
16. **Exponent obstruction.** eta(N) has exponent dividing M!, even
    with infinitely many finite orbits. A fixed odd prime ell>M yields
    an ell-cycle in every A_m with m>=ell. Such A_m cannot be a quotient
    of eta(N).
17. **Returning to the finite sequence.** A factorization theta_i through
    G would preserve its nontrivial image of vbar, contrary to the bound.
    Therefore theta_i(R)=A_(n_i) for all sufficiently large i.
18. **Witness choice.** [R:L_j] is bounded by the product of the earlier
    target orders. The next target order is larger, preventing theta_i
    from killing L_j. The chosen word is in R, so all sufficiently late
    maps kill it. This proves the required separation in both directions.
19. **Residual property.** The quotient by an intersection of kernels
    embeds in their product and still maps onto each selected factor.
    Thus the conclusion uses epimorphisms onto finite alternating groups.
20. **Isomorphism types.** For a fixed countable target there are at most
    countably many homomorphisms from the fixed finitely generated Gamma.
    Continuum many different kernels consequently give continuum many
    target types. No continuum-hypothesis assumption is involved.
21. **Infinite quotients.** An infinite selected subset gives factors of
    unbounded order and hence an infinite quotient. Restricting to these
    subsets preserves the continuum count and all witness arguments.

## Source audit and reading scope

The primary input is Conder's DPhil thesis, submitted in 1980. Its PDF was
created in 2012; that metadata is not its research publication date. The
scan has 135 pages and 34,829,469 bytes. The later 1981 QJMath article was
identified through primary publisher metadata but was not obtained in full.

Read in text and checked visually: printed pp. 90-91 and 117-120
(PDF 97-98,124-127), including the full final construction argument;
the S,U,W diagram tables on printed pp. 92,94,97,99,100,102,104,106,108,
109,111,114,116. Those tables cover every component needed by the
simplified stock-plus-U families, with W added in the two odd-x cases.
The other T,V diagrams are not claimed to have been fully audited. The
whole 135-page thesis is not claimed to have been read.

The elementary extended-triangle generator identities in Lemma 1.4 and
the useful-cycle Corollary 1.12 were read in text. The finite construction
uses the classical Jordan theorem on a primitive group containing a prime
cycle with at least three fixed points. The original Jordan proof is an
imported classical result.

Jones--Pierro 2017, Sections 2.1-2.4, part of Section 3, and relevant tables
were read as an initial r= 7 route. Jones's 2018 preprints supplied the
infinite-chain lead. Their maximal-subgroup statements are not substituted
for claims about normal kernels, and neither is needed for the final
abstract deduction. The Conder 1984 survey and Stephenson 1992 thesis were
retrieved during discovery; neither is a proof premise.

The 13 transcribed cycle tables and their joins are replayed exactly in
`scripts/check_15_92_cycles.py`. A marked token such as (a6) denotes a
7-cycle, not a 6-cycle. Every marked handle point lies in its own cycle,
so joining a pair of handles simply merges the corresponding cycle pairs.
Remaining handles lie in unaffected cycles, preventing cycle lengths from
growing with the number of subsequent joins. For d>=2 only 6-cycle
multiplicities grow, exactly as the source states.

All 150 arithmetic cases pass, with actual process exit 0 and the clean
sentinel `15_92_CYCLE_CONTROLS_DONE150240` (spaces in the actual log).
They include every h and each of the three source regimes d=0,1,>=2.
The extra d=3,10 cases verify the linear multiplicities. Binary powering
of explicit permutations with the transcribed cycle structures agrees
with the separate cycle-length calculation. This does not independently
reconstruct all the source's x,y,t permutations.

## Independent diagram reconstruction

The separate Python script `verify_15_92_diagrams.py` reconstructs the
two r= 7 base diagrams S(7,0) and U(7,0) from their drawn triangles and
edges, then sends their permutations and five joined examples to GAP.
It checks all six defining relations, the word identity, support and order
of the 11-cycle word, even generator parity, transitivity and primitivity.
Jordan's theorem then gives the exact alternating image in each degree
78, 120, 162, 246 and 456: the primitive group contains A_n, and even
generators put it inside A_n. Internal labels `chain1`, etc., count stock copies; the routine's
choice of the first available handle can form a tree of copies, and is
not a claim that each tested drawing is a linear chain.

The first reconstruction reversed the orientations of the side triangles
in each rectangle. GAP correctly reported a relation failure but returned 0;
the outer sentinel assertion rejected the run with actual exit 1. Script,
input permutations, GAP input, logs and process records are preserved with
`-initial` suffixes. The orientation correction was made by returning to
the source drawing. No source theorem or cycle table was changed.

The corrected reconstruction initially used general GAP group-order
calculation. That verified the exact orders n!/2 in degrees 78, 120, 162
and 246, then spent over twenty minutes on degree 456. A separate exact
primitivity check for degree 456 passed, together with all six relations,
the 11-cycle and even parity. The redundant order calculation was then
terminated deliberately. SIGTERM was sent; its recorded GAP return code is 1 and its outer return
code is 1; it is not represented as a completed run. All associated files
are retained with `-order-stopped` suffixes.

The final script uses the primitive-group criterion for all five examples.
Its successful completion requires the clean GAP sentinel, actual exit 0,
the outer sentinel and actual outer exit 0. The earlier four general-order
results are retained as additional controls, not as a five-case completed
order computation. The final GAP and outer processes both exit 0, all five controls and both
completion sentinels are present, and the logs are clean. The integrity
auditor enforces these distinctions. The first integrity audit expected a
raw signal return code of -15, whereas GAP returned 1 after SIGTERM. That
audit failure and original auditor are retained; the final check uses the
observed process record. Neither status is treated as success.
