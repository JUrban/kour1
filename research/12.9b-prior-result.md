# Problem 12.9(b): prior affirmative consequence

Checked 11 September 2026. No addition to the new-candidate count.

The printed problem defines a tree-free group by a free action without
inversions on a tree over an ordered abelian group Lambda. Part (a)
assumes finite generation and part (b) finite presentation. The printed
page 58 was visually checked; the two hypotheses must not be conflated.

## Deduction

Kharlampovich--Myasnikov--Serbin, *Actions, length functions, and
non-archemedian words*, Theorem 62(1), states that every abelian subgroup
of a finitely presented Lambda-free group is free abelian of finite
rank. This is exactly the class in part (b).

Let A_1 <= A_2 <= ... be an ascending chain of abelian subgroups of
such a group G. Its union A is an abelian subgroup: any two of its
elements belong to one common A_i. By the quoted theorem A has a
finite generating set. All of these generators belong to A_j for
some j, whence A=A_j and A_i=A_j for i>=j. Therefore G satisfies the
maximal condition for abelian subgroups, as required.

The same argument applies to any totally ordered ascending family:
a finite generating set of its union is contained in one family member.
No uniform rank bound is needed. Nothing here proves part (a).

## Sources and audit limits

- O. Kharlampovich, A. Myasnikov, D. Serbin, [survey, arXiv:1211.3207v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1211.3207v2),
  revised 26 January 2013; published in *International Journal of
  Algebra and Computation* 23(2) (2013), 325--455,
  DOI 10.1142/S0218196713400031. The cached filename includes 2012,
  the first-submission year, but the retained PDF is v2 from 2013.
  Read the end of Section 12.2 and Section 12.3, Theorems 57--64,
  including the entire short proof of Theorem 62; printed pp.119--120
  were visually checked. The other sections were not fully audited.
- The second route explicitly given in the proof uses Theorem 58:
  every finitely presented Lambda-free group is R^n-free for some n.
  It then imports the abelian-subgroup theorem for R^n-free groups
  from V. Guirardel, *Limit groups and groups acting freely on R^n-trees*,
  *Geometry & Topology* 8 (2004), 1427--1470. That original proof was
  not read in this audit.
- The structural input is KMS, [*Groups acting freely on Lambda-trees*, arXiv:0911.0209v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.0209v4),
  revised 2 November 2011. Read the introductory statements and
  corollaries, and the short proof of Theorem 2. The long elimination
  argument was not audited. Theorem 3, Theorem 4 and Corollary 4 give
  the required R^n-free consequence under finite presentation.

The survey mentions an accessibility proof gap near Theorem 61. The
deduction above relies on its explicit Theorem 62, using the separate
R^n-free route stated in that theorem's proof. It does not silently
repair that accessibility argument or claim independent verification
of the imported structure theorems. Likewise the surrounding prose
about a finitely generated question does not remove the finitely
presented hypothesis in Theorems 58--62.
