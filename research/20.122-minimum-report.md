# 20.122 completed: soluble minimum-order counterexample

September 12, 2026. This supersedes the unresolved-(a) status of the
earlier order-48 stage, whose proof and 138-file packet remain frozen.

**Outcome:** an explicit soluble group of order 36,864 and nilpotent
subgroups A,B=C satisfy

`min_G(A,B,B)=Min_G(A,B,B)=A`, but `A` is not contained in `F(G)`.

All of (a), (b), and both assertions of (c) consequently have negative
candidate answers. This completes the existing 20.122 entry; it does
not add another entry to the canonical total of 45. Outside reviews are
zero and priority remains unestablished.

The self-contained proof is `research/20.122-minimum-proof.md`. It uses
the signed affine group Q=F3^2 semidirect D8 on nine points and its
permutation wreath product with C2. The base is exactly F(G), of order
512. The inputs have orders 16,2,048,2,048. The conjugacy orbit of B
consists of nine point stabilizers, so all possible intersections reduce
to an explicit four-case table covering 81 ordered pairs. The ten
distinct intersections have orders 2,2,2,2,2,4,4,4,4,8. Exactly the five
order-two subgroups are minimal by either convention, and they generate A.

## Validation

The GAP model is constructed directly as permutations of eighteen
points. GAP verifies the group and input orders, nilpotency, solubility,
the exact Fitting subgroup, the complete conjugacy orbit, and all 81
intersections. It returned actual exit zero with empty stderr and the
unique completion marker at 15:12:59 UTC, after 1.850 seconds.

The independent Python calculation constructs all 36,864 permutations
from the coordinate formula. It regenerates G from its 13 exported
generators, independently rebuilds all nine order-2,048 point
stabilizers, and verifies all 117 orbit edges under the generators.
These checks establish orbit completeness, so no enumeration of all
36,864 squared conjugating-element pairs is needed.

It checks all 5,184 quotient products and 256 products in A, matches
every retained intersection, and computes C_G(E)=E directly. In Q it
checks the normal closures of all 39 nonidentity 2-elements: nine have
normal closure of order18 and thirty have normal closure of order36.
It explicitly excludes order-eight elements, proving O2(Q)=1 in the
finite model. The quotient derived-series orders are 72,18,9,1.
Together these independently confirm the two facts used in the elementary
Fitting-subgroup proof. Function composition supplies associativity;
no full multiplication table of the 36,864-element group is claimed.

Six altered certificates are rejected: a wrong quotient product, an
omitted conjugate, a wrong point stabilizer, a fabricated trivial
intersection, an inside-Fitting witness, and a false Fitting order.
The independent process returned actual exit zero, empty stderr and its
unique marker at 15:15:18 UTC, after 1.432 seconds.

`scripts/audit_20_122_minimum.py --replay` verifies the file bindings and
process receipts, regenerates the native model in a temporary directory,
and replays the independent reconstruction and exploratory grid screen.
Both proof computations were short single-worker processes within the
resource limits; there are no continuing native jobs from this stage.

point-stabilizer construction above. The other ten patterns remain
exploratory; their sufficient screen output is not presented as ten
independently verified group certificates. The general screen criterion
is unnecessary for the final proof.
